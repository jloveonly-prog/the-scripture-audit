> **[Analysis of the Problem]**: precisely identified! This describes the glorious resurrection body as though it were an **'Avatar'** from a science-fiction film. In the effort to force Scripture to exclude angels, this fatally unbiblical spiritualization degrades the resurrection body into 'an avatar awaiting a soul.'
