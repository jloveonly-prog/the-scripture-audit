> * James 2:19, "Thou believest that there is **one God**; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble."
> the preacher's 'two Gods' claim directly collides with these unmistakable biblical texts.

### Claim #2: Since the Soul Is the Criterion Dividing Being, There Are Two Gods
> **Timecode**: 13:28-21:54 | **C-Code**: C-09 | **Verdict**: ❌ UNBIBLICAL
> **[Preacher's Claim (Paraphrased)]**: "the unit dividing being is 'the soul.' Since Isaiah records that the Father has a soul and the Son has a soul, two souls exist, meaning 'two Gods.'"
> **[Analysis of the Problem]**: it is true that a soul is mentioned for each of the Father and the Son (Isa 42:1, Isa 53:11), but mapping the mechanism of 'spirit-soul-body' that constitutes a human being directly, 1:1, onto the essence of the Creator God (the Godhead), and mathematically separating them by saying "two souls, therefore two Gods," is an extreme doctrinal over-extrapolation. Scripture nowhere provides the formula "the number of souls = the number of Gods" — rather, it explicitly states "One God."

### Claim #3: the Origin of Jesus (Coming Out of the Father and Taking On a Body in the Beginning)
> **Timecode**: 34:17-36:23 | **C-Code**: C-10 (Christology) | **Verdict**: ⚠️ UNSUPPORTED / 🟡 OPINION

It is understandable that the preacher is motivated to break through the difficulty of the traditional theological concept of 'Eternal Generation,' but the claim that "He existed as a soul within the Father, then took on a body and was separated out" is a 100% arbitrary, speculative inference (⚠️ UNSUPPORTED) recorded nowhere in Scripture.
