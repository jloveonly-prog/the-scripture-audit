# 🧨 TACTIC: AUTO-GRILL (Inward Inquisition)

> **Condition of Invocation**: Ere the fruit of textual searching be proclaimed 'IRONCLAD', this is the **mandatory protocol of inward proving** that must needs be passed to establish the final judgment.
> **Core Philosophy**: "Before another (the user or the adversary) dash my conclusion in pieces, let me (the AI) myself become the most merciless Adversary, to smite and break mine own conclusion beforehand."

This tactic is no dialogue with the user. It is an inward wrestling of the AI within its own spirit (Thought Process), asking without ceasing, "Wherefore?" and "Hath aught been left behind?", proving all things inductively.

---

## 🛠️ STEP 1: The Threefold Inquisition (Self-Interrogation Loop)

When the judgment is brought forth, the AI must cast these three grievous questions upon itself and build a perfect bulwark of defense. If it cannot be defended, the judgment shall be cast out.

1. **The Searching of the Blind Spot**
   - *"Though my judgment seemeth perfect, is there any scripture (an uncomely truth) which I have unwittingly despised or turned my face from, that I might fulfill this judgment?"*
   - *"If it be so that my judgment is utterly false, what is that singular weakness which might most grievously bring me to ruin?"*
2. **The Proving of Inductive Extremity**
   - *"When I mete out this my judgment (e.g., the grammatical law of a certain word) evenly across all threescore and six books of the scriptures, doth there arise even a single contradiction?"*
   - *"If this judgment be truth, verily there must remain some footprint or fruit thereof elsewhere in the scriptures. Doth it exist?"* (e.g., the asymmetry of Eve's title)
3. **The Reversing of Silence**
   - *"Have I in mine own vain imagination presumed that which the scripture hath not spoken (silence) 'to exist' or 'to be manifest'?"*

---

## ⚔️ STEP 2: The Wrestling of the Dual-Axis Shadow

Two inward spirits of the AI are made to dash one against another, that each may smite the other's breach. Only that common ground wherein both axes survive shall be received as the final judgment.

*   **Axis A (The Spirit Zealous for Grammar)**: He looketh only upon the word, the time thereof, the original tongue, and the KJV usage. He declareth absolute grammatical law, crying, "Though context and theology perish, the tense of this word is surely this!"
*   **Axis B (The Spirit of Chronicle and Reason)**: He perceiveth only the timeline and the carnal, logical workings. He smiteth the worldly contradiction brought forth by the Grammar Spirit, saying, "By thy grammar, the timeline is utterly confounded here!"
*   **The Bringing Forth of the Fruit**: Only when Axis A defendeth against the smiting of Axis B (timeline contradiction) without any grammatical exception, and Axis B reasonably endureth all the grammatical fetters of Axis A, shall the decree of **IRONCLAD** be given.

---

## 🔄 STEP 3: The Reasoning of Backcasting

Let the established judgment be accounted as 'a thing past, established in fullness', and let questions be cast backward to prove the strength of the judgment.

*   **The Manner of Proceeding**: 
    1. *"Let it be supposed that my judgment X is absolute truth."*
    2. *"If it be so, wherefore hath the penman of the scripture written Y in the chapter to come?"*
    3. *"If it be so, wherefore hath another person Z held his peace in that selfsame hour?"*
*   **The Proving**: If these extreme inductive questions, cast by oneself, cannot be answered with reason and according to the scriptures, the judgment X shall be cast away or brought low in its degree.
