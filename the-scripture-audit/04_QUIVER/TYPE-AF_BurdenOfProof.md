# 🏹 SKILL-33: TYPE-AF — Burden of Proof Transfer
**"The one who attacks must prove it first"**

> **When it triggers**: When an opponent attempts to dismiss a claim that has internal biblical evidence **without any evidence**.
> Transferring the burden of proof to the attacker, establishing that claims external to the Bible must first present **internal biblical counterevidence**.
>
> **One-line definition**: *"A claim that something is absent cannot dismiss a claim that something is present without evidence."*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Trigger Principle**: For a claim where an internal biblical argument exists,
> those who attempt to dismiss it solely with external tradition or academic consensus **must first present internal biblical counterevidence.**

---

## Trigger Conditions
`
[General]
1. When an opponent attempts to dismiss our claim without evidence
2. When an internal biblical argument is rebutted solely by external tradition/authority
3. When formalizing that "the burden of proof lies with the one who attacks"
4. When an opponent attempts to dismiss an internal biblical structural argument solely on the grounds that "it is not explicitly stated in the Bible"

[Typical Code] Trigger immediately upon detecting the following sentences:
  - "[Our claim] is not explicitly stated" (Attempting to dismiss without grounds)
  - "[External tradition/church fathers/academic community] views it differently"
  - When an opponent objects using only the authority of tradition without evidence
  → Rebuttal: "To dismiss a claim that has internal biblical evidence, first present counterevidence from within the Bible."
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Identify the Location of the Burden of Proof
  → Who is currently claiming what?
  → Our claim: Internal biblical evidence presented ✅ / ❌
  → Opponent's claim: Internal biblical evidence presented ✅ / ❌

[STEP 2] Declaration of Responsibility Transfer
  → "To dismiss [our claim] which has internal biblical evidence,
     present evidence from within the Bible that refutes [our claim]."

[STEP 3] Dismiss External Tradition/Authority Evidence
  → Traditions outside the Bible (church fathers, historians, academic consensus, etc.) cannot dismiss internal biblical arguments.
  → Bible = Supreme Authority / Tradition = Secondary Source

[STEP 4] Verdict
  → If the opponent fails to present internal biblical counterevidence → Our claim is maintained. ✅
`

## 📌 Reference Case: Peter's Calvary Martyrdom Theory

| Claim | Evidence | Burden of Proof |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| Calvary Theory | Internal biblical argument from John 13:36 + 19:17 | ✅ Presentation Complete |
| Rome Theory | Church father tradition after AD 180 | ❌ Internal biblical counterevidence not presented |

> **TYPE-AF Verdict**: The Rome Theory attacks the Calvary Theory solely with external tradition without presenting internal biblical counterevidence.
> Under the principle of the burden of proof, the Calvary Theory, which has internal biblical evidence, takes precedence. ✅ CONFIRMED
