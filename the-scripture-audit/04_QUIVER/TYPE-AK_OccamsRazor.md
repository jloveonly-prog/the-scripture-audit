# 🏹 SKILL-38: TYPE-AK — Occam's Razor (Principle of Parsimony)
**"Among hypotheses explaining the same evidence, the simpler one prevails"**

> **When it triggers**: When two or more hypotheses explain the same biblical text.
> When the explanatory power of the two hypotheses seems similar, select the hypothesis that requires fewer assumptions and is simpler.
>
> **One-line definition**: *"Eliminate unnecessary assumptions. A simpler explanation is closer to the truth."*

---

## Trigger Conditions
`
[General]
1. When two hypotheses appear to explain the same evidence.
2. When the opposing hypothesis requires additional assumptions (external tradition, historical background, unconfirmed premises).
3. When there is a simple explanation that operates entirely within the Scripture.
4. When a choice is needed between "a more complex explanation vs. a simpler explanation."

[Typical Code] Trigger immediately upon detecting the following situations:
  - When claiming that two or more interpretations can explain the same text.
  - When the opponent's interpretation requires additional assumptions (external history, patristic tradition, archaeology, etc.).
  - When our interpretation is completed solely within the Scripture.
  → Create a table comparing the number of assumptions (STEP 1~2) and declare the priority of simplicity.
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Create a List of Assumptions per Hypothesis
  → List the premises/assumptions required for each hypothesis to be true.

[STEP 2] Compare the Number of Assumptions
  → Which hypothesis explains the evidence with fewer assumptions?

[STEP 3] Determine Simplicity
  → Fewer assumptions = Simpler explanation = Priority based on Occam's Razor criterion.

[STEP 4] Verdict
  → "Adopt the simpler hypothesis."
`

## 📌 Reference Case: Calvary Hypothesis vs. Roman Hypothesis

| Assumption Items | Calvary Hypothesis | Roman Hypothesis |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| John 13:36 ὅπου = Golgotha (Internal to Scripture) | ✅ Required | ❌ Not required |
| Scriptural basis that Peter went to Rome | ❌ Not required | ⚠️ Required (but absent) |
| Adoption of patristic traditions after 180 AD | ❌ Not required | ✅ Required |
| Acceptance of extra-biblical historical sources | ❌ Not required | ✅ Required |
| Completion solely through internal scriptural consistency | ✅ Complete | ❌ Incomplete |

> **TYPE-AK Verdict**: The Calvary hypothesis is a simple explanation completed solely within the Scripture.
> The Roman hypothesis requires the additional adoption of extra-biblical tradition to operate.
> Based on Occam's Razor criterion — Calvary hypothesis takes priority. ✅ CONFIRMED
