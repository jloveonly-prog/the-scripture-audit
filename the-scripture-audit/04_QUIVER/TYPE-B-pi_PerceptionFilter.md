# 🔬 SKILL-02π: TYPE-B-π — Perception Filter (Witness Capacity Limit)

> **When to Activate**: When a witness was present at the scene and physically saw or heard, but **failed to process the information normally** due to psychological, physical, or environmental reasons.
> **Core Suspicion**: "Did this witness not see it, or did they see it but were in an unprocessable state?"

> **Classification**: TYPE-B's independent expansion weapon (Can be activated in parallel with TYPE-B)
> **Source**: Derived from `[B]_TombEvent_ResurrectionMorning_SequentialIntegration.md` (S-Rank War Log)

---

## Core Principle: The 3-State Model of Witnessing

```
Existing System (2-State):
  State 1: Witnessed
  State 2: Did Not Witness

TYPE-B-π Addition (3-State):
  State 1: Witnessed → Normal processing → Reporting possible
  State 2: Did Not Witness → No processing → No report
  State 3: Witnessed → Processing impossible (Shock/Grief/Cultural preconception) → Distorted report ← NEW
```

> **Core Principle**: **Witness ≠ Perception**
> Even if the eyes are open and the ears can hear — the brain (psychology) may refuse to process.

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Check the Witness's Psychological/Physical State
  → What was the witness's emotional state at the time of the event?
  → Were they in a state of shock, extreme grief, fear, cultural preconception, fatigue, or confusion?
  → Determine if activation conditions are met

[STEP 2] Detect Input vs. Output Discrepancy
  → Information the witness actually received (Input): Angel's message, Jesus's voice, a specific scene
  → What the witness actually reported (Output): Suspicion of theft, mistaking him for the gardener, etc.
  → If Input ≠ Output, TYPE-B-π activation is confirmed

[STEP 3] Classify Cause of Processing Impossibility
  → SHOCK: Cognitive paralysis due to sudden shock
  → GRIEF: Cognitive distortion due to extreme state of mourning
  → CULTURAL: Information rejection due to cultural preconception (premise of impossibility of resurrection)
  → PHYSICAL: Cognitive limitation due to fatigue, injury, or environmental constraints
  → DIVINE: Supernatural blockage (Luke 24:16 "their eyes were holden")

[STEP 4] Verify Internal Consistency of Distorted Report
  → Is the distorted report logically consistent with the witness's unprocessable state?
  → Consistent → Fully explainable by perception filter ✅
  → Inconsistent → Seek additional causes

[STEP 5] Verify Perception State in Original Language
  → Analyze the tense/voice of the related verb: Active (see) vs. Passive (be shown)
  → Luke 24:16: "ἐκρατοῦντο" (Passive Imperfect) = "were continuously restrained"
  → Greek passive voice → Clear evidence of external (divine) blockage
```

## Application Cases

### Case 1: Mary Magdalene's Distorted Report (Shock + GRIEF)

```
[Input]:  Time T1-B: The angel says "why weepest thou?, He is not here"
[State]:  Extreme mourning state (Luke 23:55-56, John 20:11 "stood without at the sepulchre weeping")
[Output]: Reports theft hypothesis (John 20:2 "They have taken away the Lord out of the sepulchre, and we know not where they have laid him")

→ Determination: TYPE-B-π (GRIEF) — Heard the angel's words, but due to the state of mourning,
                 'resurrection' interpretation was impossible → Output distorted to 'theft' interpretation ✅
```

### Case 2: Mary Magdalene Mistaking Jesus (Shock + GRIEF)

```
[Input]:  Resurrected Jesus standing behind her (John 20:14)
[State]:  Weeping state / Cognitive frame lacking the premise of resurrection
[Output]: Mistakes him for the gardener (John 20:15)

→ Determination: TYPE-B-π (GRIEF + CULTURAL) — Physically witnessed, but
                 could not perceive due to lacking the premise that "the dead are alive" ✅
```

### Case 3: Disciples of Emmaus (Divine Blockage)

```
[Input]:  Walking and conversing with the resurrected Jesus (Luke 24:13-31)
[State]:  Luke 24:16 "But their eyes were holden that they should not know him"
[Output]: Perceives him as a stranger

→ Determination: TYPE-B-π (DIVINE) — ἐκρατοῦντο (Passive) = Perception was actively
                 blocked by external divine intervention. Not a human emotional issue ✅
```
