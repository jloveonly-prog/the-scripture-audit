# 🏹 TYPE-Y: Multi-Perspective Witness

> **Alias**: Resolution of the Witness
> **Old Version Code**: A-04
> **Hermeneutical Background**: Legal Evidentialism (Spontaneous perspective differences among independent witnesses)
> **Core Philosophy**: In the Gospels, differences in the details of the same event (e.g., 1 angel vs. 2 angels, 1 blind man vs. 2 blind men) are not 'errors' but rather the perfect **characteristics of 'independent witness testimonies'** proving the records were not fabricated or colluded. Fabricated lies match 100% in detail, but genuine multiple witnessing shares the same framework while being recorded from different perspectives based on each person's field of vision and emphasis.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Critics attack minor visual differences in biblical records as 'contradictions'. At this time, activate TYPE-Y to counterattack with the legal logic that **"Inclusion is not Exclusion"**, and strongly argue that this is evidence of vivid, unfabricated eyewitness accounts.

---

## Activation Conditions

```
Activate TYPE-Y in the following situations:
1. When the number of characters or descriptions of objects in an event differ in parallel passages (especially in the Gospels).
2. When attacked with "Matthew says there were 2 but Mark says 1, so the Bible is wrong."
3. When the authors' theological emphasis (King, Servant, Man, God) viewing the same event is different.
```

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Check for Exclusion
  → Verify: "Does one record explicitly deny the existence of the other record?"
  → Example: Did Mark say "There was only 1 angel," or did he say "saw 1 angel"?
  → If there is no exclusionary word like "only one" in the Bible, rule that it emphasized 1 out of the 2.

[STEP 2] Identify the Focus
  → Grasp through context why the recorder emphasized that part/person.
  → Example: Matthew tends to record all facts (2 people), while Mark/Luke tend to focus the camera lens on the 1 person who leads the conversation or stands out.

[STEP 3] Anti-Collusion Argument
  → Use a modern forensic/rhetorical analogy: "In a police investigation, if the statements of multiple witnesses are exactly identical down to the letter, the police consider it collusion (a fabricated lie)."

[STEP 4] 3D Integration
  → Combine the records seen from each perspective, reconstruct the event like a 3D hologram, and deliver the ruling.
```

## 📌 Reference Cases

### Number of Blind Men in Jericho (Matthew 20:30 vs Mark 10:46)
*   **Controversy**: Was there 2 blind men healed in Jericho (Matthew) or 1 (Mark - Bartimaeus)?
*   **TYPE-Y Application**:
    *   **Check for Exclusion**: Mark did not write "There was only 1 person." He wrote, "a blind man, Bartimaeus... was sitting by the road."
    *   **Identify the Focus**: There were two blind men, but Mark's lens focused on the individual (Bartimaeus) who shouted louder and more passionately, leading the conversation. Matthew captured the whole scene (2 people) with a wide lens, and Mark zoomed in from the wide lens to take a close-up shot of the 1 instigator.
*   **Ruling**: Recording a close-up of 1 out of 2 people is not a contradiction but a difference in visual focus (resolution). It proves they are records of genuine, uncolluded witnesses.
