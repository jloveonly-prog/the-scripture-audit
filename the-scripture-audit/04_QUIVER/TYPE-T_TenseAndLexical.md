# 🗡️ TYPE-T: Tense & Lexical Forensics

> **"The enemy's most grandiose theological attacks are often built upon ignorance of basic tenses and lexical meanings of words."**

## 1. 🎯 Weapon Overview
**TYPE-T** is a forensic weapon that **detects misreadings by precisely targeting the tense (past/present/future/perfect) and the exact lexical and contextual meaning of the KJV original text** when a critic makes biblical contradictions or forced theological claims based on a specific verse.

*   **Classification:** Grammar/Lexical Forensic
*   **Target:** Invalidates the very foundation of the enemy's logic claiming theological attribute conflicts (TYPE-C) through tense/lexical verification.
*   **Main Subject:** Spiritualizing that ignores the original meaning of the word, or interpretations that reverse or ignore the tense.

## 2. ⚙️ Trigger Conditions
Triggers when detecting the following types of attacks:
1.  **Excessive Spiritual Leaps:** When grand doctrines are built by excessively interpreting a common word in the biblical text (e.g., disquieted) into a 'spiritual state' (e.g., corruption of spiritual order) that doesn't fit the context.
2.  **Timeline (Tense) Ignorance Error:** When distorting the sequence of events or spiritual state by ignoring the explicit tense (past, perfect, etc.) in the KJV original text.
3.  **Suspected Translation Error:** When the critic uses the nuances of translated versions to undermine the temporal and contextual accuracy of the original KJV English word.

## 3. 🔍 Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Anchor KJV Tense
*   Accurately retrieve the KJV English original text of the verse the critic uses as a weapon.
*   Isolate the verb tense (past/present/future/perfect) of the sentence to establish **when the action occurred** or **what state it presupposes**.

### Step 2: Lexical Analysis
*   Trace the dictionary and contextual meaning of the original language (Hebrew/Greek) of the target word and the KJV English word.
*   **Enemy's Interpretation:** Leaps to a spiritual/figurative interpretation of the word.
*   **Original Structure:** The most basic, physical, and contextually appropriate meaning of the word (Literal meaning).

### Step 3: Theological Reversal
*   By restoring the tense and the original meaning of the word, prove how the "theological conflict" claimed by the enemy turns out to be fiction, and conversely, **how it proves our side's claim (inducing self-destruction, etc.)**.
*   Write a verdict on why this is a **"Lexical Fallacy"** and a **"Tense Error"**.

## 🆕 Detection Pattern: Temporal Adverb Anchoring — v1.1

> **Trigger Condition**: When the enemy interprets by ignoring or omitting a temporal adverb (ἄρτι/νῦν/ὕστερον, etc.).
> **Core Question**: *"Does the sentence hold up without this temporal adverb? What meaning is erased if it is absent?"*

```
[Detection Pattern 1] Misreading by omitting ἄρτι (now)
  → "cannot follow now" — ἄρτι anchors an immediate, physical timeline
  → If omitted: "cannot follow" = vague impossibility → erases specific time information
  → Heaven interpretation: "Cannot go to heaven now" → Self-evident for all living humans → ἄρτι unnecessary → Misreading

[Detection Pattern 2] Tense misreading of ὕστερον (afterwards)
  → ἀκολουθήσεις = Future Indicative = "Will certainly follow in the future"
  → Misreading: Reduced to "Follow spiritually now" by reading it as a present imperative
  → Future Indicative = Prophetic declaration (Confirmed future, not a command)

[Detection Pattern 3] Spiritual reduction misreading of physical verbs
  → Usages of ἀκολουθέω throughout the Gospels: A verb for physically following behind
    (Matt 4:20 Fishermen leaving nets and following, Mark 15:41 Women following to Jerusalem)
  → Misreading: Reduced to "spiritual imitation" → Erases the physical dimension of movement
```

### 📌 Reference Case: Detecting a 3-Tier Misreading of Temporal Adverbs in John 13:36

```
| Original Element | Misreading (Heaven/Spiritual) | Original Text (Calvary Interpretation) | Verdict |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|
| ἀκολουθέω (word) | "Mentally imitate" | Verb for physically following behind | ❌ Misreading |
| ἀκολουθήσεις (tense) | Present/Imperative → Follow now | Future Indicative = Prophetic confirmation | ❌ Misreading |
| ἄρτι (now) | Vaguely cannot go to heaven | The physical path He is walking right now | ❌ Misreading |
| ὕστερον (afterwards) | Meet later in heaven | Physically follow at a specific point after resurrection | ❌ Misreading |
→ Simultaneous 3-tier misreading of word+tense+adverb detected ✅
```
