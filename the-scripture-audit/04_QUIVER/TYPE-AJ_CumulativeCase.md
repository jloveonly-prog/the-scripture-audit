# 🏹 SKILL-37: TYPE-AJ — Cumulative Case Argument
**"Not a single evidence, but a multitude of independent converging evidences establishes the conclusion"**

> **When to Trigger**: When the adversary doth attack individual evidence one by one saying, "This alone is insufficient."
> While acknowledging the weakness of a single evidence, declare that **the simultaneous convergence of multiple independent evidences** is a stronger confirmation.
>
> **One-line Definition**: *"Even if one evidence be weak, if ten independent evidences point to the same conclusion, it is the truth."*
