# 🔥 SKILL-47: TYPE-AT — Divine Attribute Reductio
**"Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights" — James 1:17 KJV**

> **When to trigger**: When a claim is made that "God directly created/did X,"
> AND the attributes of that X **directly conflict** with God's **essential attributes** (holiness, goodness, light, truth).
> **Core Question**: *"If the Holy God directly created this, which of God's attributes is compromised?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse with TYPE-AC/TYPE-AI:**
> - `TYPE-AC` = Establishing a counter-hypothesis (¬A) to expose **contradictions between texts**
> - `TYPE-AI` = Substituting the opponent's hypothesis into the Bible → Exposing **logical contradictions**
> - `TYPE-AT` = Exposing the conflict between God's **essential attributes** vs. the **results** of an action
> TYPE-AC/AI involves logical destruction / TYPE-AT involves **destruction at the level of blasphemy** (Dual structure of emotional+logical).

---

## ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ When a claim is made that "God directly created/did X."
□ AND the attributes of that X directly conflict with God's essential attributes (holiness, goodness, light, truth).
□ When a question in the vein of "Did God create evil?" is the point of contention.
□ When a response is needed for "Why did God make unclean spirits/evil spirits?"
```

## ⚙️ Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Specify the Contested Action/Result
  → "God directly created (Bara) X."
  → Identify the nature of X in the KJV.
  → | X | KJV Name | Verse | Attribute |

[STEP 2] Check the Attributes of X in the KJV
  → Specify the attributes that X possesses (uncleanness, evil, defect, etc.) in KJV terminology.
  → | Target | KJV Name | Attribute Keyword |

[STEP 3] Fix God's Essential Attributes with KJV Anchors
  → Collect at least 3 of God's attributes that directly conflict with the attributes of X.
  → 【 List of God's Attribute Anchors (Standard) 】
     ① Jas 1:17 — "Every good gift... Father of lights" (Good+Light)
     ② Hab 1:13 — "Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil" (Purity+Intolerance of Evil)
     ③ 1 John 1:5 — "God is light, and in him is no darkness at all" (Light+Absence of Darkness)
     ④ Deut 32:4 — "his work is perfect" (Perfection)
     ⑤ Isa 6:3 — "Holy, holy, holy" (Absolute Holiness)

[STEP 4] Fill out the Attribute Conflict Verdict Table
  → | X | Attribute of X | God's Attribute | Conflict? | KJV Anchor |
  → Mark with 💥 if a conflict occurs.
  → Conflict = The claim that "God directly Bara-ed X" undermines divine attributes.

[STEP 5] Demand an Alternative → Present the Only Alternative
  → "Explain the origin of X without compromising God's attributes."
  → The Only Alternative: Derivatives of fallen creatures (Originating from beings other than God).
  → God created good things (sons), and the sons fell, becoming unclean derivatives.
  → God's creation = Good → Creatures' fall = Evil → God's attributes perfectly preserved.
```

## 📌 Reference Case: Did God create unclean spirits?

```
[STEP 1] Contention:
  → "God directly created (Bara) devils (unclean spirits, spirits of divination, dumb spirits)."

[STEP 2] Check Attributes of X:

  | Type of Devil | KJV Name | Attribute Keyword | Verse |
  |:---|:---|:---:|:---|
  | Unclean spirit | unclean spirit | Unclean | Mark 1:23 |
  | Divining spirit | spirit of divination | Divination | Acts 16:16 |
  | Dumb spirit | dumb spirit | Defect (dumb) | Mark 9:25 |
  | Evil spirit | evil spirit | Evil | 1 Sam 16:14 |

[STEP 3] God's Attribute Anchors:
  → Jas 1:17 — "Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above... Father of lights"
  → Hab 1:13 — "Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil"
  → 1 John 1:5 — "God is light, and in him is no darkness at all"

[STEP 4] Attribute Conflict Verdict:

  | X | Attribute of X | God's Attribute | Conflict? | KJV Anchor |
  |:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
  | Unclean spirit (unclean) | Unclean | Purity | 💥 | Hab 1:13 |
  | Divining spirit (divination) | Divination | Declared abomination | 💥 | Deut 18:10-12 |
  | Dumb spirit (dumb) | Defect | Perfection | 💥 | Deut 32:4 + Exod 4:11 |
  | Evil spirit (evil) | Evil | Good·Light | 💥 | Jas 1:17 + 1 John 1:5 |

  → 4/4 Total Conflict = The claim that "God directly Bara-ed them" 
    compromises God's attributes of purity, goodness, perfection, and light in four ways.

[STEP 5] Alternative:
  → The only explanation that preserves the attributes:
    God created (Bara) good beings (sons of God),
    and those sons fell of their own free will, splitting and deriving their spiritual energy,
    and the spirits of these nations became devils.
  → God's creation = Good sons
  → Sons' fall = Unclean derivatives (devils)
  → ∴ God's attributes perfectly preserved ✅
```
