# 🏹 SKILL-41: TYPE-AN — Moving Goalposts Detection
**"Catches the fallacy of changing criteria when evidence is provided"**

> **When to Activate**: When the opponent continues to avoid accepting the conclusion by introducing new criteria
> even after the initially demanded criteria have been met.
>
> **One-Line Definition**: *"Providing what was asked for, then asking for something else — that in itself is a fallacy."*

---

## Activation Conditions
`
[General]
1. When the opponent introduces a new criterion despite the initially demanded criterion being met
2. When the criterion has moved at least once during the argument
3. When the pattern of "continuing to demand different criteria even after evidence is provided" repeats

[Typical Code] Activate immediately upon detecting the following patterns:
  - "Provide internal biblical evidence" → After provision → Changed to "There must be a [Stronger Criterion]"
  - Criterion met → New criterion demanded → Met → Yet another new criterion (Repeating pattern)
  - "That's different, this time [New Condition] is needed"
  → Crucial to accurately record the initial criterion in STEP 1
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Record Initial Criterion
  → Accurately record the criterion the opponent initially demanded.
  → "I will acknowledge it if there is internal biblical evidence."

[STEP 2] Confirm Criterion Met
  → Has that criterion been met? ✅

[STEP 3] Detect Goalpost Moving
  → Point out the exact utterance where the new criterion appeared.

[STEP 4] Declare Dismissal
  → "This is moving the goalposts. The argument holds because the initial criterion was met."
`

## 📌 Reference Case: Moving Goalposts in the "Explicit" Debate

| Step | Opponent's Criterion | Met Status |
|:---:|:---|:---:|
| 1 | "Must have internal biblical evidence" | ✅ Provided John 13:36+19:17 |
| 2 | "Must have a direct declaration in a single verse" | ⚠️ Criterion moved |
| 3 | "John must directly declare 'this is Golgotha'" | ⚠️ Moved again |
| 4 | "Cannot accept because AI yielded to pressure" | ⚠️ Off-topic move |

> **TYPE-AN Verdict**: The initial criterion ("internal biblical evidence") was met.
> Subsequently, the criterion moved 3 times. This is the moving goalposts fallacy.
> Based on the initial criterion — the Calvary theory stands. ✅
