# 🏹 SKILL-31: TYPE-AD — Abductive Reasoning (Inference to the Best Explanation)
**"The hypothesis that most completely explains all the evidence is true"**

> **When to Invoke**: When determining which of several competing hypotheses is the truth.
> Given all collected evidence, comparatively evaluate **how well each hypothesis explains those evidences**
> and adopt the hypothesis with the most complete explanatory power.
>
> **One-line Definition**: *"Which hypothesis explains all the evidence most simply and completely?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Difference from TYPE-E (Dismissal of Competing Models):**
> - TYPE-E = Dismisses each competing hypothesis individually
> - TYPE-AD = **Simultaneously compares** all hypotheses based on evidence explanatory power → Adopts the best hypothesis
> TYPE-E is elimination (removal) / TYPE-AD is selection (adoption).

---

## Invocation Conditions

`
Invoke TYPE-AD in the following situations:
1. When determining the superiority of multiple hypotheses, such as "Which is more biblical, the Calvary theory or the Rome theory?"
2. When reinforcing the basis for adopting the remaining hypothesis after eliminating alternatives via the method of elimination (TYPE-N)
3. When the opponent argues, "It is merely the most powerful interpretation, not a confirmation"
   → Abductive reasoning formalizes that "The most powerful explanation = The hypothesis that must be adopted"
`

---

## Execution Steps

`
[STEP 1] Evidence Inventory
  → List all evidence secured from the text.
  → (Ex: Place adverb ὅπου, John 21:19 ἀκολουθέω, σημαίνων formula, emphatic pronoun σύ...)

[STEP 2] Competing Hypotheses
  → Specify the hypotheses subject to verification.
  → (Ex: Calvary theory, Rome theory, general martyrdom theory, Heaven theory)

[STEP 3] Compose Explanatory Matrix
  → Does each hypothesis explain each evidence? Yes(✅) / No(❌) / Partial(⚠️)

[STEP 4] Best Explanation Adoption
  → The hypothesis that explains all evidence with ✅ = The target of the abductive reasoning verdict
  → Criteria for adoption: Completeness of explanatory power + Simplicity (auxiliary application of Occam's Razor)

[STEP 5] Verdict Declaration
  → "Hypothesis X is the only hypothesis that explains all the evidence. ∴ Adopt X."
`
