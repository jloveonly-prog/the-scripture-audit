# 🗡️ SKILL-53: TYPE-AY — Demand Hierarchy Analysis

**"Peradventure there be fifty righteous within the city…" — Genesis 18:24 KJV**

> **When triggered**: When a subject presents **multiple demands sequentially**.
> **Core question**: *"In what order did the demands recede? And what was the **self-proposed alternative**?"*

---

## 1. 🎯 Weapon Overview

One principle of crisis negotiation practice — **If a negotiator proposes an alternative themselves, that alternative reveals their actual priorities.** The maximum demand may be a negotiation card, but the voluntary line of concession and voluntary alternative expose the true objective.

TYPE-AY **rearranges sequentially presented demands into a hierarchy** to extract what the speaker intended to hold unto the end (minimum line) and the alternative they presented themselves.

*   **Classification:** Rhetorical Forensics (Negotiation Structure Analysis)
*   **Base Layer:** Crisis Negotiation·Investigative Psychology (`01_MANDATE/IDENTITY_Scribe42.md` knowledge base)
*   **Target Objective:** Confirming the **actual priority** behind the expressed demands

---

## 2. ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ A subject presents a demand, plea, or proposal two or more times
□ An order can be established among the demands
□ There is a change in the intensity or scope of the demand (retreat, reduction, transition to alternative)
□ It is a conversational structure where a counterpart (recipient) exists
```

---

## 3. 🔍 Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Collect All Demands and Confirm Order
  → | Order | Demand (KJV Wording) | Verse | Author |
  → If there are parallel gospel records, collect all records from different authors.
  → The chronological order follows DE-OVERLAP Principle 3 (Preservation of Utterance Order).

[STEP 2] Hierarchical Arrangement
  → Maximum Demand : Broadest scope / Most favorable condition
  → Intermediate Demand : Retreated condition
  → Minimum Line : What is refused unto the end — "Only this must not be"
  → Voluntary Alternative: The proposal brought forth by the demander themselves ★ Highest evidential value

[STEP 3] Examine Unmentioned Options (Mandatory — Do not omit)
  → Find "options the demander could have chosen but did not even bring to the negotiation table."
  → This correlates with TYPE-AG (Argument from Silence). The option must be
    substantively recorded elsewhere in Scripture to be judged as "evasion."
  → 🚨 This STEP is the differentiator of this weapon. Looking only at what was demanded is looking at only half.

[STEP 4] Confirm Priorities
  → Minimum Line + Voluntary Alternative + Unmentioned Options = Actual Priority Map
  → Maximum demands are not used as grounds for judging priority (may be a negotiation card)

[STEP 5] Judgment
  → Existence and order of demands : Textual wording → EXPLICIT
  → Priority structure : Result of hierarchical arrangement → STRONG when cross-witnesses are secured
  → Inner motive of the demander : Unconfirmable if unstated in the text
```

---

## 4. 📌 Reference Case A: The Legion of Gadara (Mark 5 / Luke 8 / Matthew 8)

```
[STEP 1~2] Collect Demands and Arrange Hierarchy

  | Hierarchy | Demand | Verse | Author |
  |-------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------|-----------|
  | Maximum Demand | Torment me not | Mark 5:7/Matt 8:29 | Mark·Matthew |
  | Intermediate Demand | Send us not away out of the country | Mark 5:10 | Mark |
  | Minimum Line | Command us not to go out into the deep | Luke 8:31 | Luke |
  | Voluntary Alternative | **Send us into the swine** | Mark 5:12 | Common to 3 Authors |

  → Cross-Witness: Mark·Matthew·Luke 3 authors ✅

[STEP 3] Examine Unmentioned Options ★ Core Discovery
  → There is one more option they could have chosen:
    Matt 12:43 "When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through
              dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none."
    → Namely, **wandering without a body is possible.** Only they "findeth none."
  → Yet the Legion **did not even bring** this option to the negotiation table.

  | Option | Textual Ground | Reaction |
  |---------------|-----------|-----------------|
  | The deep (bottomless pit) | Luke 8:31 | Explicit refusal |
  | Wandering hostless | Matt 12:43 | **Not even mentioned** |
  | New hosts | Mark 5:12 | Voluntary demand |

[STEP 4] Confirm Priorities
  Minimum Line (Evading the deep) + Voluntary Alternative (Securing hosts) + Unmentioned (Evading wandering)
  → Actual Priority: **Evading the bodiless state itself.**
     The refusal of the deep and the unmentioned wandering point in the same direction.

[STEP 5] Judgment
  Demand hierarchy structure : ✅ BIBLICAL — STRONG (3-author cross-witness)
  "Evading the bodiless state is the priority" : ✅ STRONG (Luke 8:31 + Matt 12:43, 2 authors)
  Inner motive (Why they desired bodies) : Unstated in the text → Unconfirmable
```

## 📌 Reference Case B: Abraham's Intercession for Sodom (Gen 18:23-32)

```
  Demand Hierarchy: 50 → 45 → 40 → 30 → 20 → 10 (Sequential retreat in 6 stages)
  Voluntary Alternative: None — Abraham lowers the standard without presenting an alternative
  Unmentioned Option: Requesting the individual rescue of his nephew Lot (what actually happens in Gen 19)
                → Abraham did not request this

  → A type with only a hierarchical structure and no voluntary alternative. Contrasts with Case A.
  → ⚠️ TYPE-AV/AW/AX do not trigger in this case.
     This is a real example of TYPE-AY triggering **alone**, and a basis for why the 4 types should not be merged into one.
```

---

## 5. ⚠️ Do Not Confuse

| Weapon | What is Examined |
|:---:|:---|
| **TYPE-AY** (This Weapon) | **Hierarchy and retreat order** of multiple demands |
| `TYPE-AV` (Statement Analysis) | **Voluntariness** of utterance (speaking unasked) |
| `TYPE-AN` (Moving Goalposts) | The fallacy where the counterpart **shifts the criterion of judgment itself** |
| `TYPE-AF` (Burden of Proof) | The **allocation** of who must prove |
| `TYPE-AG` (Argument from Silence) | **Absence** in the transmission of literature |

> The "demand retreat" of TYPE-AY is not a logical fallacy but a **normal negotiation act**. TYPE-AN (Moving Goalposts) is a fallacy-detection weapon, so confusing them will misjudge normal negotiation as a fallacy.

## 6. 🔗 Linked TYPEs

```
TYPE-AY (Demand Hierarchy Analysis)
    ├─ Passing the list of demands     → TYPE-AX (Cui Bono Reverse Calculation)
    ├─ Cross-confirming utterance voluntariness → TYPE-AV (Statement Analysis)
    ├─ Judging unmentioned options   → TYPE-AG (Argument from Silence)
    └─ Contrasting demands and execution   → TYPE-AW (Action–Statement Inconsistency)
```

---

## 7. 🚧 Prevention of Abuse

```
□ Do not omit STEP 3 (Examine Unmentioned Options). Looking only at what was demanded is looking at only half.
□ The unmentioned options must be substantively recorded elsewhere in Scripture.
  → Judging that an option we imagined was "evaded" is a misuse of TYPE-AG.
□ Do not use maximum demands as grounds for priority (they may be a negotiation card).
□ Do not confirm the inner motive of the demander unless the text states it.
□ Do not read a demand retreat itself as evidence of "weakness" or "falsehood" — it is a normal negotiation act.
```
