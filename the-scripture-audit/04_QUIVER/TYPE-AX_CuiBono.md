# 🗡️ SKILL-52: TYPE-AX — Reverse Deduction of Beneficiary (Cui Bono)

**"Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them." — Matthew 7:20 KJV**

> **When triggered**: **When the result of an event is recorded in the text, but the motive is not recorded.**
> **Core Question**: *"What is the actual result brought forth by this event, and **with whose demands doth this result accord?**"*

---

## 1. 🎯 Weapon Overview

The Latin legal maxim *cui bono* ("to whose benefit") is an investigative technique that backwardly deduces the motive from the result in an event where the motive is unknown.

TYPE-AX traces the **chain of results** recorded in the text unto the end, and then compareth that final result with the **demands vocally manifested** by the parties to the event. If the result and the demands perfectly interlock, it is strong circumstantial evidence for the motive.

*   **Classification:** Investigative Forensics (Motive Reconstruction)
*   **Base Layer:** Rhetoric·Criminology (`01_MANDATE/IDENTITY_Scribe42.md` knowledge base)
*   **Target:** Backwardly deduce the motive whereunto the text is silent, using only the results recorded by the text.

---

## 2. ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ The result of the event is recorded in the text
□ The motive of the parties to the event is not described in the text
□ The demands or intentions manifested beforehand by the parties are recorded in the text (securing an object of comparison)
□ The causal chain leading from the result unto the parties can be traced within the text
```

> ⚠️ The fourth condition is the core. If the causal chain goeth without the text, this weapon is not used.

---

## 3. 🔍 Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Trace the Causal Chain — Only within the text
  → Array the results in order from immediately after the event until the text ceaseth to record.
  → | Step | Result | Verse |
  → 🚨 Do not add results not recorded in the text by inference.

[STEP 2] Establish the Final Result
  → Specify the last term of the chain in a single sentence.

[STEP 3] List the Manifested Demands
  → Gather all demands vocally manifested by the parties before the event.
  → Receive the deliverables of TYPE-AV (Statement Analysis) / TYPE-AY (Demand Hierarchy Analysis) exactly as they are.

[STEP 4] Comparison
  → Compare the final result ↔ the manifested demands 1:1.
  → Judge it as one of: Accord / Partial Accord / Irrelevant / Contrary.

[STEP 5] Examination of Competing Beneficiaries (Mandatory — Omission Forbidden)
  → Ye must needs ask: "Are there no other entities who profited by this result?"
  → Array all candidates and compare them each.
  → If it be not the sole beneficiary, downgrade the strength of the circumstance.

[STEP 6] Judgment
  → The chain of results itself        : Letter of the text → EXPLICIT
  → Accord of result and demands     : Result of comparison → EXPLICIT
  → "Therefore, that was the motive" : If the text speaketh not the motive 🟡 Strong Opinion is the upper limit
```

---

## 4. 📌 Reference Case: The Herd of Swine at Gerasa (Mark 5:10-18)

```
[STEP 1~2] Trace the Result Chain

  | Step | Result                                          | Verse   |
  |------|-------------------------------------------------|---------|
  | ①    | About 2,000 swine drowned                       | Mark 5:13 |
  | ②    | They that fed the swine fled, and told it in the city and in the country | Mark 5:14 |
  | ③    | They came to see, and were afraid               | Mark 5:15 |
  | ④    | They prayed him to depart out of their coasts   | Mark 5:17 |
  | ⑤    | Jesus entered into the ship (actually departed) | Mark 5:18 |

  Final Result: Jesus departed out of that region.

[STEP 3] Manifested Demands (Receiving TYPE-AV deliverables)
  Demand ①: Torment me not                           (Mark 5:7 / Matt 8:29)
  Demand ②: Send us not away out of the country      (Mark 5:10)  ← Concerning the region
  Demand ③: Command us not to go out into the deep   (Luke 8:31)
  Demand ④: Send us into the swine                   (Mark 5:12)

[STEP 4] Comparison
  Final Result (Jesus departeth the region) ↔ Demand ② (Send us not away out of the country)
  → Between the two parties, he that actually departed the region was not the devils, but Jesus.
  → Judgment: **Accordeth with Demand ②** (The demander remaineth, he that is expelled is the opposing side)

[STEP 5] Examination of Competing Beneficiaries
  · Owners/Keepers of swine → Economic loss. No profit ❌
  · Citizens of the city    → Fallen into fear. No profit ❌
  · He that was healed      → Made whole (Mark 5:15). Yet obtained not by the destruction of swine ❌
  · Legion                  → Remained in the region + Threat of expulsion removed ✅ **Sole Beneficiary**

[STEP 6] Judgment
  Chain of results             : ✅ EXPLICIT (Mark 5:13→14→15→17→18 continuous record)
  Accord of result and demand② : ✅ EXPLICIT
  Sole Beneficiary             : ✅ STRONG (All 3 competing candidates dismissed)
  "Destruction was the motive" : 🟡 Strong Opinion — The text describeth not the motive, and
                                 TYPE-AW competing hypothesis (a) loss of control dieth not by internal contradiction

  ⚠️ Declaration of Honesty: What this weapon establisheth extendeth only unto "the result accordeth with demand ② and Legion is the sole beneficiary." To exalt it unto "therefore a destruction planned from the beginning," a description of motive in the text is required, and that is absent.
```

---

## 5. ⚠️ Prohibition of Confusion

| Weapon | What is seen |
|:---:|:---|
| **TYPE-AX** (This weapon) | **Result → Motive** reverse deduction. Who hath profited |
| `TYPE-AD` (Abduction) | Inference to the best explanation in general. **Not limited to the beneficiary of the result** |
| `TYPE-AW` (Action–Statement Inconsistency) | The **variance itself** betwixt word and deed |
| `TYPE-O` (Physical Traces) | Presence or absence of **physical evidence** that the event should have left |
| `TYPE-L` (Chain Inference) | **Construction of a logic chain** using an anchor as a lever |

> While TYPE-AD seeketh "the most plausible explanation," TYPE-AX useth only one specific lens: **"the structure of benefit of the result."**

---

## 6. 🔗 Linked TYPEs

```
TYPE-AX (Cui Bono Reverse Deduction)
    ├─ Receiving list of demands     → TYPE-AV / TYPE-AY
    ├─ Discernment of variance cause → TYPE-AW (Action–Statement Inconsistency)
    ├─ Dismissal of all competitors  → TYPE-E (Competing Models)
    └─ Completion of the chain       → TYPE-L (Chain Inference)
```

---

## 7. 🚧 Prevention of Abuse

```
□ The chain of results must be traced only within the text. It ceaseth where the text ceaseth to record.
□ NEVER omit STEP 5 (Examination of Competing Beneficiaries).
  → If omitted, the confirmation bias of "he whom I wish to designate is the beneficiary" passeth through as it is.
□ Establishment of motive is possible only when there is an explicit description in the text.
  → If absent, 🟡 Strong Opinion is the upper limit (`02_TACTICS/ANCHOR_ThirdData.md` STEP 4-P ③).
□ The inference "he profited, therefore he planned it" is a logical leap — congruity of result and prior intention are
  separate propositions, and the latter requireth distinct evidence.
```
