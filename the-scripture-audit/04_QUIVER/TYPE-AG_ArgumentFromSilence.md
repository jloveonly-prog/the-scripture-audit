# 🏹 SKILL-34: TYPE-AG — Argument from Silence
**"The scriptures are not silent — it is always their grounds that are silent"** (Revised 2026-07-22)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Whose silence is it? — Ye must strictly distinguish**
> - **"The scripture is silent"** ❌ Never use this. The scriptures are not silent on what is necessary for faith and life (2 Tim 3:16-17). This expression is a frame that implies a flaw in the scriptures themselves, and conflicts with our identity (`IDENTITY_Scribe42.md` — The King's Searcher).
> - **"They (opposing claims/traditions) are silent"** ✅ This is the correct expression. If the adversary brings forth a claim, yet no grounds to support it are found anywhere in the entire transmission of related texts — **that is not a flaw of the scripture, but a flaw of their claim.** Our identity forgives not this silence: we shall point out unto the end that a groundless claim is groundless.

> **When to invoke**: When the adversary's claim is **utterly silent** in the whole transmission of related texts — that is, when they cannot present even a single verse to back their claim.
> This silence could mean "they knew not," or "there was no need to record," or "that event came not to pass" — in any case, **the fact remains unchanged that the adversary cannot prove their claim by the text.**
>
> **One-line definition**: *"That which the scripture mentioneth not at all, they cannot call scriptural truth."*

---

## Conditions of Invocation
```
[General Use]
1. When the adversary's claim appeareth nowhere in the scriptures.
2. When a certain doctrine/event/person is utterly silent in the whole transmission of related texts.
   (e.g., the journey of a specific person, an event in a specific place, an unmentioned doctrine)
3. When formulating "not in the scripture = their claim hath no grounds."
4. When outward tradition/supposition/interpretation attempteth to precede the inward record of the scripture.

[Typical Code] Invoke immediately upon detecting one or more of the following sentences:
  - "There is no record of [Claim] in the scriptures"
  - "The scripture maketh no mention of [Doctrine/Person/Event]..."
  - "It is a tradition from [after AD 180, after 400...]"
```

## Execution Steps
```
[STEP 1] Determine the scope of their silence
  → Confirm verses where the adversary's claim is mentioned in related texts: 0 or N
  → Scan the whole of related scripture books (Acts, Epistles, Gospels, etc.)

[STEP 2] Analyze the nature of the silence — Understand it by the guidance of the Holy Ghost (2 Pet 1:21)
  → Was there an opportunity for their claim to be recorded? (Did the author, who recorded other details precisely, skip only this?)
  → If there was an opportunity, yet their claim appeared not → Elevate to "meaningful silence"
  → If there was no opportunity at all (texts unrelated to the subject) → Use not as evidence in this STEP

[STEP 3] Contrast their silence vs. our grounds
  → Our claim: Inward structural grounds in the scripture exist ✅ / ❌
  → Opposing claim: Utter silence in the whole transmission of related texts

[STEP 4] Judgment
  → Their claim, which is silent in the whole transmission of related texts, hath no scriptural grounds.
  → Our claim, which hath active grounds inward of the scripture, prevaileth.
  → ⚠️ However, do not declare a final "CONFIRMED" solely upon this silence — determine the final grade (Probability/STRONG/IRONCLAD) through COMBO combination with other TYPEs. Silence confirmeth their lack of grounds, but doth not automatically grant the highest grade of certainty to our active proposition.
```
