# 🏹 TYPE-X: Chiastic Structure / Chiasmus

> **Alias**: Textual X-Ray
> **Hermeneutical Background**: Ancient Hebrew literary cognitive systems and symmetric recording techniques
> **Core Philosophy**: The text is designed in a decalcomania (mirror) structure of A-B-C-B'-A', and the true core message of the text is located at the **very center (C)**, not the first or last verse.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Western (Hellenistic) logic develops linearly from introduction-body-conclusion, but biblical (Hebrew) logic is chiastic. When the prosecution cherry-picks a peripheral verse (A or B) to attack, deploy TYPE-X to expose the entire skeleton and prove that the verse presented by the prosecution is not the 'main theme'.

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Define the Boundary
  → Establish the beginning and end of the debated paragraph.

[STEP 2] Map the Pairs
  → Find and match the 'symmetric pairs' (A and A', B and B') where the meaning of the sentences or core vocabulary is repeated.

[STEP 3] Isolate the Center (C)
  → Identify the 'C' part, which is the only verse without a pair, or located at the apex of the symmetry.

[STEP 4] Defend via the Axis
  → Counterattack: "The core theme of this paragraph is 'C'. The 'B' verse you quoted is an auxiliary device to explain 'C', so making B an independent doctrine is destroying the context."
```
