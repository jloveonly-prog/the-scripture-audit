# 🏹 SKILL-36: TYPE-AI — Reductio ad Absurdum
**"If you push the opposing hypothesis to the end, it collapses within the Scripture"**

> **When it triggers**: When assuming the opposing hypothesis (Roman hypothesis) as true and tracing its logical consequences to the end.
> If the consequence causes an internal contradiction within the Scripture, it proves that the hypothesis itself is false.
>
> **One-line definition**: *"If what you say is true, this should happen in the Bible — but that is impossible."*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Difference from TYPE-AC (Counterfactual Contradiction)**:
> - TYPE-AC = Confirms that the opposing hypothesis causes internal contradictions within the Scripture.
> - TYPE-AI = **Actively assumes** the opposing hypothesis as true, logically develops its consequences, and leads it to self-destruction.
> TYPE-AC is defense / TYPE-AI is offense.

---

## Trigger Conditions
`
[General]
1. When the opposing hypothesis avoids logical consequences.
2. When tracing "If [Hypothesis X] is true, what must happen in the Scripture?"
3. When inducing the self-destruction of the opposing hypothesis through reductio ad absurdum.

[Typical Code] Trigger immediately upon detecting one or more of the following situations:
  - When the opponent repeats superficial claims without addressing the consequences of the hypothesis.
  - When developing "If [Hypothesis] is true, how do you explain [Scripture X verse/doctrine]?"
  - When a logical structure where two claims cannot both be true simultaneously is detected.
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Hypothetical Adoption
  → Temporarily assume the opposing hypothesis as true.
  → "Let us assume Peter died in Rome."

[STEP 2] Consequence Tracing
  → If that assumption is true, what must hold true within the Scripture?

[STEP 3] Internal Scriptural Verification of Consequences
  → Do the traced consequences actually exist within the Scripture?

[STEP 4] Absurdity Declaration
  → If the consequences do not exist in the Scripture or cause a contradiction:
  → "∴ The hypothesis is false."
`

## 📌 Reference Case: Reductio ad Absurdum Verification of the Roman Hypothesis

**Assumption**: "Peter died by crucifixion in Rome."

| Consequence | Actual Presence in Scripture | Judgment |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| Consequence 1: Rome must be the ὅπου of John 13:36 | ❌ Not in the Scripture | Contradiction |
| Consequence 2: ἀκολούθει in John 21:19 must mean "follow to Rome" | ❌ No mention of Rome | Contradiction |
| Consequence 3: The exclusivity of σύ (only you) must hold true in Rome | ❌ Other disciples also ministered in Rome | Contradiction |
| Consequence 4: The Scripture must mention Rome as the place fulfilling the martyrdom prophecy | ❌ Complete silence | Contradiction |

> **TYPE-AI Verdict**: Assuming the Roman hypothesis as true, all 4 internal scriptural consequences fail to hold.
> ∴ By reductio ad absurdum, the Roman hypothesis is false. ✅ CONFIRMED
