# 🔬 SKILL-01: TYPE-A — Chronological Serial Decomposition Pipeline

> **When to Activate**: When the numbers in two texts conflict, and existing academia treats it as "the two periods overlap".
> **Core Suspicion**: "Do the periods really overlap? Or does adding them sequentially yield a new number?"

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Collect All Related Verses
  → List all related verses without omission (parallel texts, genealogies, reign years of kings, etc.) alongside the two conflicting verses.
  → Make a list of all extractable numbers (age, reign period, age at death) from each verse along with the KJV original text.

[STEP 2] Timeline Serial Placement (Excel Method)
  → Do not view the periods as 'overlapping (Inclusive)', but place them 'serially (Sequential)' in the order of the text's narrative.
  → Create a Table: | Period | Start Age/Year | End Age/Year | Biblical Basis |
  → However, unless the text explicitly expresses "at the same time" or "during that period", unconditionally assume it is serial.

[STEP 3] Reverse Calculation of Unknown (X)
  → Adding the conflicting numbers in a serial placement reveals the hidden total years (X).
  → Verify if this X matches the second number under attack (e.g., 42 years old).

[STEP 4] Biological Coherence Verification
  → Is the derived age (X) biologically possible based on the era's standards?
  → Cross-validate with father-son relationships, marriage ages, and figures of other contemporaries.

[STEP 5] Linguistic Verification
  → Do the prepositions, tenses, and grammatical structures of the KJV original text support the serial interpretation?
  → Check the roots and semantic ranges of the Hebrew/Greek original texts.
```

## 📌 Reference Case: The Ahaziah Chronology Dilemma

```
[Collected Verses]: 2 Kings 8:26 (22 years old), 2 Chronicles 22:2 (42 years old), 2 Chronicles 21:5 (8 years reign), 2 Chronicles 21:18-19 (2 years illness)

[Serial Placement]:
  | Period          | Start | End | Biblical Basis       |
  |:-------------|:---:|:---:|:--------------|
  | Jehoram's Age at Accession | 32 yrs | -   | 2 Chron 21:5 reverse calc |
  | Jehoram's Reign Period | 32 yrs | 40 yrs | 2 Chron 21:5 (8 years)|
  | Jehoram's Illness Period | 40 yrs | 42 yrs | 2 Chron 21:18-19 |
  | Ahaziah's Accession   | -   | 22 yrs | 2 Kings 8:26      |

[Reverse Calculation]:
  32 + 8 + 2 = 42 ← The reality of '42 years old' in 2 Chron 22:2 (Jehoram's age coordinate) ✅

[Verification]:
  Jehoram had a son around age 18 (40 - 22 = 18 years old) → Biologically possible ✅
  KJV "forty and two years old WAS Ahaziah"
  Hebrew Ben idiom = Relational age expression (age coordinate) ✅
```
