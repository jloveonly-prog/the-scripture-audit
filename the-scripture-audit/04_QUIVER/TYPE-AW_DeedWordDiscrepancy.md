# 🗡️ SKILL-51: TYPE-AW — Deed–Word Discrepancy

**"They profess that they know God; but in works they deny him." — Titus 1:16 KJV**

> **When to trigger**: When both the **words** and **deeds** of the same subject are recorded in the text.
> **Core question**: *"Are the words spoken and the deeds done pointing in the same direction? If they differ, **the deed is the truth.**"*

---

## 1. 🎯 Weapon Overview

The first principle of investigation: **Statements can be manipulated, but deeds leave traces.** When words and deeds contradict, deeds are adopted first.

TYPE-AW is a weapon that places a subject's recorded words and deeds side by side to examine the **consistency of direction**. If a discrepancy is confirmed, that point becomes the forensic core of this case.

*   **Classification:** Behavioral Forensics
*   **Base Layer:** Rhetoric·Behavioral Analysis (Based on `01_MANDATE/IDENTITY_Scribe42.md` knowledge)
*   **Target:** Detection of self-contradiction. It is established regardless of theological stance, making it the easiest to refute.

---

## 2. ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ The words of the same subject are recorded in the text
□ The deeds of that subject are also recorded in the text
□ The chronological order between the two can be established (DE-OVERLAP Principle 1 applied)
□ The words and deeds are directed at the same object and the same matter
```

---

## 3. 🔍 Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Separate Record of Words and Deeds
  → | Time | Words (KJV) | Deeds (KJV) | Verse |
  → Chronological order must be established. If not specified as "at the same time," place in series
    (`02_TACTICS/DEOVERLAP_Serial.md` Principle 1).

[STEP 2] Direction Contrast
  → Describe the direction indicated by the words and the actual direction of the deeds in one sentence each.
  → Judge as one of: Consistent / Discrepant / Indeterminable.

[STEP 3] Upon Discrepancy — Adopt Deeds First
  → Adopt the deeds as truth, rather than the statements.
  → 🚨 However, do not conclude the statement is a "lie." Unless the text says so,
    all that can be established is the fact that "words and deeds contradicted."

[STEP 4] Exhaustive Competing Explanations (Linked with TYPE-E)
  → List all hypotheses explaining the discrepancy. Minimum 2.
    Usually (a) Loss of control·limit of ability (b) Statement was a means to begin with (c) Change in circumstances
  → Contrast each hypothesis with text data and reject those that can be rejected.
  → If not all are rejected, 🟡 Prevailing View is the upper limit (IRONCLAD impossible).

[STEP 5] Judgment
  → The fact of discrepancy itself: Can be established by the text's wording → EXPLICIT
  → The cause of discrepancy    : Cannot be established if the text does not describe motives → Prevailing View or lower
```

## 4. 📌 Reference Case A: Legion of the Gadarenes (Mark 5:12-13)

```
[STEP 1] Separation of Words and Deeds

  | Time | Words                                 | Deeds                         | Verse    |
  |------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------|----------|
  | T1   | "Send us into the swine, that we may  |                               | Mark 5:12|
  |      |  enter into them" (Asking for a host) |                               |          |
  | T2   |                                       | Entered into the herd of swine| Mark 5:13|
  | T3   |                                       | About 2,000 drowned immediately| Mark 5:13|

[STEP 2] Direction Contrast
  Direction of Words : Secure a host (desiring a habitation)
  Direction of Deeds : Immediate destruction of all hosts
  → 💥 Discrepancy

[STEP 3] Deeds First
  → The statement that "securing a host" was the ultimate goal contradicts the deeds.

[STEP 4] Exhaustive Competing Explanations
  (a) Loss of control — Legion size could not handle the herd of swine
  (b) Destruction was the goal — The host was a means
  (c) Change in circumstances — Unexpected factor arose after entering (No textual basis → Rejected)

  → Both (a) and (b) do not die from internal textual contradiction.
  → Trigger TYPE-AX (Cui Bono) in link to compare outcome consistency.

[STEP 5] Judgment
  Fact of discrepancy   : ✅ EXPLICIT (Mark 5:12 and 5:13 are recorded consecutively in one paragraph)
  Cause of discrepancy  : 🟡 Prevailing View upper limit — The text does not describe motives
```

## 📌 Reference Case B: Peter's Denial (Matt 26:35 → 26:69-75)

```
  Words T1 : "Though I should die with thee, yet will I not deny thee" (Matt 26:35)
  Deeds T2 : Denied three times (Matt 26:70, 72, 74)
  → 💥 Discrepancy. And in this case, the text itself reveals the cause —
     Because Jesus' foretelling (Matt 26:34) precedes it, it is established by the text
     that it is an error in the speaker's self-awareness, not (c) a change in circumstances.
  → Judgment: ✅ EXPLICIT (Both discrepancy + cause specified in text)

  ⚠️ Difference from Case A: In A, the text is silent on motive; in B, the text speaks the motive.
     Even with the same weapon, it is only established as much as the text tells.
```

---

## 5. ⚠️ Do Not Confuse

| Weapon | What It Looks At |
|:---:|:---|
| **TYPE-AW** (This weapon) | **Words vs Deeds** of the same subject |
| `TYPE-AV` (Statement Analysis) | Voluntary/Involuntary distinction **within** the spoken content |
| `TYPE-AC` (Counter-Hypothesis Test) | Substituting ¬A to detect contradictions **between texts** |
| `TYPE-AI` (Reductio ad Absurdum) | Substituting opponent's hypothesis to explode **logical** contradictions |
| `TYPE-AN` (Moving the Goalposts) | Opponent **shifting the standard itself** |

> TYPE-AW only looks at the word↔deed contradiction **within a single subject**. A clash between two documents or two statements is TYPE-AC.

---

## 6. 🔗 Linked TYPEs

```
TYPE-AW (Deed–Word Discrepancy)
    ├─ Compare discrepancy cause candidates → TYPE-AX (Cui Bono Reverse Calculation)
    ├─ Exhaustively reject competing hypotheses → TYPE-E (Competing Models)
    ├─ Adopt the simplest explanation → TYPE-AK (Occam's Razor)
    └─ Internal analysis of words precedes → TYPE-AV (Statement Analysis)
```

---

## 7. 🚧 Prevention of Abuse

```
□ Do not conclude they "lied." All that can be established is they "contradicted."
□ First establish the chronological order of words and deeds — Do not use this weapon if the order is unclear.
□ The cause of discrepancy has the Prevailing View as its upper limit if the text does not speak it.
  → If competing explanations do not die from internal contradiction, IRONCLAD is impossible
    (`02_TACTICS/ANCHOR_ThirdData.md` STEP 4-P Gateway ③).
```
