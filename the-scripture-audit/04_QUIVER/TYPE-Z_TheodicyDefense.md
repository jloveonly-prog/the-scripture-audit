# 🏹 TYPE-Z: Theodicy Defense

> **Alias**: Altitude of the Judge
> **Old Version Code**: A-10
> **Hermeneutical Background**: Theodicy (The coexistence of the existence of evil and the goodness of God)
> **Core Philosophy**: Some of the dilemmas raised by critics are not about numbers in the text being wrong, but are attacks on philosophical and moral character: **"Why does a God of love and omnipotence allow evil?"** This originates from the visual limitation of a human trapped in a finite 100-year timeline attempting to judge the purpose of infinite eternity.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Activate TYPE-Z when the prosecution mocks, "If God is omnipotent, shouldn't He kill Satan right away? He is a failing God." Replace God's 'delay of judgment' not with an absence of power, but with 'patience of grace', and prove the inevitability of free will to shatter philosophical attacks.

---

## Activation Conditions

```
Activate TYPE-Z in the following situations:
1. When the essence of the dilemma is C-08 (Theological Inquiry / Moral Attack).
2. When asked, "Why was hell created?", "Why was the Tree of Knowledge created?", or "Why is the devil kept alive?".
3. When the attack is not on contradictions between texts, but on the character of God depicted in the Bible.
```

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Free-Will Retorsion
  → Argue that if the possibility of evil (the Tree of Knowledge / the Fall) had been completely blocked from the source, humans would be nothing more than programmed robots without the right to choose.
  → Defend with: "Forced love is not love, and to obtain a true object of love, God took the immense risk of the creature's betrayal (evil)."

[STEP 2] Extending the Timeline
  → The attacker attacks looking only at the narrow dot of 'present suffering'.
  → Extend the line, bringing forward the entire line of ultimate victory and restoration in Revelation 20 (the New Heaven and the New Earth). Prove that in the face of infinite eternity, present evil and suffering are merely temporary stage props.

[STEP 3] Nature of the Delay
  → "Not judging immediately is not because of incompetence" (2 Peter 3:9)
  → Return it with TYPE-P (Retorsion / Boomerang) that if evil were to be eradicated immediately, all sinners on earth, including the critic (prosecution) themselves, would have to be thrown into hell right now. Rule that the delay of judgment is extreme grace giving creatures a chance to repent.
```

---

## 📌 Reference Cases

### Case: Why doesn't God destroy Satan immediately?
*   **Controversy**: If God is omnipotent, why didn't He kill the devil immediately when he fell, but instead let him ruin the earth? Is it God's incompetence or cruelty?
*   **TYPE-Z Application**:
    *   **Free Will and Rebellion**: Satan (Lucifer) was also created not as a forced machine but as a creature with perfect freedom. Perfection without the possibility of rebellion is a program, not life.
    *   **Delay of Judgment and Boomerang**: If God were someone who judges 'immediately' when sin and rebellion occur, no one in human history could survive. The critic demanding immediate execution for Satan falls into the fatal contradiction of having to endure their own immediate trip to hell for their own sins.
    *   **Final Timeline**: In Revelation 20, the devil is eventually thrown into the lake of fire forever. It is only delayed; justice will absolutely be executed.
*   **Ruling**: Satan's temporary persistence is not God's incompetence. It is the sublime governing act of the Judge, historically proving the destructive end of sin to creatures, while simultaneously buying 'time' for sinners to turn back through the grace of the cross. Attack dismissed.
