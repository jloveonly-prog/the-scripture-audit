# ⚖️ TYPE-AA: Dissection of Ancient Law & Culture

> **When to Invoke**: When the 'subject' of an action recorded in the biblical text differs (e.g., did the centurion come directly, or did he send a delegation?), or when figures or expressions appear that seem like obvious 'exaggerations' from a modern perspective.
> **Core Doubt**: "Is this a narrative from a modern Western perspective, or an established legal/cultural custom (Idiom) of the 1st-century ancient Near East?"

---

## Execution Steps

```text
[STEP 1] Identify the Conflict Aspect and Custom Type
  Find the corresponding one among the following ancient laws/cultural customs:
  □ Ancient Agency Law (Shaliach Principle)
    : The ancient Near Eastern and Jewish law that "the act of the one sent (agent) is legally perfectly identical to the act of the sender (principal)."
  □ Hebrew Idiomatic Figures and Hyperbole (Hyperbole / Round Numbers)
    : Not exact statistical figures, but established rhetorical expressions intended to indicate literary emphasis or overall scale.

[STEP 2] Prove with Biblical Cross-References
  → Find and contrast other biblical passages where the same custom is applied.
  → Through this, prove that the narrative in question is not a simple 'error' but the 'standard narrative style' of the time.

[STEP 3] Apply Modern Legal/Cultural Analogy
  → Refute the attacker's unreasonableness using the same logic that modern people also unconsciously use in everyday life as an analogy.
  → Ex: "The President built a road" (A true proposition by agency law, even though the President did not pick up a shovel directly).

[STEP 4] Counterattack with Retorsion (Reductio ad absurdum)
  → Point out the comedic absurdity that occurs when the attacker's logic (literalist modern standard) is applied to the entire Bible.
```

## 📌 Reference Case 1: Healing of the Centurion's Servant (Ancient Agency Law)

```text
[Conflict]:
  Matthew 8:5 — The centurion "directly" comes to Jesus and pleads.
  Luke 7:3 — The centurion sends "Jewish elders (a delegation)" to Jesus.

[Custom Identification]: Ancient Agency Law
  → The "Shaliach (apostle/agent)" principle specified in Jewish rabbinic literature (Talmud, etc.): "A person's agent is like the person himself."

[Modern Analogy]:
  If an article says "The US bombed Iraq," did President Biden directly fly a fighter jet?
  No. The act of the US military is described as the act of the US.
  Matthew focused on the centurion as the 'legal subject (principal)', and Luke focused on the 'historical process (agent)'; both are the perfect truth.

[Verdict]: ✅ CONSISTENT — Perfect coherence by ancient agency law
```

## 📌 Reference Case 2: Healing of the Blind and the Size of the Crowd (Hebrew Hyperbole)

```text
[Conflict]:
  Expressions like "The whole city gathered," "walls reaching to heaven." Does this mean literally 100% of the population came, or the walls reached the atmosphere?

[Custom Identification]: Hebrew Idiomatic Hyperbole

[Proof]:
  Modern people also say "I went to the mart yesterday and everyone in the world was there," or "I'm starving to death." Attacking this by saying "80 billion people didn't come to the mart, so you are a liar" is autistic literalism that ignores the social function of language.
```
