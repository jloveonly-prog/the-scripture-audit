# 🏹 TYPE-U: Law of First Mention

> **Alias**: Anchor of the Prototype
> **Hermeneutical Background**: Progressive Revelation
> **Core Philosophy**: The verse where a word, object, or concept **first** appears in the Bible determines its 'spiritual DNA' and the absolute standard of its meaning.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> As eras pass and we move toward the latter parts of the Bible, the usages of a word may seem to diversify, but its fundamental root can never deviate from the definition of its first mention. When the prosecution (critic) argues for the 'flexibility' or 'dispensational mutation' of meaning, drop the anchor of the first mention to forcefully constrain the expansion of meaning.

---

## Trigger Conditions

```
Trigger TYPE-U in the following situations:
1. When a debate arises over the meaning of a specific theological term (e.g., Babylon, Serpent, Blood, Grace, etc.).
2. When the prosecution claims, "This word must be interpreted differently/symbolically in this context."
3. When the spiritual nature (whether it is good or evil) of a specific word needs to be established.
```

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Identify the Keyword
  → Identify the original language (Hebrew/Greek) of the controversial key word.

[STEP 2] Find the First Mention
  → Find the verse where the word/concept is first used in the entire Bible (mostly in Genesis).

[STEP 3] Extract the DNA
  → Define the positive/negative nature and prototypical meaning the word holds through the context of its first mention verse.

[STEP 4] Drop the Anchor and Counter-Attack
  → Apply the derived prototypical meaning to the currently debated verse (e.g., in Revelation).
  → Declare, "According to the law of biblical consistency, this word cannot be interpreted in a way that destroys the DNA of its first mention."
```

## 📌 Reference Cases

### Case 1: The Identity of 'Babylon'
*   **Debate**: Is the 'Babylon' in Revelation 17-18 symbolic or literal?
*   **First Mention**: Genesis 11 (Tower of Babel - Babel, the etymology of Babylon)
*   **DNA Extraction**: Human pride, rebellion against God, religious/political amalgamation, the curse of scattering.
*   **Verdict**: The Babylon in Revelation is also not merely a simple political power, but is established as a 'global anti-God system (amalgamated religion + economy)' that inherits the spiritual DNA of Genesis 11.

### Case 2: The Nature of the 'Serpent'
*   **First Mention**: Genesis 3:1.
*   **DNA Extraction**: Subtil, altering the word of God ("Yea, hath God said?").
*   **Verdict**: Every motif of the 'serpent' that appears subsequently in the Bible cannot escape this DNA of deception and altering the word.
