# ⚡ SKILL-46: TYPE-AS — Authority Hierarchy Analysis
**"The heaven, even the heavens, are the LORD'S: but the earth hath he given to the children of men." — Psalm 115:16 KJV**

> **When to trigger**: When the **distinction** between "actions only God can do" and "actions creatures can also do" is the **point of contention**.
> In cases where words translated identically as "make/create" in the Bible actually indicate **different authority levels**,
> establish the **Authority Hierarchy** by isolating original words and thoroughly verifying subject qualifications.
> **Core Question**: *"Is this action the exclusive domain of God, or can a delegated creature perform it?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse with TYPE-G:**
> - `TYPE-G` = Anatomy of grammatical structure (conjunctions, gender/number/case, verb voice/mood)
> - `TYPE-AS` = Analysis of the **authority level** of an action (Exclusive vs. Delegated)
> TYPE-G dissects the grammar of a word / TYPE-AS judges the qualification for an action.

---

## ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ When a question regarding creation sovereignty is raised, such as "Can creatures create life?"
□ When an authority delegation question is raised, such as "Did God delegate X?"
□ When the same original "make" family of verbs is used at different meaning levels.
□ When an instance of "attempt without delegation → failure" is observed, such as Exod 8:18-19 (Failure of the magicians).
□ When needing to explain a creature's action without undermining God's creation sovereignty.
```

---

## ⚙️ Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Isolate Original Word for Action — Precisely isolate the original word of the contested action
  → Reveal the original word differences among words translated identically in KJV English.
  → Table Format:
  → | Original Word | English (KJV) | Precise Meaning | Action Subject Qualification | KJV Evidence |

[STEP 2] Exhaustive Verification of Subject Qualification — Search the subject of the original word throughout the KJV
  → Exhaustively collect the subjects from all verses where the original word verb is used.
  → If the subject is 100% God → Exclusive to God (Monopoly)
  → If the subject includes humans/angels/other entities → Delegation possible

[STEP 3] Collect "Delegation Failure" Cases — Cases of attempt→failure without God's delegation
  → Find cases in the Bible where a creature attempted the action but failed.
  → If there is a failure case → Evidence that it is "impossible without delegation"
  → If the reason for failure is specified → Basis for monopoly, such as "the finger of God"

[STEP 4] Output Authority Hierarchy Table
  → | Level | Authority | Exclusive to God? | Delegation Possible? | KJV Evidence | Delegation Failure Case |
  → Structure the hierarchy from Level 1 (Highest, Exclusive) to Level N (Lowest, Free).

[STEP 5] Placement of Contested Action in Hierarchy → Resolve Debate
  → Place the contested action into its corresponding Level.
  → If the Level is "Delegation Possible" → Creature's action possible + God's sovereignty preserved
  → If the Level is "Exclusive" → Confirmed that creature's action is impossible
```

## 📌 Reference Case: Bara vs Asah/Yatsar — Formation of the First World Nations

```
[STEP 1] Isolate Original Word:

  | Original Word | English (KJV) | Precise Meaning | Subject Qualification |
  |:---:|:---:|:---|:---:|
  | בָּרָא (Bara) | Create | Bringing forth the source of matter and life out of nothing | God only |
  | עָשָׂה (Asah) | Make | Combining already existing materials to make something new | God + Delegation possible |
  | יָצַר (Yatsar) | Form | Giving shape to already existing materials (dust, etc.) | God + Delegation possible |

[STEP 2] Exhaustive Verification of Subject Qualification:
  → Bara: Gen 1:1, 1:21, 1:27, 2:3, 5:1, 5:2, 6:7, Isa 40:26, 42:5, 43:1, 45:7, 45:18...
    → Subject = 100% God (0 exceptions) ✅
  → Asah: Gen 1:7, 1:16, 1:25, 1:31, 2:2, Exod 20:11...
    → Subject = God frequently + Humans can also be the subject (Exod 31:4 "Bezaleel made")
    → Delegation possible confirmed ✅
  → Yatsar: Gen 2:7, 2:8, 2:19, Isa 44:2...
    → Subject = God frequently + Isa 29:16 "the potter (human) frameth" usage exists
    → Delegation possible confirmed ✅

[STEP 3] Delegation Failure Case:
  → Exod 8:18-19 — Pharaoh's magicians tried to bring forth lice (living creatures)
    "but they could not"
    → Magicians' confession: "This is the finger of God"
    → Attempted dust→life without God's delegation → Failure = Evidence that it's impossible without delegation

[STEP 4] Authority Hierarchy Table:

  | Level | Authority | Exclusive to God? | Delegation Possible? | KJV Evidence | Delegation Failure Case |
  |:---:|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
  | 1 | Bara (Creation from nothing) | ✅ | ❌ | Gen 1:1 Subject 100% God | — |
  | 2 | Blessing of proliferation (Be fruitful) | ✅ | ❌ | Gen 1:22,28 Only God declares | — |
  | 3 | Asah/Yatsar (Formation) | Possible | ✅ | Exod 31:4 Bezaleel | Exod 8:18 Magicians failed |
  | 4 | Dominion | Possible | ✅ | Gen 1:28 Delegated to Adam | — |

[STEP 5] Placement of Contested Action:
  → The action of the sons of the first world = Level 3 (Asah/Yatsar) = Within the scope of possible delegation
  → God's creation sovereignty (Level 1~2) is not undermined
  → The magicians' failure in Exod 8:18-19 = The core is the presence/absence of Authorization
    → Pharaoh's magicians = Illegal attempt → Failure
    → Sons of the first world = Formation under legal delegation → Possible
```

## ⚠️ Precautions

```
□ A "Delegation Possible" verdict does not mean "Delegation actually occurred."
  → The actual occurrence of delegation must be proven by a separate anchor verse.
  → TYPE-AS establishes "possibility," and actual occurrence is confirmed by TYPE-L (Chain) + TYPE-AD (Abduction).
□ The exclusivity of Level 1 (Bara) is absolute.
  → Under no circumstances is there a case where a creature is the subject of Bara.
  → An interpretation that undermines this exclusivity directly conflicts with Col 1:16.
□ Exod 8:18-19 is the most powerful ammunition of this weapon.
  → The confession of "the finger of God" is an admission by the devil himself of having no delegation.
```
