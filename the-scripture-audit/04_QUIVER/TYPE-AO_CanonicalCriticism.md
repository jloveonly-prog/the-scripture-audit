# 🏹 SKILL-42: TYPE-AO — Canonical Criticism
**"What function does this verse serve within the overall biblical narrative?"**

> **When to Activate**: When confirming theological meaning by analyzing the position and function
> a specific verse occupies within the structure of the entire biblical Canon.
>
> **One-Line Definition**: *"A part of the Bible must be read in the context of the whole to reveal its function."*

---

## Activation Conditions
`
[General]
1. When a verse's meaning is unclear on its own but is confirmed in the flow of the entire canon
2. When OT prophecy and NT fulfillment operate throughout the entire canon
3. When proving what function a specific verse serves in the biblical overall narrative unit
4. When declaring a specific doctrine/event as necessary within the structure of the biblical canon

[Typical Code] Activate immediately upon detecting the following questions:
  - "What role does this verse/event play in the overall biblical narrative?"
  - "Without this, which part of the overall biblical narrative gets cut off?"
  - "The narrative unit completeness of OT [Prophetic Verse] → NT [Fulfillment Verse]"
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Confirm Canonical Position
  → Where is the analyzed verse positioned in the whole Bible?

[STEP 2] Analyze Narrative Function
  → What role does this verse play in the overall biblical narrative?
  → Prophecy / Fulfillment / Turning Point / Conclusion / Opening

[STEP 3] Check Connection Structure
  → How does this verse connect with other canonical books?

[STEP 4] Declare Canonical Meaning
  → "This verse serves the [Function] of the overall biblical narrative."
`

## 📌 Reference Case: The Canonical Function of Peter's Prophecy in John

| Verse | Canonical Function |
|:---|:---|
| John 13:36 | **Prophetic opening (開門)** of Peter's narrative — "What will follow hereafter" |
| John 18:15-27 | Denial — The **first failure point** of the prophecy |
| John 21:15-19 | Restoration + Reprophecy — **Confirmation and transition to command** of the prophecy |
| John 21:19b | ἀκολούθει — **Command to fulfill prophecy** (Inclusio closing) |

> **TYPE-AO Verdict**: The ὅπου prophecy of John 13:36 is canonically a narrative unit completed by the command of John 21:19.
> In this structure, leaving the referent of ὅπου as "somewhere" destroys the canonical narrative completion.
> ∴ ὅπου = Golgotha is the only canonical completion. ✅ CONFIRMED
