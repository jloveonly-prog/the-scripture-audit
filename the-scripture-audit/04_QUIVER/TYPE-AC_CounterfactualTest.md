# 🔬 SKILL-30: TYPE-AC — Counterfactual Contradiction Test
**"If applied inversely, Scripture itself collapses"**

> **When to Invoke**: When a claim is **difficult to prove directly**, or when you want to **securely confirm** a proof.
> Establish the opposite proposition (¬A) of your claim (A), and substitute it directly into the biblical text to verify **whether internal contradictions explode**.
> If a contradiction occurs → ¬A is false → A is confirmed as the only truth.
>
> **Core Question**: *"If this claim were wrong, which verses of the Bible would be contradicted?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse with TYPE-P (Reverse Logic):**
> - `TYPE-P` = Reversing the **analogy/inference** brought up by the opponent and returning it (taking the opponent's weapon and using it)
> - `TYPE-AC` = **You proactively** set a counter-hypothesis and substitute it into the biblical text → confirm automatic occurrence of contradiction
> TYPE-P is defensive reverse usage / TYPE-AC is active reductio ad absurdum.

---

## ⚙️ Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Establish Counterfactual Hypothesis (¬A)
  → Clearly declare the opposite proposition of your claim (A).
  → Ex: A = "Peter died at Calvary"
       ¬A = "Peter died somewhere other than Calvary"

[STEP 2] Substitute into Biblical Text
  → Directly substitute ¬A into relevant biblical verses.
  → Substitution formula: "If ¬A, what does this verse mean?"
  → Substitute into at least 3 or more independent verses.

[STEP 3] Determine the Occurrence of Contradiction
  → If a contradiction occurs in each verse, mark it with an ❌.
  → Contradiction classification:
    - 🔴 Severe: The words of Jesus/the biblical author themselves become false
    - 🟠 Moderate: Destruction of logical consistency
    - 🟡 Minor: Neutralization of meaning (becomes an unnecessary word/structure)

[STEP 4] Block Escape Routes
  → List all alternative interpretations (escape routes) intended to save ¬A.
  → Repeat STEP 2~3 for each escape route and block them equally.
  → If all escape routes are blocked → A = Confirmed as the sole truth.

[STEP 5] Create a Comprehensive Judgment Table
  → | Verification Item | Result of ¬A Substitution | Contradiction Level |
  → If all items are ❌ → A = IRONCLAD

[STEP 6] ⚡ COUNTER PUNCH — Constructive Dilemma Ultimatum (Optional, strongest finish)
  → After contradictions are established, present the opponent with only two options.
  → Whichever they choose, the opponent's claim cannot survive.

  Official Declaration:
  "You now have only two choices left.

  [Choice 1] Admit that there are contradictions in the Bible.
    → Admit that ἄρτι (now) is an unnecessary redundancy,
    → σύ (thou) is a meaningless emphasis,
    → and John 13:33 and 13:36 cannot simultaneously be true.
    → This is an abandonment of biblical inerrancy.

  [Choice 2] Interpret these verses without contradiction.
    → While maintaining ¬A (Peter ≠ Calvary),
    → For all the contradiction verses presented above,
    → Without missing a single one, simultaneously, and without new contradictions
    → Present an alternative interpretation.

  Whichever you choose, your claim cannot survive.
  Choice 1 destroys your theological foundation.
  Choice 2 is practically impossible."

  → This ultimatum wins whether the opponent objects or remains silent.
  → If they object → Duty to present an interpretation arises → Re-verify with STEP 2~5
  → If they remain silent → Acquiescence = Agreement
```

---

## 📊 Invocation Condition Checklist

```
□ Can the opposite proposition of my claim (A) be clearly established?
□ Are there 3 or more relevant biblical verses?
□ When substituting ¬A, do problems occur independently in each verse?
□ Can each problem be simultaneously resolved with "a single same counterargument"? (If so, TYPE-AC is weakened)
□ Do alternative interpretation escape routes exist? (If they exist, STEP 4 is mandatory)
```

> **Core Strength**: I don't have to directly prove A.
> ¬A destroys the biblical text itself → A is reflexively confirmed.

## 📌 Reference Case: Peter's Martyrdom at Calvary Counterfactual Test

**Claim (A)**: Peter was martyred at Calvary (Golgotha).
**Counter-Hypothesis (¬A)**: Peter died somewhere other than Calvary (Rome or an unspecified location).

```
[STEP 2~3] Results of Substitution into Biblical Text:

① Substitution of Exclusivity in John 13:36
  ¬A → "Follow" = General martyrdom
  → Since James, Andrew, and Philip were also martyred, exclusivity disappears
  → The prophecy Jesus gave only to Peter becomes a meaningless declaration 🔴

② Substitution of ἄρτι (now) in John 13:36
  ¬A → "Following" = Heaven or general martyrdom
  → "You cannot go to heaven now" → A self-evident fact for all living humans
  → The adverb of time becomes a completely unnecessary redundancy 🔴

③ Substitution for Simultaneous Truth of John 13:33 vs 13:36
  → John went to Calvary (John 19:26 — Fact)
  → If 13:33 = Calvary, Jesus' words are false → 13:33 = Confirmed as Heaven
  → If 13:36 is also Heaven, it's a contradiction where only Peter goes to Heaven
  → Remaining possibility: 13:36 = Calvary only 🔴

④ Substitution of σύ Emphatic Pronoun in John 21:22
  ¬A → "Follow me" = General discipleship or martyrdom anywhere
  → John also fits discipleship/death → No reason to exclude John
  → The very existence of the emphatic pronoun σύ cannot be explained 🔴

[STEP 4] Blocking Escape Routes:
  Attempting to escape with "general martyrdom" →
  ① Place adverb ὅπου — Cannot point to "martyrdom" (event) → Blocked at grammar stage
  ② ἄρτι (now) — No reason why martyrdom is impossible now → Blocked at meaning stage
  ③ Exclusivity — James and Andrew were also martyred → Blocked at logic stage
  ④ Structural chain — Only B-2 (Calvary) remains → Blocked at structural stage
  → All escape routes fully blocked

[STEP 5] Comprehensive Judgment:
  All 4 verifications are 🔴 + All 4 escape route stages blocked
  → A (Peter = Calvary) Confirmed IRONCLAD
```

## ⚠️ Precautions

```
□ The more contradictions that occur, the stronger it is — Secure at least 3 or more.
□ Each contradiction must be "independent of each other."
  → If they can be simultaneously resolved with a single counterargument, TYPE-AC is weakened.
□ Escape routes must be left open and blocked in advance.
  → If escape routes are ignored, the opponent will bring them up later to shake the argument.
□ TYPE-AC has maximum effect when invoked in the "final seal" stage after the proof is complete.
□ STEP 6 COUNTER PUNCH must only be invoked after STEP 1~5 are completed.
  → An ultimatum when contradictions are not established will be counterattacked.
□ If the opponent attempts Choice 2, immediately initiate a re-verification loop with STEP 2~5.
  → Even if a new interpretation appears, pass it through the same verification pipeline.
```
