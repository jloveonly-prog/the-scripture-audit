# 🏹 SKILL-40: TYPE-AM — False Dichotomy Detection
**The "A or B" framing actually hides more options**

> **When to Activate**: When the opponent restricts to only two categories what actually has more categories,
> like "Calvary or Rome" or "Explicit or Inference."
>
> **One-Line Definition**: *"Only two options? There are actually more."*

---

## Activation Conditions
`
[General]
1. When the opponent presents an "A or B" dichotomy (hiding options C, D...)
2. When a third possibility exists besides two biblical positions
3. When defending their argument with the logic that "If X is wrong, Y is right"

[Typical Code] Activate immediately upon detecting one or more of the following sentences:
  - "If not [Argument A], it must be [Argument B]"
  - "It's either explicit or inference"
  - "If [Hypothesis X] is wrong, [Hypothesis Y] is automatically right"
  → Present options C, D... in STEP 2 to collapse the dichotomy, then connect to TYPE-AD (Abduction)
`

## Execution Steps
`
[STEP 1] Identify Dichotomy Structure
  → Explicitly state the "A or B" structure presented by the opponent.

[STEP 2] Present Additional Categories
  → Show that C, D... actually exist besides A and B.

[STEP 3] Declare Dichotomy Collapse
  → "This is a false dichotomy. C exists between A and B."

[STEP 4] Reset Actual Categories
  → Reconstruct the argument with the correct classification system.
`

## 📌 Reference Cases

**Case 1: "Explicit or Inference"**

| Dichotomy | Actual Categories |
|:---|:---|
| Explicit (Single verse declaration) | ① Direct declaration in a single verse |
| Inference (Reader's connection) | ② Same author/same book narrative structure connection ← Calvary theory |
| — | ③ Different author/different book structure connection (Trinity) |
| — | ④ Interpretation by external tradition (Rome theory) |

**Case 2: "If Calvary theory is wrong, Rome theory is right"**
> Actually: Multiple possibilities exist such as Calvary theory, Rome theory, Ephesus theory, Jerusalem theory, etc.
> However, the only hypothesis with internal biblical evidence is the Calvary theory.
> → Collapse dichotomy, then confirm Calvary theory via Abduction (TYPE-AD)

> **TYPE-AM Verdict**: "Explicit or inference" is a false dichotomy.
> There are actually 4 categories, and the Calvary theory belongs to ②.
> ② is the same structure as the Trinity argument — downgrading this to "inference" also downgrades the Trinity. ✅
