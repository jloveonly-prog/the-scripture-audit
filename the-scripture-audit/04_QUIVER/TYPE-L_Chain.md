# 🔬 SKILL-12: TYPE-L — Inductive Chain Reasoning Pipeline

> **When to trigger**: When a chain structure is detected where one word/number observation leads to another observation.
> **Core Philosophy**: An unbroken chain of evidence is overwhelmingly stronger than a single piece of evidence.
> **Know-how**: *"A method of continuously digging deeper, biting the tail of the preceding observation"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> TYPE-L is not an independent TYPE but a **meta-methodology that connects other TYPEs**.
> TYPE-A~K are individual verification tools, and TYPE-L is a **connection methodology** that links those tools into a chain
> to ultimately complete a single massive picture of biblical design.

---

## Execution Steps

```
[STEP 0] Selection of Starting Observation
  → Select the simplest and indisputable textual fact as a starting point.
  → Example: "In Gen 1:27, bara (created) is repeated 3 times" — A fact verifiable by anyone using the KJV text.

[STEP 1] Asking the "Why?" Question (Why-Interrogation)
  → Ask, "Why is this structured this way?" regarding the starting observation.
  → If an answer is found → The answer itself becomes the new observation for the next step.
  → If an answer is not found → Collect more patterns from related verses and retry.

[STEP 2] Chain Extension
  → Ask "Why?" again to the new observation.
  → The answer at each step must be supported by the biblical text or linguistic/mathematical data.
  → Construct the chain solely with textual facts, not inferences or theological arguments.

[STEP 3] Chain Visualization
  → Visualize the entire chain by connecting them with arrows (→).
  → | Step | Discovery | Applied TYPE | Biblical Basis |
  → Specify which TYPE was triggered at each step.

[STEP 4] Determination of Terminus
  → The point where the chain naturally stops is the terminus.
  → At the terminus: "Are all steps of this chain possible by human intentional design?"
  → If impossible → Conclude as Divine Design.

[STEP 5] Reverse Validation
  → Move backward from the terminus to the starting point to ensure each step maintains consistency.
  → If any single link in the chain is broken → Reinforce or remove that step.
```

## 📌 Reference Case: Genesis Chapter 1 → 1 John 5:7 Entire Chain of Inductive Reasoning

```
【Starting Point】 In KJV Gen 1:27, bara (created) is repeated exactly 3 times
   ↓ TYPE-I (Frequency Counting)
   ↓ "Why 3 times?"

【Step 1】 Gen 1:26 "us" — Whether this is the plural of majesty or what the plural expression refers to is a point of interpretive division (See `LEXICON_Bible.md` Section 6, do not use the theological system label as a conclusion)
         Verify in the next step whether bara × 3 corresponds to the number of expressions of "God/Spirit/said" appearing in Gen 1:1~3
   ↓ TYPE-F (Internal Textual Repetition/Parallel Structure)
   ↓ "How many times and in what order do these expressions appear in Gen 1:1-3?"

【Step 2】 Gen 1:1 God / Gen 1:2 "Spirit of God" / Gen 1:3 "said" (Cross-check with John 1:1-3)
         → Confirm the textual fact that the three expressions appear together in the creation scene — The theological conclusion supported by this parallel is not fixed at this step but carried over to GATE 5 (Final Determination)
   ↓ TYPE-I + TYPE-G
   ↓ "If bara appears 5 times, how many times does called appear?"

【Step 3】 called = exactly 5 times (Day·Night·Heaven·Earth·Seas)
         bara 5 times = called 5 times → 5:5 perfect balance
         "New Creation" and "Reorganization" operate equally within the 6 days ✅
   ↓ TYPE-I (Symmetrical Design)
   ↓ "If Gen 1:27 is 22 words, is the other verse mentioning the three that bear record in heaven also 22 words?"

【Step 4】 1 John 5:7 (KJV) = exactly 22 words ✅
         22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet = Symbol of the perfect Word
   ↓ TYPE-I (Word Count Symmetry)
   ↓ "If 1 John 5:7 is deleted, is the grammar of verse 8 maintained?"

【Step 5】 Verse 8 masculine participle (οἱ μαρτυροῦντες) modifies neuter nouns
         → Without verse 7: Greek gender mismatch occurs
         → With verse 7: Masculine nouns become antecedents → Grammar is perfect ✅
   ↓ TYPE-G (Proof of Grammatical Chain Break)
   ↓ "Is there any independent source supporting verse 7 textually?"

【Step 6】 North Africa (Cyprian 250 AD) + Hispania (Priscillian 380 AD)
          + 484 AD Council of Carthage Official Confession of Faith = 4 independent sources
   ↓ TYPE-H (Reversal of Independence)

【Terminus】 The structural connection of Gen 1:27 (Triune Creation) ↔ 1 John 5:7 (Triune Witness) is
           confirmed threefold by mathematics (22 words) + grammar (gender agreement) + manuscripts (independent sources)
           → Impossible by coincidental human design → Divine Design confirmed ✅

【Reverse Validation】
  Step 6 → Step 5 → Step 4 → Step 3 → Step 2 → Step 1 → Starting Point
  → All links are maintained based on textual facts ✅
```
