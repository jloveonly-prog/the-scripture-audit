# 🔬 SKILL-09: TYPE-I — Vocabulary Frequency Symmetrical Design Verification Pipeline

> **When to trigger**: When the usage frequency of specific verbs/nouns exactly matches in the same biblical passage or adjacent verses.
> **Core Suspicion**: "Is this symmetry a coincidence? Or is it a theologically intended structural design?"

---

## Execution Steps

```text
[STEP 1] Exhaustive Counting of Core Verbs/Words
  → Select the core verbs to analyze in the target passage.
  → Exhaustively count how many times the word appears in the KJV original text (without omission).
  → | Word | Verses Appeared | Total Count |

[STEP 2] Discover Symmetrical Pairs
  → Check if the frequencies of two words corresponding theologically to each other match perfectly.
  → Ex: "created (bara)" 5 times = "called" 5 times
    → Perfect balance of New Creation and Reorganization.

[STEP 3] Create Semantic Correspondence Table
  → | Count | Word A Context | Semantic Connection Basis | Word B Context |
  → Describe the theological reason why these two words correspond.

[STEP 4] Word Count Cross-Verification
  → Check if the word count of different verses is identical.
  → Hebrew alphabet 22 letters = Symbol of the complete word of God
    → 22-word symmetry is an intended design.
  → Must cross-verify if the same structure holds true in the Greek TR original text as well.

[STEP 5] Argument Excluding Probabilistic Coincidence
  → Present "What is the probability of this symmetry being a coincidence?" to argue for intentional design.
  → Persuade with the probability that word count, symmetrical structure, and theological meaning hold true simultaneously.
```

---

## 📌 Reference Case: Genesis Chapter 1 bara/called Symmetry

```text
[Counting]:
  bara (create, ברא):
    Gen 1:1  — "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." (1 time)
    Gen 1:21 — "And God created great whales..." (1 time)
    Gen 1:27 — "So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him;
                male and female created he them." (3 times)
    Total 5 times ✅

  called (name):
    Gen 1:5  — "called the light Day" / "the darkness he called Night" (2 times)
    Gen 1:8  — "called the firmament Heaven" (1 time)
    Gen 1:10 — "called the dry land Earth" / "the gathering together of the waters called he Seas" (2 times)
    Total 5 times ✅

[Symmetrical Meaning]:
  bara = Creation anew from nothing (heaven/earth, whales, humans) — Axis of Creation
  called = Reorganization and naming from chaos (Day, Night, Heaven, Earth, Seas) — Axis of Reorganization
  → 5:5 Perfect balance = "New Creation" and "Reorganization" operate equally within the 6-day creation ✅

[Word Count Cross-Verification]:
  Genesis 1:27 (KJV Original Text):
  "So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him;
   male and female created he them."
  → Exactly 22 words ✅

  1 John 5:7 (KJV Original Text):
  "For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word,
   and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one."
  → Exactly 22 words ✅

  Hebrew alphabet = 22 letters = Symbol of the complete word of God
  → Trinity creation revelation verse (Gen 1:27) = Trinity testimony verse (1 John 5:7)
  → Identically 22 words: Confirms theological design, not a coincidence ✅
```
