# 🌌 TYPE-AB: Entity & Space Timeline Tracking Matrix

> **When to Invoke**: When doctrinal conflicts are raised regarding spiritual entities like angels, devils, Satan, or post-mortem spaces such as Hell, Hades, the Bottomless Pit, or the Lake of Fire.
> **Core Doubt**: "Is the critic overlapping different spiritual entities or spaces of different dimensions into one, taking advantage of translational confusion?"

---

## Execution Steps

```text
[STEP 1] Precise Lexical Separation
  → By comparing the KJV text with Greek/Hebrew, separate the words that were lumped together in translations into their original independent entities.
  □ Space Separation: Hell/Hades ≠ Tartarus ≠ Bottomless Pit/Abyss ≠ Lake of Fire / Gehenna
  □ Entity Separation: Satan/Devil ≠ Fallen Angels ≠ Devils/Demons

[STEP 2] Compose Entity-Specific Timeline-Space Matrix (Matrix Tracking)
  → Create a tracking table showing which space each separated spiritual entity is located in according to the timeline of [Past - Present - Great Tribulation - Millennial Kingdom - Great White Throne Judgment].

[STEP 3] Dismiss Category Error
  → When the critic attacks, "If A should currently be in B, why is it in C?", prove through the matrix that A and B themselves are different entities or spaces at different times, and dismiss it.
```

## 📌 Reference Case: "Why are the devils on earth?" (Refuting Space Confusion)

```text
[Critic's Attack]:
  "Matthew 25:41 says the Lake of Fire is prepared for 'the devil and his angels'. 2 Peter 2:4 says the angels that sinned were cast into hell (Tartarus). But why are devils wandering around the earth in the Gospels? It is a contradiction in the Bible!"

[Vocabulary and Entity Separation (STEP 1)]:
  The critic lumped together 'Angels that sinned', 'Devils', and 'Satan (Devil)' as a single entity.
  Also, they overlapped the timeline of the current temporary prison 'Tartarus' and the final place of judgment 'Lake of Fire'.

[Matrix Tracking (STEP 2)]:
  1. The 'angels that sinned' who united with human women in Genesis 6 are currently bound in **Tartarus** and are not on earth. (2 Pet 2:4)
  2. Those cast out by Jesus in the Gospels are not angels but disembodied **Devils**, and they are currently active on earth.
  3. The **Lake of Fire** in Matthew 25:41 is not 'the place where they are currently imprisoned', but the 'final destination they will enter after the Great White Throne Judgment'.

[Verdict]: ✅ CONSISTENT
  The critic's argument is a strawman attack stemming from an ontological failure to separate (angels ≠ devils) and a temporal confusion of spaces (current Tartarus ≠ future Lake of Fire). When each entity is arranged according to its timeline, not a single contradiction exists.
```
