# 🏹 SKILL-32: TYPE-AE — Inclusio (Bookend Structure)
**"A structure that opens and closes with the same vocabulary is the author's intentional design"**

> **When it triggers**: When the biblical author closes the **beginning and end** of a paragraph, chapter, or Gospel with the same vocabulary or theme.
> This inclusio structure is not a coincidence, but the **author's intentional literary design**,
> and it binds all the content between the two points connected by that vocabulary into a single narrative unit.
>
> **One-line definition**: *"If the beginning and end are closed with the same vocabulary, it is intentionally designed by the author."*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Difference from TYPE-S (Lexical Chiasm/Connection):**
> - TYPE-S = Lexical repetition/chiasm occurring anywhere in the text
> - TYPE-AE = A structural design where the same vocabulary specifically appears at the **beginning-end** positions
> TYPE-S is a lexical connection / TYPE-AE establishes a literary structure.

## Execution Steps

`
[STEP 1] Bookend Identification
  → Find the same vocabulary in the first verse and the last verse of a paragraph/chapter/Gospel.

[STEP 2] Lexical Verification
  → Verify if it is the same stem/form at the original language (Greek/Hebrew) level.

[STEP 3] Narrative Unit Confirmation
  → Once the inclusio is confirmed, declare that all the content between the vocabulary is a single semantic unit.

[STEP 4] Authorial Intent Declaration
  → "This structure is a literary framework knowingly arranged by the author — it is not a coincidence."

[STEP 5] Argument Application
  → Derive the theological and argumentative implications of the two points connected by the inclusio.
`

## 📌 Reference Case 1: Peter's Calvary Martyrdom Theory — Key Inclusio

**Trigger Verse Pair:**

| Position | Verse | Key Vocabulary |
|:---:|:---|:---:|
| **Start** | John 13:36 *"but you **will follow**(ἀκολουθήσεις) afterward"* | **ἀκολουθ-** |
| **End** | John 21:19 *"**Follow**(ἀκολούθει) me"* | **ἀκολουθ-** |

**Structural Analysis:**

`
John 13:36 ── ἀκολουθήσεις (will follow afterward — prophecy, future)
    │
    │  [Entire narrative of John 13–21]
    │  Gethsemane · Arrest · Denial · Cross · Resurrection · Restoration
    │
John 21:19 ── ἀκολούθει (Follow me — command, present)
`

> **TYPE-AE Verdict:**
> John used ἀκολουθήσεις as a future prophecy in John 13:36,
> and used ἀκολούθει as a present command in John 21:19.
> The same root (ἀκολουθ-) **closes the beginning and end** of Peter's narrative in the Gospel.
> This is an inclusio structure intentionally designed** by the author John.
> A structure where the prophecy of John 13:36 is fulfilled in the command of 21:19 — **The author's intentional connection = Explicit.**
