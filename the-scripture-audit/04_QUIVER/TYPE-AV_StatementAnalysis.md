# 🗡️ SKILL-50: TYPE-AV — Statement Analysis

**"Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh." — Matthew 12:34 KJV**

> **When to trigger**: When an **interrogation, dialogue, or Q&A scene** is recorded in the text, and the speaker answers something.
> **Core Question**: *"What was asked, and what was **leaked voluntarily without being asked**?"*

---

## 1. 🎯 Weapon Overview

In investigative practice, the highest evidentiary value lies in a **volunteered statement**. When unplanned information leaks out under pressure, it reveals the speaker's **actual cognitive map**.

TYPE-AV is a weapon that separates an utterance recorded in the text into **① what was answered upon being asked and ② what was spoken voluntarily without being asked**, securing the information leaked by the speaker in ② as an anchor.

*   **Classification:** Forensic Interrogation
*   **Base Layer:** Rhetoric/Statement Psychology (Based on `01_MANDATE/IDENTITY_Scribe42.md` knowledge)
*   **Target:** Detecting information the speaker intended to conceal, or exposing premises the speaker themselves was unaware of

---

## 2. ⚙️ Trigger Conditions

```
□ An interrogation, Q&A, or dialogue scene is recorded in the text
□ The speaker answers a question or presents a request
□ The volume/content of the answer exceeds the scope of the question
□ The speaker is under pressure/in a crisis (increasing the probability of voluntary disclosure)
```

---

## 3. 🔍 Execution Steps

```
[STEP 1] Determine the Scope of the Question
  → Quote the question actually asked in the text verbatim.
  → Specify the scope of the information demanded by the question in one sentence.

[STEP 2] Total Breakdown of the Answer
  → Divide the speaker's answer into information units.
  → | Information Unit | Did the question demand it? | Verse |

[STEP 3] Extract Over-disclosure
  → Leave only items classified as "not demanded by the question."
  → This is the output of this weapon.

[STEP 4] Reverse-engineer Exposed Premises
  → For the over-disclosure to be valid, what must the speaker know?
  → State that premise as a proposition.

[STEP 5] KJV Cross-verification
  → Cross-verify the exposed premise with verses from other books/authors.
  → If verified, promote to anchor. If not verified, record only as a "solitary statement."

🚨 Do not skip STEP 5 and draw a conclusion in STEP 4.
   The speaker's utterance only confirms the fact that the speaker said so
   (`01_MANDATE/CREED_Override.md` OVERRIDE-1 Prohibition of metaphorical interpretation).
```

## 4. 📌 Reference Case: Interrogation of the Gadarene Legion (Mark 5:1-13 / Luke 8:26-33 / Matt 8:28-34)

```
[STEP 1] Scope of the Question
  Mark 5:9 "And he asked him, What is thy name?"
  → Information demanded: One name

[STEP 2~3] Breakdown of Answer and Extraction of Over-disclosure

  | Information Unit                           | Question Demanded? | Verse    |
  |--------------------------------------------|:------------------:|----------|
  | "My name is Legion"                        | ✅ Yes             | Mark 5:9 |
  | "for we are many"                          | ❌ No              | Mark 5:9 |
  | "torment us before the time"               | ❌ No              | Matt 8:29|
  | "send us not away out of the country"      | ❌ No              | Mark 5:10|
  | "command them not to go out into the deep" | ❌ No              | Luke 8:31|

  → 4 instances of over-disclosure. Asked for a name, but answered with scale instead of entity name,
    and subsequently leaked unasked information consecutively.

[STEP 4] Exposed Premises
  ① They know there is "the time" appointed for them
  ② They premise that "torment" can be executed even before that time
  ③ They know "the deep" is a real place they can go to
  ④ They know geographical movement can be restricted

[STEP 5] KJV Cross-verification
  ① → 2 Pet 2:4 "delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment"
       Jude 1:6 "reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day"
       → Independent verification by 2 authors (Peter, Jude) ✅ Promoted to anchor
  ③ → Rev 20:1-3 "bottomless pit" / Rev 9:1-11
       → Verified by John ✅

  Judgment: Premises ①③ secure cross-witnesses → Anchor. ②④ are recorded as solitary statements only.
```

## 5. ⚠️ Do Not Confuse

| Weapon | What it looks at |
|:---:|:---|
| **TYPE-AV** (This weapon) | **Voluntariness of utterance** — What was spoken without being asked |
| `TYPE-AP` (Speech Act) | The **act** the utterance performs (declaration, promise, command) |
| `TYPE-Y` (Cross-witness) | Differences in **viewpoint among distinct eyewitnesses** |
| `TYPE-AW` (Action-Statement Discrepancy) | The discrepancy between words and **actions** |

> TYPE-AV distinguishes voluntariness/involuntariness **within the spoken content**. Contrasting with action is TYPE-AW.

---

## 6. 🔗 Linked TYPEs

```
TYPE-AV (Statement Analysis)
    ├─ Cross-verify exposed premises → TYPE-Y (Cross-witness)
    ├─ Expand to contrast words and actions → TYPE-AW (Action-Statement Discrepancy)
    ├─ When requests are plural → TYPE-AY (Request Hierarchy Analysis)
    └─ When a statement is a vocabulary misreading → TYPE-S / TYPE-AL
```

---

## 7. 🚧 Prevention of Abuse

```
□ Do not assign "intention" or "psychological state" to the speaker without textual evidence.
  → What this weapon confirms is strictly "what was spoken and what was not asked."
□ Psychology is used only as a Base Layer (pattern recognition) and not as a basis for conclusions.
  → OVERRIDE-1 item 2 (Prohibition of metaphorical interpretation) applies.
□ If there are 0 instances of over-disclosure, record honestly as "no result."
```
