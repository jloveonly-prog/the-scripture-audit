# ⏱️ SKILL-17: TYPE-Q — Biblical Language Quantification Constraint Pipeline
**"One day is with the Lord as a thousand years." — 2 Peter 3:8 KJV**

> **When to Trigger**: When the opponent assigns an **arbitrary specific number** to the Bible's **qualitative/adjectival time language** ("a little", "shortly", "quickly", "nigh").
> **Core Question**: *"If this qualitative language is quantified by the Biblical internal time ratio, does the opponent's assigned number fall within the linguistically permissible range?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse with TYPE-A, TYPE-I:**
> - `TYPE-A` = Reverse calculation by placing **explicit numbers** in series (Number→Number)
> - `TYPE-I` = Proof of design through word repetition counts and structural symmetry
> - `TYPE-Q` = Quantifying **qualitative language** into Biblical time ratios to **reject numbers outside the permissible range** (Language→Numerical range)

## 📌 Reference Case: Rejection of "A little = 250 years"

> **Target Verse**: Rev 20:3 — *"...he must be loosed a little season."* (KJV)
> **Opponent Claim**: "The 'a little season' when Satan is loosed from the bottomless pit is about 250 years."

```
[STEP 1] Extract qualitative language:
  Rev 20:3 KJV: "a little season"
  Greek: ὀλίγον χρόνον (oligon chronon)
  - ὀλίγον (G3641): little, few, short
  - χρόνον (G5550): time, season
  → Meaning: "a short time"

[STEP 2] Collect anchor:
  2 Pet 3:8: "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years"
  → Ratio: 1 day = 1,000 years

[STEP 3] Apply conversion table:
  250 years = 6 hours = 25% (1/4) of a day

[STEP 4] Verify range excess:
  | Qualitative Language | Original | Everyday Permissible | God's Time Conversion | Opponent Claim | Verdict |
  |:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
  | "a little" | ὀλίγον | 5~30 mins | 3.5~20.8 yrs | 250 yrs = 6 hrs | ❌ |

[STEP 5] Everyday language verification:
  Calling 25% of the total reign period (1,000 years) "a little" is
  the same as calling 6 hours out of a 24-hour day "a moment".
  → Entire history from the founding of the US (1776) to present (2026) = 250 years
  → Calling this "a little" is linguistically impossible to establish

[STEP 6] Verdict:
  ❌ Rejected — "A little = 250 years" exceeds the permissible range of Biblical time language by about 12~70 times
  → Nowhere in the Bible is there a calculation formula of "a little = 250 years"
  → An arbitrary assignment forced to fit the timeline to a secular year (1776)
```
