# ⏱️ SKILL-17: TYPE-Q — Biblical Language Quantifier Constraint Pipeline
**"One day is with the Lord as a thousand years." — 2 Peter 3:8 KJV**

> **When to trigger**: When the opponent assigns **arbitrary specific numbers** to the Bible's **qualitative/adjectival temporal language** ("a little season", "shortly", "quickly", "nigh").
> **Core Question**: *"If this qualitative language is quantified by the temporal ratios within the Bible, does the number assigned by the opponent fall within the permissible linguistic range?"*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse with TYPE-A, TYPE-I:**
> - `TYPE-A` = Reverse calculation by arranging **explicit numbers** in series (Number→Number)
> - `TYPE-I` = Proof of design through word repetition counts and structural symmetry
> - `TYPE-Q` = Quantifying **qualitative language** via biblical temporal ratios to **dismiss numbers outside the permissible range** (Language→Numerical range)

## 📌 Reference Case: Dismissal of "A little season = 250 years"

> **Target Verse**: Rev 20:3 — *"...he must be loosed a little season."* (KJV)
> **Opponent's Claim**: "The 'little season' when Satan is loosed from the bottomless pit is about 250 years."

```
[STEP 1] Extract qualitative language:
  Rev 20:3 KJV: "a little season"
  Greek: ὀλίγον χρόνον (oligon chronon)
  - ὀλίγον (G3641): little, small, short
  - χρόνον (G5550): time, season
  → Meaning: "short time"

[STEP 2] Collect anchor:
  2 Peter 3:8: "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years"
  → Ratio: 1 day = 1,000 years

[STEP 3] Apply conversion table:
  250 years = 6 hours = 25% (1/4) of a day

[STEP 4] Check for range exceedance:
  | Qualitative Language | Original | Everyday Permissible | God's Time Conversion | Opponent's Claim | Verdict |
  |:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
  | "a little" | ὀλίγον | 5~30 mins | 3.5~20.8 years | 250 years = 6 hours | ❌ |

[STEP 5] Everyday language verification:
  Calling 25% of the total reigning period (1,000 years) "a little" is like calling 6 hours out of a 24-hour day "just a moment."
  → The entire history from the founding of the USA (1776) to the present (2026) = 250 years.
  → Calling this "a little season" is linguistically impossible.

[STEP 6] Verdict:
  ❌ Dismissed — "A little season = 250 years" exceeds the permissible range of biblical temporal language by about 12 to 70 times.
  → There is no calculation of "a little season = 250 years" anywhere in the Bible.
  → It is an arbitrary assignment forced to match timelines with secular years (1776).
```
