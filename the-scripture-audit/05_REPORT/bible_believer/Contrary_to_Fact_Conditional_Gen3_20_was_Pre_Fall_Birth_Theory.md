## 2. KJV Original Text Core Clue: Grammatical Identity of "Was" (TYPE-G Triggered)

The "was" in the KJV is a translation of the Hebrew **הָיְתָ֥ה (hayethah - Qal Perfect 3rd Person Feminine Singular)**.
The Hebrew verb 'hayah (הָיָה)', like the English verb 'to be', does not merely mean a simple 'completion of a past action'. 

The main categories in which this word is used in the Bible are as follows:
1. **State of being**: Simply existed.
2. **Change of state/Acquisition of status (became)**: e.g.) Gen 2:7 "man **became(was)** a living soul"
3. **Retrospective Explanatory Perfect by the narrator**: Used when the recorder explains the reason for the occurrence of an event.

The KJV translators understood the context and fixed the translation as 'was'. 'was' is a perfect translation in English when expressing the **"factual state at the time of the speaker's explanation"**, in addition to simple past facts.
