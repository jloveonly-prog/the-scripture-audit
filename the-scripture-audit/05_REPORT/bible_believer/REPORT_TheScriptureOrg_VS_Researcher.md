### 🔧 TYPE-G (KJV Grammar Dissection) — Genesis 6:3 Original Language Dissection

> **Activation Reason:** Need for Hebrew structural analysis of the subject, object, and reason clause in the judgment sentence of Gen 6:3

#### 📖 Genesis 6:3 KJV Original Text Breakdown

> *"And the LORD said, My spirit shall not always strive with **man**, for that **he also** is **flesh**: yet **his days** shall be an hundred and twenty years."*

| KJV Word | Hebrew Original | Grammatical Analysis |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **man** | **אָדָם (ha-adam)** | Target of the judgment sentence = Adam (Mankind). Not angels. |
| **he also** | **בְּשַׁגָּם הוּא (beshaggam hu)** | `be-` (because) + `she-` (that) + `gam` (also) + `hu` (he). Subject of the reason clause = ha-adam |
| **flesh** | **בָּשָׂר (basar)** | Perishable mortal flesh. The exact opposite category of ruach (spirit) in Isa 31:3. |
| **his days** | **יָמָיו (yamav)** | Biological lifespan. Same root as the genealogy formula in Genesis 5. |

#### 🔍 TYPE-G Verification Results

**Analyzing the entire judgment sentence (Gen 6:3), there is not a single mention of angels:**

| Judgment Element | Original Subject/Object | Expected Content in Angel Theory | Actual Original Text |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---:|
| God's wrestling opponent | `ha-adam` | Angels (Criminals) | ❌ ha-adam |
| Reason clause (Why judge?) | `he also is flesh` | Attributes/crimes of angels | ❌ Attributes of mankind (basar) |
| Target of lifespan/period sentence | `his days` | Angels or Angels + Mankind | ❌ days of ha-adam |

> **TYPE-G Conclusion:** The judgment sentence of Gen 6:3 deals exclusively with **ha-adam (mankind)** from beginning to end. If angels were the ringleaders of the crime, the complete absence of angels in God's judgment sentence is a **Structural Void**.

It is true that the words are separated. However, that separation is not **"angels vs. humans" but "the dead vs. the living"**:

| Word | Original | KJV Usage | Actual Meaning |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|

```
v19: spirits in prison = those who died and are in prison in a disembodied state (spirits)
v20: eight souls       = those who were saved alive in the ark with physical bodies
```
