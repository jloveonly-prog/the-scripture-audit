## 1. Reconstruction of the Issue (The Challenge)
KJV Romans 7:6 is translated as "that being dead (it, that is, the law being dead)". The Majority Text supports "we being dead (ἀποθανόντες)".
In the previous verification, the expression "the law is dead" was seen as conflicting with the eternity of the law, "one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law" (Matthew 5:18), and thus the KJV was judged as an error (UNBIBLICAL). However, reverse-verification revealed that this was a **fatal semantic ambiguity error (TYPE-AL) confusing "Death" and "Annihilation/Disappearance"**.

## 2. BVCAP 2.0 Precision Re-verification (Acceptance of Reverse-verification)

In the Bible, "Death" never means 'annihilation of existence (disappearance)'. The biblical definition of death is **'Separation' or 'severance of relationship/authority'**.
- Physical death = Separation of soul and body (James 2:26)
- Spiritual death = Separation of God and humans (Ephesians 2:1)
- Therefore, "The law being dead" does not mean God's holy law evaporated from the universe and 'disappeared', but means **"the governing authority of the law that condemned and bound us has been severed (separated)"**.

### 💥 2nd Precision Strike: Dismissal of TYPE-AT Conflict and Perfect Resurrection of TYPE-AU
Applying the biblical definition of "death" (severance of authority), the KJV's translation "the law being dead" does not conflict at all with God's attributes (eternity of the law). Rather, it perfectly completes the husband and wife analogy in Romans 7:1-3 to a spine-chilling degree.
- **Analogy in verses 1-3**: If the husband dies, the wife is freed not because the husband disappears from the world, but because his 'authority as a husband' is dead.
- **Application in verse 6**: Since the 'condemning authority that bound us' of our first husband, the law, is dead (that being dead), we are freed from the law and can be joined to a new husband (Christ).
- If translated as "we are dead" like the Majority Text, it would mean the wife is still dead, completely destroying the structural isomorphism (TYPE-AU) of the husband and wife analogy.

### 🎯 3rd Precision Strike: Harmony with "ye also are become dead" in Verse 4
The fact that "the condemning authority of the law is dead (verse 6)" and the fact that "we are dead (verse 4)" are two sides of the same coin occurring at the cross.
Through the substitutionary atonement of Jesus Christ on the cross, the law satisfied all demands of condemnation against us, so its authority became dead (that being dead), and we also were crucified and died with Christ, thus becoming dead to the law. Paul completes perfect liberation by crossing the declaration of our death in verse 4 and the death of the law (condemning authority) in verse 6. Beza and the KJV translators accurately traced back and translated the original counterpart of the analogy, "the death of the law," in verse 6.
