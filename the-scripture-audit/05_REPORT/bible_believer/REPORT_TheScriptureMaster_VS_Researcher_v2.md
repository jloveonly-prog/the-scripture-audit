* **(Job 1:6)** Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan came also among them.
* **(Job 38:7)** When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?
  * When God created the world and laid the foundations of the earth (38:4), the sons of God shouted for joy. Since this was before humanity was created, the sons of God mentioned here refer to angels who existed before creation.

  * Daughters of men = Human women
  * Nephilim = The giant race born from this union

Here, a question might arise: 'How can a spiritual being put on human flesh?'

#### - Angels are inherently spiritual beings, but they can appear in human form.
* **(Heb 13:2)** "Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares."

Furthermore, in **(Gen 18–19)**, angels ate a meal with Abraham (Gen 18:8), and the men of Sodom considered them ordinary men and tried to commit sexual crimes against them (Gen 19:1–5). Through this, we consider it possible for angels to appear in the form of humans with actual physical bodies. (Of course, the angels in Genesis 18 include the incarnate God.)

#### - Fallen angels left their original habitation and destroyed the order established by God.
* **(Jude 1:6-7)** "And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day. Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire."
  * In the text, leaving their "own habitation" means that they crossed the boundaries of existence permitted by God and infiltrated the human realm.
  * Jude 1:7 mentions the "strange flesh" of Sodom and Gomorrah, explaining that the sin committed by the angels was closely related to sexual crimes.

#### - The Nephilim are the result of this abnormal union.
* **(Gen 6:4)** "There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown."
  * Nephilim refer to the abnormal beings born between fallen angels and human women. Because humanity became so corrupt and God's order of creation was severely destroyed, God had no choice but to judge the world through Noah's flood.
  * The idea that angels do not have reproductive capabilities might be our stereotype. Although we don't know the exact method, they obtained blood and became capable of reproducing.
  * The genetic elements of the Nephilim continued after the flood through Noah's family. This is precisely why Noah cursed his grandson Canaan instead of his son Ham.
* **(Gen 9:25)** And he said, Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren.
  * During the war when the Israelites conquered Canaan, groups of giants continued to appear. God mobilized angels to utterly destroy those giants.

#### - The connection between (2 Pet 2:4–5) and (Jude 1:6-7)
* "For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment; And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;"
  * The Apostle Peter naturally connects the story of the sinning angels with the judgment of Noah's time.
