## ⚖️ 4. 5 Reasons Why TSM's Claim is Stronger Than IFB's

### ❶ Linguistic Division of the Same Author — Decisive Blow

| Verse | Language | Target | Meaning |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|

### ❷-A. Comparison of 2 Pet 3:6-7 — "The world that then was" vs "The heavens and the earth, which are now" ★ Decisive Blow

> The fact that these two verses sit side-by-side completely dismantles IFB's Noah's flood interpretation.

| Verse | KJV Text | Reference World | Core Point |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **2 Pet 3:6** | *"the world **that then was**, being overflowed with water, **perished**"* | **First World** | Completely perished — the world itself disappeared |
| **2 Pet 3:7** | *"**the heavens and the earth, which are now**... reserved unto fire"* | **Second World** | Still present even after Noah's flood — not yet perished |

```text
2 Pet 3:6 vs 3:7 — Peter himself distinguishes the two worlds
┌───────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┐
│   2 Pet 3:6 — World that then was │   2 Pet 3:7 — Heavens and earth now  │
│   "the world that THEN WAS"       │   "the heavens and earth WHICH ARE NOW"│
├───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│   = First world (Gen 1:1)         │   = Second world (Post Gen 1:3)      │
│   Completely PERISHED by water    │   Did not perish in Noah's flood     │
│   Both heaven + earth covered     │   Heavens were not submerged         │
│   Gen 1:2 "face of the waters"    │   World that is "ARE NOW" today      │
│   World itself disappeared        │   Reserved for fire judgment         │
└───────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘

Core Logic (TYPE-AL):
  "The world that then was" = PERISHED (disappeared)
  "The world which is now" = Still ARE NOW even after Noah's flood (exists)
  → "The world which is now" did not perish during Noah's flood
  → The "world that then was" which perished ≠ Noah's flood
  → "The world that then was" = The first world of Gen 1:1 ~ 1:2
```

**Decisive Differences Between Noah's Flood and the First World's Water Judgment:**

| Item | First World Water Judgment (Gen 1:2) | Noah's Flood (Gen 6-8) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Fate of the world | **perished** — world was destroyed | World was not destroyed (still exists) |
| State of heavens | Both heaven+earth covered (Gen 1:2) | Heavens not submerged — only earth flooded |
| World continuation | Disappeared → Recreation needed (Gen 1:3) | Water receded and ground appeared (Gen 8:13) |
| Peter's expression | "The world that then was" (Past) | Event of "heavens and earth, which are now" (Present) |
| God's attitude | "spared NOT" | "longsuffering" |
| KJV Verses | 2 Pet 3:6, 2 Pet 2:4, Jude 1:6 | 2 Pet 2:5, 1 Pet 3:20 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Fatal Flaw of IFB:** If you interpret 2 Pet 3:6 "the world that then was perished by water" as Noah's flood, then 2 Pet 3:7 "the heavens and the earth, which are now" still exists after Noah's flood — **which means the same author commits self-contradiction just one verse apart**. IFB cannot explain this contradiction.
>
> **TSM's Solution:** 2 Pet 3:6 = First world water judgment of Gen 1:2 / 2 Pet 3:7 = Current world after recreation → An accurate distinction between the two worlds is contained within a single verse.
