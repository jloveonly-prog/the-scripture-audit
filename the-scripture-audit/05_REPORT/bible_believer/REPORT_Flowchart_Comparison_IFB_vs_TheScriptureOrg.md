## ⚖️ 4. 5 Reasons Why TSB's Argument is Stronger than IFB's

### ❶ Division in the Same Author's Language — The Decisive Blow

| Verse | Language | Target | Meaning |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| 2 Pet 2:4 | *"spared not"* | **Angels** | Immediate, decisive judgment — Executed without room for patience |
| 1 Pet 3:20 | *"longsuffering"* | **Noah's Days** | Judgment after enduring and waiting for a long time |

> It is impossible for Peter to record **opposite attitudes of God** toward the same target (Gen 6 angels + Noah). Such a description is only possible because the two verses point to **different events in different eras**.

Judgments presented by the IFB camp:

### ❷-A. Comparison of 2 Pet 3:6-7 — "The world that then was" vs "The heavens and the earth, which are now" ★ Decisive Blow

> The fact that these two verses sit side-by-side completely destroys IFB's Noah's Flood interpretation.

| Verse | KJV Original Text | Target World | Core Point |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **2 Pet 3:6** | *"the world **that then was**, being overflowed with water, **perished**"* | **First World** | Total destruction (perished) — The world itself ceased to exist |
| **2 Pet 3:7** | *"**the heavens and the earth, which are now**... reserved unto fire"* | **Second World** | Still exists after Noah's Flood — Not yet perished |

```text
2 Pet 3:6 vs 3:7 — Peter himself distinguishes the two worlds
┌───────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┐
│   2 Pet 3:6 — The world that then was │   2 Pet 3:7 — The heavens and earth which are now│
│   "the world that THEN WAS"        │   "the heavens and earth WHICH ARE NOW"│
├───────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│   = First World (Gen 1:1)          │   = Second World (Post-Gen 1:3 Recreation)│
│   Completely PERISHED by water     │   Did not perish even in Noah's Flood│
│   Both heaven + earth covered in water│   Heavens were not submerged (Flood only on earth)│
│   Gen 1:2 "face of the waters"     │   The world that is "ARE NOW" even today│
│   The world itself disappeared (perished)│   "Reserved" for fire judgment         │
└───────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘

Core Logic (TYPE-AL):
  "The world that then was" = PERISHED (Disappeared)
  "The world which is now" = Still ARE NOW (Exists) even after Noah's Flood
  → During Noah's Flood, the "world which is now" did not perish
  → The "world that then was" that perished ≠ Noah's Flood
  → "The world that then was" = The first world of Gen 1:1 ~ 1:2
```

**Crucial Differences Between Noah's Flood and the First World Water Judgment:**

| Item | First World Water Judgment (Gen 1:2) | Noah's Flood (Gen 6-8) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Fate of the World | **perished** — The world was destroyed | The world was not destroyed (Still exists today) |
| State of the Heavens | Both heaven + earth covered in water (Gen 1:2) | Heavens were not submerged — Flood only on earth |
| Continuation of World | Disappeared → Needed recreation (Gen 1:3) | Water receded, dry land appeared (Gen 8:13) |
| Peter's Expression | "the world that then was" (Past) | Event of "the heavens and the earth, which are now" (Present) |
| God's Attitude | "spared NOT" — Did not spare | "longsuffering" — Endured with patience |
| KJV Verses | 2 Pet 3:6, 2 Pet 2:4, Jude 1:6 | 2 Pet 2:5, 1 Pet 3:20 |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **IFB's Fatal Flaw:** If 2 Pet 3:6 "the world that then was... perished" is interpreted as Noah's Flood, then 2 Pet 3:7 "the heavens and the earth, which are now" still exist and did not perish after Noah's Flood — **causing the same author to contradict himself just one verse apart**. IFB cannot explain this contradiction.
>
> **TSB's Solution:** 2 Pet 3:6 = First world water judgment of Gen 1:2 / 2 Pet 3:7 = Current world after recreation → An accurate distinction between the two worlds is contained within consecutive verses.
