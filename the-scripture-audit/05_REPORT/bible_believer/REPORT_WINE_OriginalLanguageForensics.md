Linguistics calls this phenomenon **metonymy**. The name of the entire beverage does not follow the ingredient with the most volume, but the name of the **core ingredient that determines its identity, taste, and function**.

Modern Greeks no longer mix their wine with water, but the name "mixture" remained and became the standard word for "wine".
