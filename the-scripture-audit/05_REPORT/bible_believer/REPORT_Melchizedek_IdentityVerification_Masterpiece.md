    The verb expression in Genesis 14:18 completely defeats the Catholic claim of "Sacrifice".

    *   💥 **Dismissal Verdict**: The direction of Melchizedek's action was not **'Offered up'** toward God, but **'Brought forth'** toward Abraham's exhausted men. Also, there is not a single ritualistic word like altar (Mizbeach) or sacrifice (Zebach) in the text.

    *   **Judges 8:5 (Gideon's army's request for survival)**:

> "of whom **it is witnessed that he liveth**."

> 💥 **Clash**: To express that the Son of God, who exists by Himself and is the source of life, **"is witnessed (certified)"** by someone that He lives is a severe ontological downgrade (lowering Jesus to the level of a creature).

> 🦁 **Everyday example for understanding (President's survival confirmation vs Rare animal's survival certificate)**
> * **In the case of a president**: When the president, the highest authority in the nation, enters his office, he does not need to be issued a 'survival guarantee' by a subordinate or guard saying, **"I guarantee that this person is the president and is currently a living person."** Because the president's existence and authority are self-proving. > * **Conclusion**: Because Melchizedek is a unique creaturely being, a son of God of the first world, the Bible guarantees (witnesses) from a third party's perspective that **"this being is really alive even now (unlike the Aaronic priests who died)."** If Melchizedek is Jesus (the Creator), it falls into the bizarre contradiction (ontological downgrade) that God, the King of the entire universe, must be issued a 'survival certificate' stating "it is confirmed that He is alive" from a creature or a document.
