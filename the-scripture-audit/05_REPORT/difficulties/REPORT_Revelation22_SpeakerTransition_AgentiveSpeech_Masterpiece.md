# 📑 [BVCAP Forensic Report] Speaker Shift and In-Depth Analysis of 'Agentive Speech' in Revelation 22

> **STATUS**: 🟢 **IRONCLAD (Fully Verified)**
> **Document Purpose**: To identify the cause of the 'Speaker Shift' phenomenon (the mixing of Jesus' and the angel's voices) that occurs transitioning from the latter half of Revelation 21 to chapter 22, and to provide an accurate speaker timeline for video dubbing and Bible readers.
> **Applied Weapons**: [TYPE-P] Speech Act Theory, [TYPE-AA] Ancient Law of Agency (Shaliach), [TYPE-AS] Hierarchy of Power Analysis

---

## 1. 🚨 The Origin of the Incident: Structural Difficulty of the Text

When reading or directing a video of Revelation 22, readers fall into a serious dilemma. **"Should this line be read with the voice of Jesus, or the voice of an angel?"**
It sounds like the angel is speaking but the content is about Jesus, and it sounds like Jesus is speaking but suddenly the angel refuses John's worship. The identities of the two beings intersect abruptly within the text without quotation marks.

Due to this, some cults (e.g., Jehovah's Witnesses) fall into the doctrinal misinterpretation (C-03 Category Confusion) that "Jesus and the angel (Michael) are the same being."

---

## 2. ⚖️ BVCAP Core Analysis Mechanism

The master key to deciphering this phenomenon is the **'Divine Messenger Formula'**, a legal and literary custom of the ancient Near East and Jewish society.

### 🔑 TYPE-AA: Shaliach Principle - The Law of Agency
In ancient Jewish law, there was a firm principle: **"The one who is sent (agent) is equal to the one who sent him (master)."**
A messenger bringing the king's edict would sometimes speak in the third person before the people, saying, "The king says...", but at the climax, he would unroll the scroll and **proclaim directly in the king's first person ("I"), as if possessed by the king.**

Because the angel in Revelation 22 is reading the royal edict in the capacity of Jesus Christ's 'plenipotentiary (Shaliach)', he is proclaiming it by directly borrowing Jesus' first-person perspective ("Behold, I come quickly").

## 3. 🎬 Speaker Timeline Breakdown of Revelation 22

We will break down how the voice of the **[Angel]**, who was guiding through the New Jerusalem from 21:9 to 22:5, fluctuates starting from 22:6, along with the true speaker of each verse and points for directing (reading).

### 📍 [SCENE 1] The Angel's Third-Person Testimony
*   **Rev 22:6** "And he (the angel) said unto me, These sayings are faithful and true: and the Lord God of the holy prophets sent **his angel** to shew unto his servants the things which must shortly be done."
    *   **Speaker**: The Angel (The Messenger Himself)
    *   **Commentary**: The angel objectifies himself in the third person ("his angel") and declares that it is God's message. This is the tone of an ordinary, authoritative guide.

### 📍 [SCENE 2] Activation of Agentive Speech (Reading the Royal Edict)
*   **Rev 22:7** "**Behold, I come quickly**: blessed is he that keepeth the sayings of the prophecy of this book."
    *   **Speaker**: The Angel (But reading by borrowing **Jesus' first person**)
    *   **Commentary**: There are no quotation marks, but this is the 'royal edict of Jesus' uttered by the angel. When reading, it should be the angel's voice, but the tone must transition to something grand and overwhelming, as if reading the edict of the King of Kings.

### 📍 [SCENE 3] Apostle John's Illusion and the Angel's Restraint
*   **Rev 22:8-9** "And I John saw these things, and heard them. And when I had heard and seen, **I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel** which shewed me these things. **Then saith he unto me, See thou do it not: for I am thy fellowservant... worship God.**"
    *   **Speaker**: Apostle John (v. 8) → The Angel (v. 9)
    *   **Commentary**: Because the angel proclaimed perfectly in Jesus' first person in verse 7 ("I come quickly"), John, overwhelmed by the glory, mistakes the messenger (angel) for the King (Jesus) and bows down. The angel urgently draws the line of identity, saying, "I am merely a creature (fellowservant)!" **(Proving the Angel ≠ Jesus)**

### 📍 [SCENE 4] Continuation of the Agent's Declaration
*   **Rev 22:10-11** "And he saith unto me, Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book... He that is unjust, let him be unjust still..."
    *   **Speaker**: The Angel (Maintaining the capacity as God's agent)

### 📍 [SCENE 5] Direct Intervention of the King of Kings (Direct Voice)
*   **Rev 22:12-15** "**And, behold, I come quickly... I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last.**"
    *   **Speaker**: Jesus Christ
    *   **Commentary**: Going beyond the level of the angel's proxy reading, the scene now shifts to the complete Direct Manifestation of Jesus Christ Himself. It is a voice filled with the absolute authority of the Creator.

### 📍 [SCENE 6] Final Traffic Control (Perfect Separation of Messenger and Sender)
*   **Rev 22:16** "**I Jesus** have sent **mine angel** to testify unto you these things in the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star."
    *   **Speaker**: Jesus Christ Himself
    *   **Commentary**: This is the most crucial conclusion. In the grand finale, Jesus perfectly separates **"the one who sent (Jesus)"** and **"the one who is sent (angel)"** within a single sentence. With this, the speaker mixing phenomenon that confused John and the readers throughout chapter 22 is completely resolved, and the Creator deity of Christ is radiantly proclaimed.

## 4. 💡 Conclusion and Application (Verdict)

1.  **Dismissal of Same Identity**: The angel of Revelation is not Jesus. The angel is strictly a creature (fellowservant) and cannot be an object of worship. (Rev 22:9)
2.  **Principle of Speaker Shift**: The reason the voices of the angel and Jesus seem to be mixed without distinction in the text is due to the use of the ancient Jewish literary device, **'First-Person Agentive Speech (Shaliach)'**. Because the angel perfectly read Jesus' royal edict in the first person, even John was mistaken.
3.  **Video Directing Guide**: When directing or reading Revelation 22, subtly distinguishing between the angel's proxy reading in verse 7 (the tone of a herald's proclamation) and Jesus' direct appearance in verse 16 (the tone of the King of Kings) to bring out the three-dimensionality is the best way to revive the dynamic nature of the original biblical text.
