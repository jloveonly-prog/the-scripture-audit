## 1. Establishing the Problem — The Reality of the Number of Manuscripts and its Limitations

### 1-1. The 3-Way Structure of Manuscript Families — Overall Status

The New Testament manuscript tradition must be understood as a **3-way structure** of 2 Greek families + 1 Latin family.

| Family | Language | Representative Manuscripts | Includes Comma | Current Usage |

### 1-6. Why did Catholics Pressure Erasmus?

Erasmus (1469-1536) was a Catholic theologian and humanist. When he published his Greek New Testament in 1516 and excluded the Comma, the Catholic side immediately started pressuring him for the following three reasons:

1. **Threat of the Collapse of the Vulgate's Authority**  
   The Latin Vulgate, the official Bible of Catholicism, had the Comma. The fact that it was absent from Erasmus's Greek text meant, *"There is an error in the Vulgate."* This was a direct challenge to church authority.

2. **Disappearance of the Proof Text for the Trinity**  
   The Comma ("the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one") was the core verse with which Catholics defended the doctrine of the Trinity. When it was left out, critics attacked Erasmus on suspicion of being an **Arian (heresy denying the Trinity)**.

3. **Spark of the Reformation (1517)**  
   It was exactly when Luther's Reformation had just begun. They feared that Erasmus's Greek text would become a weapon for the reformers opposing Catholicism.

**Core Irony — Erasmus and GA 61:**
```
Erasmus 1516: Published 1st Edition — Excluded because Comma absent in Greek manuscripts
Erasmus 1519: Published 2nd Edition — Still excluded Comma → Strong backlash from Catholic side
Catholic Side: Presented GA 61 (Codex Britannicus) (Production suspicions exist)
Erasmus 1522: 3rd Edition — Inserted Comma (Though he expressed doubts about the manuscript's reliability)
Result:       Pressure to protect the Catholic Latin Vulgate → led to TR → led to KJV
```
> ⚠️ **Academic Correction:** There is a widely circulated anecdote that Erasmus traditionally made a promise saying, "I will insert it if there is a manuscript," but according to H.J. de Jonge's research (1980), **documentary evidence of this promise does not exist anywhere in Erasmus's writings.** Bruce Metzger also withdrew this "rash promise" anecdote in later editions. It is the currently corrected view of academia that Erasmus inserted it into the 3rd edition due to Catholic pressure and the issue of consistency with the Vulgate.
> That is, **Catholics pressured Erasmus to protect their own Latin Vulgate**,  
> and as a result, the Comma was included in the KJV via the TR in a paradoxical history.  
> Calling this verse a "Catholic insertion" misunderstands historical causality.

### 1-7. Recognizing the Limitations of the Number of Manuscripts Debate

The formula "Number of manuscripts = Closeness to original" **does not hold true.** The reasons are as follows:

- Having many manuscripts means **that text was copied a lot**, not that it is **close to the original**.
- The reason Alexandrian manuscripts are few is because the papyrus physically survived thanks to Egypt's dry climate, not because the Alexandrian tradition was a minority.

> **⚠️ Strategic Conclusion:**  
> Greek manuscripts containing the Comma **do exist** (GA 629, GA 61, GA 918, etc.).  
> However, their numbers are small and there are reliability debates surrounding each manuscript.  
> Therefore, the front line of apologetics must **move from a "fight over the number of manuscripts" to "internal textual evidence".**  
> **Manuscripts are supporting evidence; the internal textual structure is the primary evidence.**

### 1-9-A. Old Latin Versions (Vetus Latina) — Latin Bibles before the Vulgate

**Old Latin Versions (Vetus Latina, "Old Latin Bible")** is a collective term for the Latin Bible translations used in North Africa and Western churches in the 2nd~4th centuries, before Jerome's Vulgate came out.  
It is not a single translation, but consists of **multiple early Latin translations that varied by region.**

> ⚠️ **Important Distinction:** To say that the "Old Latin tradition" existed from the 2nd century means  
> **translation activities began in the 2nd century.**  
> **Existing physical manuscripts** containing the Comma exist only from the **5th century onwards.**  
> Evidence for the Comma from the 2nd~3rd centuries is confirmed only through **citations within Patristic writings** (Tertullian, Cyprian).

```
Greek Original (1st C)
    ↓
Old Latin Translation Begins (2nd C~) ← Start of independent early Latin translation tradition
    ↓
Patristic Citation Evidence: Tertullian ~200 AD / Cyprian ~250 AD ← Citation in theological works, not manuscripts
    ↓
Oldest Existing Latin Ms with Comma: 5th C~ (Speculum, etc.)
    ↓
Jerome's Vulgate (382~405 AD) ← Revised and unified Old Latin
    ↓
Medieval Vulgate Spread → Officialization at Council of Trent (1546)
```

**Latin Original Core Verse:**
```latin

 tres efficit cohaerentes, alterum ex altero.
 Qui tres unum sunt, non unus."
```
*(Translation: Thus the connection of the Father in the Son, and of the Son in the Paraclete,  
makes three coherent ones, from one another, **which three are one [essence]**(unum), not one [Person](unus).)*

**English Translation (ANF Standard):**
> *"Thus the connection of the Father in the Son, and of the Son in the Paraclete,  
> produces three coherent Persons, who are yet distinct One from Another.  
> **These Three are one [essence], not one [Person]**, as it is said,  
> 'I and my Father are One,' in respect of unity of substance not singularity of number."*

A wealthy and reputed intellectual, he was such an outstanding figure that he was elected Bishop of Carthage just 2 years after converting to Christianity in his mid-40s.

The 10 years he served as bishop (248~258 AD) precisely overlapped with a **period of extreme persecution in the Roman Empire**:

```
249~251 AD  Persecution of Emperor Decius
            → Systematic oppression of Christianity, massive emergence of the Lapsed (Lapsi)
            → Cyprian fled and guided the church via letters

251 AD      Novatian Schism
            → Church division crisis over how to handle the Lapsed
            → Cyprian responded by writing "On the Unity of the Church (De Unitate)"

257~258 AD  Persecution of Emperor Valerian
            → Order for death penalty of clergy

Sep 258 AD  Cyprian sentenced to death at public trial
            "Thanks be to God (Deo gratias)." — His first words upon hearing the verdict
            → Martyred by beheading
```

> **Core:** Cyprian's quote appears in an **official document written by a martyr-bishop** appealing for church unity while protecting the church amidst persecution.  
> It is not a personal theological opinion, but evidence of an **official Latin biblical text** shared by the entire North African church at the time.

#### 📜 Direct Testimony regarding the Comma (*De Ecclesiae Catholicae Unitate*, Ch.6, ~250 AD)

**Latin Original:**
```
"Dicit Dominus: 'Ego et Pater unum sumus';
 et iterum de Patre et Filio et Spiritu sancto scriptum est:
 'et hi tres unum sunt.'"
```

**Translation:**
> *"The Lord says: 'I and the Father are one' (John 10:30);*  
> *and again concerning the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit it is **written (scriptum est)**:*  
> ***'and these three are one.'***"

**Background of Writing:**  
This text was written by Cyprian amidst the crisis of church division after the Decian persecution, **explaining the Trinity as a basis for church unity.**  
He quoted this verse when drawing the oneness of the Trinity from the Bible.

## 2. Core Evidence 1 — The Disappearance of "In Earth"

> **"If verse 7 is an insertion, why must verse 8 be modified?"**  
> This is a structural proof confirming the original design of the text.

### 2-1. Grammatical Contrast: In Heaven vs In Earth

**【 KJV Structure (Original Text containing Comma) 】**
> Verse 7: "For there are three that bear record **in heaven (ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ)**, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one."  
> Verse 8: "And there are three that bear witness **in earth (ἐν τῇ γῇ)**, the Spirit, and the water, and the blood: and these three agree in one."

**【 NA28 Structure (Alexandrian text without Comma) 】**
> "ὅτι τρεῖς εἰσιν οἱ μαρτυροῦντες," (For there are three that testify:)  
> "τὸ πνεῦμα καὶ τὸ ὕδωρ καὶ τὸ αἷμα, καὶ οἱ τρεῖς εἰς τὸ ἕν εἰσιν." (the Spirit, the water and the blood; and the three are in agreement.)

### 2-2. The Chain Editing Phenomenon of Deletion

> **When Alexandrian copyists deleted verse 7 (Comma), they also had to remove "ἐν τῇ γῇ (in earth)" from verse 8.**

The logical implications of this fact:

1. **Why was "in earth" of verse 8 deleted together?**  
   The place modifier "in earth" **only makes complete sense when contrasted with "in heaven" (verse 7).**  
   If only "in earth" is left after deleting verse 7, readers will naturally ask: *"What about in heaven?"*  
   To prevent this question, "in earth" was also removed.

2. **What this means:**  
   This is evidence that the Alexandrian copyists themselves recognized that "ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ (in heaven)" and "ἐν τῇ γῇ (in earth)" were **structurally designed as a pair from the original text**.  
   They recognized the structural rupture caused by the deletion of verse 7 and tweaked verse 8 to patch it up.

3. **Conclusion:**  
   Because the "heaven/earth" contrast structure of verses 7 and 8 is original, a chain editing occurred where removing one necessitated removing the other. **This chain editing itself proves the existence of the original structure.**

### 2-3. Structure Visualization

```
【 TR Original Structure (Internally Complete) 】
─────────────────────────────────────────────
 v.7  ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ  (in heaven)  → 3 Witnesses in Heaven: Father, Word, Holy Ghost
      ↕ Contrast
 v.8  ἐν τῇ γῇ      (in earth)   → 3 Witnesses in Earth: Spirit, water, blood
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Heaven and earth, above and below, form a perfect rhetorical symmetry.

【 Structure after Alexandrian Editing (Collapse of Contrasting Pair) 】
─────────────────────────────────────────────
 v.7  (Deleted)
 v.8  (ἐν τῇ γῇ also deleted)  → 3 Witnesses: Spirit, water, blood
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Because "in earth" disappeared, the place contrast itself vanished. Verse 8 alone
  does not even raise the question of "Why specifically limit the place?"
  Alexandria knew this question would arise and deleted "in earth."
```

## 4. Core Evidence 3 — Historical Apologetic Function and the Cerinthian Heresy

The context of 1 John 5:6:

> "This is he that came **by water and blood**, even Jesus Christ; **not by water only, but by water and blood**." (1 John 5:6)

### 4-1. Historical and Exegetical Meaning of 'Water' and 'Blood'

The overwhelming consensus of early Church Fathers (Tertullian, Irenaeus, Augustine) and modern exegesis:

| Evidence | Historical Meaning | Event Pointed To |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Water** | **Start** of Jesus' public ministry | **Baptism at Jordan** — Descent of Spirit, Voice of Father |
| **Blood** | **Completion** of Jesus' public ministry | **Death on the Cross** — Atonement, Redemption |
| **Spirit** | Confirms the past two events in us now | **Indwelling Spirit** — Continuous testimony |

### 4-2. Direct Connection to Cerinthian Heresy

**"not by water only, but by water and blood"** in 1 John 5:6 targets **Cerinthus**.  
Cerinthus: *"The divine Christ-spirit descended upon Jesus at baptism (water), and departed right before the cross (blood). Therefore, the divine Christ did not shed blood and did not die."*

John's refutation: **The same Jesus Christ came both by water (start of baptism ministry) and by blood (cross).**

> **If the Comma (verse 7) is a later insertion**, the inserter must have been someone who perfectly understood the core of the Cerinthian heresy debate and intricately designed the theological structure of verses 7-8.  
> At that level, it should rather be the Apostle John himself, or a figure of comparable authority.

## 5. Core Evidence 4 — Structural Resonance with Genesis 1:26-27

### 5-1. Grammatical Structure of Genesis 1:26-27

Verses 26 and 27 are an inseparable theological unit:

- **Verse 26 (Plan):** "Let **us** make man in **our** image, after **our** likeness..."  
  → God's plural pronouns ("us", "our") — Joint declaration of the Trinity

- **Verse 27 (Execution):** "So God **created** man in his own image, in the image of God **created** he him; male and female **created** he them."  
  → **"created" (Hebrew: בָּרָא, bara) is repeated exactly 3 times.**

### 5-2. Threefold Repetition of 'bara' — Rhetorical Analysis

In Hebrew prose, a threefold repetition of the same verb is a **Climactic Repetition**. The subject of creation declared as "us (Trinity)" in verse 26 is expressed as a **threefold act of creation** in verse 27.

```
Genesis 1:26  "us / our"    → Declaration of plural subject of creation
Genesis 1:27  bara × 3      → Threefold emphatic act of creation
                   ↕ Old-New Testament structural resonance
1 John 5:7    3 Witnesses   → Father, Word, Holy Ghost
```

> ⚠️ **Caution:** The Bible does not explicitly assign each 'bara' as an **individual, separated act** of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. The correct reading is "The act of creation accomplished together by the Triune God is expressed with a threefold emphasis," and this resonates with the structure of the three witnesses in 1 John 5:7.

### 5-3. Honest Evaluation regarding '22 Words'

Genesis 1:27 and 1 John 5:7 each coincide with exactly 22 words in the KJV English translation, a pattern connected to the 22-letter Hebrew alphabet structure (symbolizing perfection) in Psalm 119.

> ⚠️ **Clarifying Limitations:** This coincidence is **limited to the KJV English translation**. The number of words in the Hebrew Gen 1:27 and the Greek 1 John 5:7 do not match at 22.  
> This pattern should only be presented as a **Supporting Observation**, and if used alone as independent evidence in textual criticism debates, it will face criticism of circular logic.
