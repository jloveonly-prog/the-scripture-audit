> **Analysis Purpose**: To trace the true biblical meaning of the "new song" appearing in Revelation 5:9 and 14:3, and the reason why they sang this song, and based on this, to present BVCAP verification standards capable of judging the biblical soundness of related sermons.
> **C-Code**: C-13 (Confusion of Spiritual Category and Subject) / C-04 (Logical Narrative Difference)

## Phase 1: Biblical Evidence Data (Raw Data)

**1. 'New Song' Verses in Revelation (Conflict/Comparison Texts)**
* **Rev 5:9** "And they sung **a new song**, saying... and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation;"
* **Rev 14:3** "And they sung **as it were a new song** before the throne, and before the four beasts, and the elders: and no man could learn **that song** but the hundred and forty and four thousand, which were redeemed from the earth."

* **Isaiah 42:10** "Sing unto the LORD **a new song**, and his praise from the end of the earth..."

## Phase 2: BVCAP TYPE Weapon Dissection and Meaning Analysis

### 1. TYPE-U (Law of First Mention)
The place where "new song" first appears or its meaning is most clearly defined in the Bible is Psalm 40.
* **State A**: Fallen into a horrible pit and miry clay (death and despair).
* **Event B**: God directly intervenes and brings them up (Deliverance/Salvation).
* **Result C**: A "new song" is put in the mouth of the saved one.
* **Analysis Conclusion**: In the Bible, a "new song" does not mean a musical genre (e.g., latest pop song, CCM) or simply a 'new mindset'. **A new song is a spiritual response (testimony) that can only be sung by "one who has directly experienced redemption and victory from the crisis of death."**

### 2. TYPE-C (Functional Category Separation - Old Creation vs New Creation)
* In Exodus 15, Moses and the people of Israel sang the 'Song of Moses (old song)' after crossing the Red Sea. This was praising the **fleshly/physical salvation** from Egypt (the world).
* The 'new song' of Revelation 5 praises the **spiritual/new creation salvation (redemption)** through the blood of the Lamb. Those belonging to the Old Creation cannot sing it, and it is the 'praise of the new covenant' that can only be sung by those who have become a New Creature in Jesus Christ.

### 3. TYPE-G (Original Language Grammar Structure) and TYPE-T (Exposing Vocabulary Misreading)
* **Rev 5:9 (a new song)**: A universal new song of redemption sung by the 24 elders (typifying the church) redeemed by blood from every nation and tongue.
* **Rev 14:3 (that song)**: The 144,000 who kept their chastity to the end and overcame the mark of the beast in the Great Tribulation of the Antichrist. They also sing the 'genre of a new song', but the depth of its lyrics and experience is something only they went through, so it is limited by the definite article (**that**).
* **Why can only they learn it?**: Salvation cannot be learned through knowledge. It is because only the survivors who directly experienced (went through) God's protection in the flesh amidst the agony of the Great Tribulation can digest the spiritual weight of that song.

### 4. Decisive Differences Between the Two 'New Songs' (Rev 5:9 vs Rev 14:3)

The new songs appearing in the two texts share the same broad category (genre) of "praise of redemption through the blood of the Lamb," but the spiritual/historical categories of the subjects singing them are completely different.

| Comparison Item | Revelation 5:9 | Revelation 14:3 | Core of the Difference (BVCAP Analysis) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subject Singing** | The 24 elders and four beasts (Representatives/Types of the Church) | The 144,000 (12 tribes of Israel) | Universal church including Gentiles vs Special group of Jews who passed through the Great Tribulation |
| **Scope of Redemption** | "every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation" | "redeemed from the earth" (In 14:4 "redeemed from among men") | Universal salvation vs Exclusive survival and redemption |
| **Original Language/Grammar (TYPE-G)** | ᾠδὴν καινὴν (Some **a new song**) | ὡς ᾠδὴν καινὴν (**as it were a new song**) <br> τὴν ᾠδὴν (**that song**) | The song of chapter 14 is not a universal new song but their own special and limited (That) testimony. |
| **Content of the Song (Experience)** | Universal joy of being freed from sin and becoming kings and priests | Testimony of unprecedented suffering passage, overcoming the mark of the beast and keeping chastity (14:4-5) | Victory song of regular soldiers (Rev 5) vs Requiem of frontline special forces survivors (Rev 14) |
| **Whether the Song is Shared** | Sung together by all the saved | **No man could learn but** the 144,000 | The weight of the unexperienced tribulation cannot be spiritually imitated by other saints (including the 24 elders) (Exclusivity). |

**A "new song" is the 'requiem' and 'victory song' of veterans.**
