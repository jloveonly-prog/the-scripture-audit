## 3. Second Court: Physical Historical Evidence of the Passover (Unleavened Bread and Transparent Diluted Wine)
For WMSCOG to claim they 'identically' restored and perform the Passover as Jesus did 2,000 years ago, it must perfectly match the actual practices in Israel at that time. However, their ritual is completely detached from the historical and symbolic context of the biblical era.

1. **Unleavened Bread and Diluted Wine Mixed with Water (Historical Background)**
   On the Passover table during Jesus' time, unfermented **unleavened bread** was served, and the wine, due to water quality issues of the time, was a **nearly transparent diluted beverage** heavily mixed with water (a ratio of 3~4 parts water to 1 part wine). If they wish to act 'identically' to what Jesus did, they must drink a transparent grape beverage diluted with water, just like the Israelite custom, not modern dark red wine. When the Apostle Paul told Timothy to "use a little wine for thy stomach's sake" (1 Tim 5:23), it was for this diluted medical/sterilization purpose.

2. **Red Wine = The Fierce Wrath of God (TYPE-T Symbol Analysis)**
   In the Bible, **'undiluted (unmixed) thick red wine'** symbolizes not the covenant of life, but **God's dreadful judgment and wrath**.
   - **[Proverbs 23:31]** "Look not thou upon the wine when it is **red**, when it giveth his colour in the cup..."
   - **[Revelation 14:10]** "The same shall drink of the **wine of the wrath** of God, which is poured out **without mixture** into the cup of his indignation..."
   - **[Revelation 17:6]** "And I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus..."
   - **Verdict:** In the Bible, those who drink 'unmixed red wine' are not saved people, but those who suffer judgment and torment. The act of drinking blood-red liquor is a severe misuse that directly contradicts the biblical symbolic system.
