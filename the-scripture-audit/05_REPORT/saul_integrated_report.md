### ❓ FAQ-6: **Final Demolition** of the Claim that "With Me" = Comprehensive Entry into Sheol

#### 🏹 TYPE-G + TYPE-L: Grammar Chain + Chain of Inference

```
[Premise 1] Between Paradise and the place of torment in Sheol = Great gulf fixed (Luke 16:26)
[Premise 2] Samuel = In Paradise (disquieted = peaceful rest disturbed)
[Premise 3] Samuel says: "tomorrow shalt thou and thy sons be with me (עִמִּי)"

[Calculation Supplement — 🚨 Principle of the Impossibility of Dual Destination Indication]
If, as the prosecution claims, Saul goes to the place of torment and the righteous Jonathan goes to Paradise:

Samuel would be saying, "Thou (torment) and thy sons (Paradise) shall be with me (עִמִּי)."

  → Grammatically, the dual indication of "with me (in Torment) and with me (in Paradise)" cannot be established.

Conclusion: If Saul and Jonathan are grouped together and referred to as "with me," it is only linguistically established if both are on the same side of the great gulf (Paradise where Samuel is). 
Therefore, "with me" cannot be an idiomatic expression for the afterlife, and accurately points to the exact same location (Paradise). ✅
```

#### 🛡️ [BVCAP Defense 1] Regarding the claim that "there is no final repentance"

#### 🛡️ [BVCAP Defense 2] Defense against the counter-rebuttal on "God becoming an enemy"

> **Summary of Rebuttal:** Whether it's 'Saul is the enemy' or 'God is the enemy', the essence is 'destruction of relationship' and 'discipline/judgment'. God becoming an enemy means he has passed over to being an object of judgment rather than being under protection, so it is heavier.

*   **Analysis:** When the people of Israel rebelled, God "was turned to be their enemy" and fought against them. But did the entirety of Israel go to hell forever? No. This is the 'heaviest dispensational/physical discipline' upon covenant people, not a sentence to hell.

**2. 1 Corinthians 5:5 (KJV) — Destruction of the flesh and salvation of the spirit**
> *"To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus."*
*   **Analysis:** Even for a saved person, if they commit serious sin, God withdraws His protection and delivers them to Satan for the "destruction of the flesh (death penalty/disease)." Saul, too, had God become his adversary to take away his physical life and kingship (1 Chron 10:13). Saul paid the price for his sin by having his flesh judged and dying in battle, but this does not guarantee the destruction of his soul (hell).

#### 🛡️ [BVCAP Defense 3] Complete refutation of the 'prison metaphor' and 'comprehensive death expression'

> **Summary of Rebuttal:** The expression 'together' is a comprehensive expression like lying down with the dead. Even if prisoners in a prison cannot freely move between cells, if asked "Where are you?", they can say "We are together in prison."

**⚖️ Verdict: ❌ Rejected (TYPE-L: Inductive Chain of Inference / Collision with Luke 16 Anchor)**

Does the Bible actually express it that way?

> **Luke 16:26 (KJV)**
> *"And beside all this, between us and you there is a great gulf fixed: so that they which would pass from hence to you cannot; neither can they pass to us, that would come from thence."*

*   **Detection of Fatal Contradiction:** Abraham never groups himself with the rich man and says "We are together (with me) in this Sheol." He strictly and perfectly separates the pronouns by calling them **"Us - Paradise"** and **"You - Place of Torment"**.
