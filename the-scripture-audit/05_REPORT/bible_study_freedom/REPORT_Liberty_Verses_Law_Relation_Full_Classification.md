# 🏛️ [Liberty] Full Survey: Complete Classification of the Correlation with Law
**— "Among all the verses contained in `Liberty.md`, which are related to the law, and at what level are they related?" A BVCAP v2.0 Classification Survey —**

> **STATUS**: Full Survey Complete | **Character**: not a Doctrinal Ruling (VERDICT), but a **Classification** Survey
> **Source Material**: `05_REPORT(전과보고서)/자유.md` (results of a full KJV search for "liberty/free/liberty," approximately 100 verses)
> **Related Document**: [REPORT_Law_Liberty_Correlation_Audit.md](./REPORT_Law_Liberty_Correlation_Audit.md) — an in-depth report that extracts only the theologically contrasting verses corresponding to Group 3 of this survey, and renders a 4-category doctrinal ruling on them
> **Purpose of Classification**: to fully classify every verse of `Liberty.md` into Groups 1-5 according to "level of relationship with the law," providing a map for which group of verses subsequent doctrinal audits (BVCAP Masterpiece) should take as their target.

---

## 1. Classification Criteria

| Group | Criterion |
|:---:|:---|
| **Group 1** | **Social-law provisions** directly stipulated by the Mosaic Law — liberty is itself the ruling/statute of the law |
| **Group 2** | **Ceremonial-law provisions** (freewill offerings and vows) stipulated by the Mosaic Law |
| **Group 3** | Verses in which the word "law" is **directly juxtaposed** in the text, theologically discussing the law itself |
| **Group 4** | Cases where the law (especially the Jubilee law) is taken as **background/allusion**, while the text itself is prophecy or narrative |
| **Group 5** | **Unrelated** to the law — social status, adverbial usage, or poetic expression |

---

## 2. Group 1 — Direct Legal Stipulation (Social Law: The Freedom/Release of Servants)

| Verse | Content of the Stipulation |
|---|---|
| Exod. 21:2, 5 | a Hebrew servant is free in the 7th year, after 6 years (the Covenant Code) |
| Exod. 21:11 | a maidservant's freedom upon three failures of provision |
| Exod. 21:26-27 | a servant's freedom upon injury to eye/tooth (a law protecting the person) |
| Lev. 19:20 | stipulating the "not free" status of a betrothed maidservant |
| Lev. 25:10 | the proclamation of Jubilee (50-year) liberty — the core of the Sabbatical-year law |
| Num. 5:19, 28 | "free from the curse" under the ceremonial law of the Sotah (the trial for jealousy) |
| Deut. 15:12-13, 18 | the law releasing a Hebrew servant free in the 7th year (paralleling Exod. 21) |
| Deut. 24:5 | the stipulation of a newly married man's one-year exemption (freedom) from military service |
| Ezek. 46:17 | "the year of liberty" until the Jubilee — the future application of the Jubilee law of Lev. 25 |

## 3. Group 2 — Direct Legal Stipulation (Ceremonial Law: Freewill Offerings and Vows)

Lev. 22:18, 21, 23 / Lev. 23:38 / Num. 15:3 / Num. 29:39 / Deut. 12:6, 17 / Deut. 16:10 / Deut. 23:23 — all legal provisions concerning "an offering given of one's own free will (freewill offering)."
(2 Chron. 31:14, Ezra 1:4, 2:68, 3:5, 7:13,15,16, 8:28 are incidentally related as **historical instances of the implementation** of this legal provision)

## 4. Group 3 — Verses Where "Law" Itself Is Named and Theologically Contrasted/Connected (The Core Group)

| Verse | Content |
|---|---|
| Ps. 119:45, 108 | specifies that the ground of liberty (living freely) is "thy precepts/statutes" — a Torah psalm |
| Rom. 7:3 | "free from that **law**" (the marriage-law analogy) |
| Rom. 8:2 | "free from the **law** of sin and death... the **law** of the Spirit of life" — the core of Paul's theology of law |
| 1 Cor. 7:39 | "bound by the **law**... there is liberty" |
| 2 Cor. 3:17 | "where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty" — the entire context (2 Cor. 3) is a contrast between 'the letter (the law) and the Spirit' |
| Gal. 2:4 | liberty in Christ vs. the bondage imposed by false brethren (legalism/the circumcision party) |
| Gal. 3:28 | the distinction of bondage/liberty dissolved — not under the law (the entire argument of Galatians 3) |
| Gal. 4:22-31 | the allegory of Hagar (the Sinai covenant = the law) / Sarah (the free woman = the promise) — **the most direct law-vs-liberty passage** |
| Gal. 5:1, 13 | "be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage [=the law]" / "called unto liberty" |
| James 1:25 | "the perfect **law** of liberty" |
| James 2:12 | "they that shall be judged by the **law** of liberty" |

## 5. Group 4 — Prophetic Allusion Set Against the Background of the Law (the Jubilee Law)

| Verse | Content |
|---|---|
| Isa. 58:6 | freeing the oppressed = the spirit of Jubilee |
| Isa. 61:1 | proclaiming liberty to the captives = Jubilee language |
| Luke 4:18 | quotes Isa. 61 — the Messiah fulfills the Jubilee |
| Jer. 34:8-17 | Zedekiah's oath to free servants, and its violation — a historical instance of the implementation/breach of the law of Deut. 15 (a border case between Groups 1 and 3) |

## 6. Group 5 — Unrelated to the Law (Summary)

Num. 11:5 (recalling food) / Josh. 9:23, 1 Sam. 14:30, 17:25, Ps. 105:20 (historical narrative) / Job 3:19, 39:5, Ps. 51:12, 88:5, Hos. 14:4 (poetic/metaphorical) / John 8:32-36 (freedom from sin — the central contrast is 'sin/truth' rather than 'the law') / Romans 6 (freedom from sin — the central contrast is sin rather than the law) / Acts 2:29, 22:28, 24:23, 26:26, 27:3 (adverbial usage/Roman citizenship) / 1 Cor. 7:21-22, 9:1,19, 10:29, 12:13, Rom. 8:21, Eph. 6:8, Col. 3:11, 2 Thess. 3:1, 1 Pet. 2:16, 2 Pet. 2:19, Rev. 6:15, 13:16, 19:18 (listings of social status)

## 7. Conclusion of the Survey

Of the approximately 100 total verses in `Liberty.md`, the verses bearing some correlation with the law, in any form, sum to **approximately 35 across Groups 1-4**, and among these, the verses that theologically intersect directly with the concept of "law" itself are condensed to **the 11 verses of Group 3**. Groups 1-2 (the provisions of the Mosaic Law) form the archetypal text upon which Group 3 (the theology of law in Paul and James) stands, while Group 4 (Jubilee allusions) serves as the prophetic bridge connecting the two.
