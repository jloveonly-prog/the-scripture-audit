# 📜 BVCAP MASTERPIECE REPORT: Biblical Meaning of "New Song" and Analysis of the Category of Redemption

> **Purpose of Analysis**: To trace the true biblical meaning of the "new song" appearing in Revelation 5:9 and 14:3 and the reason they sang this song, and based on this, present BVCAP verification standards to determine the biblical Soundness of related sermons.
> **C-Code**: C-13 (Confusion of Spiritual Category and Subject) / C-04 (Logical Narrative Difference)

---

## Phase 1: Biblical Ground Data (Raw Data)

**1. The 'New Song' Verses in Revelation (Collision/Comparison Text)**
* **Revelation 5:9** "And they sung **a new song**, saying... and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation..."
* **Revelation 14:3** "And they sung **as it were a new song** before the throne, and before the four beasts, and the elders: and no man could learn **that song** but the hundred and forty and four thousand, which were redeemed from the earth."

**2. The First Mention and Usage of 'New Song' in the Old Testament (Anchor Data)**
* **Psalm 40:2-3** "He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay... And he hath put **a new song** in my mouth, even praise unto our God..."
* **Psalm 98:1** "O sing unto the LORD **a new song**; for he hath done marvellous things: his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory."
* **Isaiah 42:10** "Sing unto the LORD **a new song**, and his praise from the end of the earth..."

---

## Phase 2: BVCAP TYPE Weapon Dissection and Semantic Analysis

### 1. TYPE-U (Law of First Mention)
The place where "new song" first appears in the Bible or its meaning is most clearly defined is Psalm 40.
* **State A**: Fallen into a horrible pit and miry clay (death and despair).
* **Event B**: God directly intervenes and pulls them up (Deliverance).
* **Result C**: A "new song" is put in the mouth of the saved one.
* **Analysis Conclusion**: In the Bible, a "new song" does not mean a genre of music (e.g., the latest pop song, CCM) or simply a 'new mindset'. **A new song is a spiritual response (testimony) that can only be sung by "one who has directly experienced Redemption and Victory from the crisis of death."**

### 2. TYPE-C (Functional Category Separation - Old Creation vs New Creation)
* In Exodus 15, Moses and the Israelites sang the 'Song of Moses (old song)' after crossing the Red Sea. This praised **fleshly/physical salvation** from Egypt (the world).
* The 'new song' in Revelation 5 praises the **salvation of the spiritual/new creation (redemption)** through the blood of the Lamb. It is a 'praise of the new covenant' that those belonging to the Old Creation cannot sing, but only those who have become a New Creature in Jesus Christ can sing.

### 3. TYPE-G (Original Grammar Structure) and TYPE-T (Detection of Vocabulary Misreading)
* **Revelation 5:9 (a new song)**: A universal new song of redemption sung by the 24 elders (typifying the church) redeemed by blood from every nation and tongue.
* **Revelation 14:3 (that song)**: The 144,000 who kept their purity to the end and overcame the mark of the beast during the great tribulation of the Antichrist. They also sing 'the genre of a new song', but the lyrics and depth of experience are exclusive to what they went through, so it is limited by a definite article (**that**).
* **Why can only they learn it?**: Salvation cannot be learned through knowledge. It is because only the survivors who directly experienced (felt on their skin) God's protection amidst the agony of the great tribulation can digest the spiritual weight of that song.

### 4. Comparison of the Decisive Differences Between the Two 'New Songs' (Rev 5:9 vs Rev 14:3)

The new songs appearing in the two texts share the same broad category (genre) of "praise of redemption through the blood of the Lamb," but the spiritual/historical categories of the subjects singing them are completely different.

| Comparison Item | Revelation 5:9 | Revelation 14:3 | Core of Difference (BVCAP Analysis) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Singing Subject** | 24 Elders and four beasts (Representatives/type of the church) | 144,000 (12 tribes of Israel) | Universal church including Gentiles vs Special group of Jews who passed through the Great Tribulation |
| **Scope of Redemption** | "every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation" | "redeemed from the earth" (in 14:4 "redeemed from among men") | Universal salvation vs Exclusive survival and redemption |
| **Original/Grammar (TYPE-G)** | ᾠδὴν καινὴν (Some one **new song**) | ὡς ᾠδὴν καινὴν (**as it were** a new song) <br> τὴν ᾠδὴν (**that song**) | The song in chapter 14 is not a universal new song, but their own special and limited (That) testimony. |
| **Content of Song (Experience)** | Universal joy of being freed from sin and becoming kings and priests | Testimony of passing through unprecedented suffering, overcoming the mark of the beast, and keeping purity (14:4-5) | Victory song of regular soldiers (Rev 5) vs Requiem of frontline special forces survivors (Rev 14) |
| **Whether the Song is Shared** | Sung together by all the saved | **No man could learn but** the 144,000 | The weight of the unexperienced tribulation cannot be spiritually mimicked by other saints (including the 24 elders) (Exclusivity). |

## Phase 4: Modern Analogy (ANALOGY-5)

**A "New Song" is the 'requiem' and 'victory song' of veterans.**
No matter how excellent a civilian's singing ability is, who has never been in the military or seen a war movie, they can never "learn" and sing in place of the hoarse 'military song' sung by old veterans who survived witnessing the deaths of their comrades on the frontlines of the Normandy Landings or the Korean War.
* **People of the world who are not saved** do not even know the existence of this military song (new song).
* **The church (24 elders) of Revelation 5** sings the victory song of 'universal salvation', delivered from sin and death by the blood of the cross.
* **The 144,000 of Revelation 14** are the most elite survivors who lived through the most horrific great tribulation (rule of the Antichrist) in history, and they sing the 'victory song of special forces (That song)' which only they can sing.
