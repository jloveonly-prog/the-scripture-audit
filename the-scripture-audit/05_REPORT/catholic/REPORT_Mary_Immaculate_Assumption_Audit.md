## Part 1 — Mary's Immaculate Conception

> **Catholic Dogmatic Definition (1854, Pius IX — Ineffabilis Deus):**
> "The Blessed Virgin Mary, in the first instant of her conception, by a singular grace and privilege of Almighty God, in view of the merits of Jesus Christ the Saviour of the human race, was preserved free from all stain of original sin."

---

### [Immaculate Conception Collapse Evidence 1] Romans 3:23 — "All have sinned"

**KJV Text:**
> *"For **all have sinned**, and come short of the glory of God."* (Rom. 3:23)

**TYPE-Q (Linguistic Quantifier Analysis)**:
- "**all**" — Greek **πάντες (pantes)**: A universal quantifier encompassing all without exception.
- This verse comes immediately after quoting Romans 3:10-12: "There is none righteous, no, not one... there is none that doeth good, no, not one."
- Catholicism claims "Mary was excluded by a special exceptional grace," but Scripture's "all" specifies no exceptions whatsoever.

**TYPE-AI (Reductio ad Absurdum)**:
```
IF Mary was born without original sin
THEN Mary must be excluded from "all have sinned" in Rom. 3:23

However:
Scripture nowhere designates Mary as an exception to the "all have sinned" rule.
Creating an exception outside Scripture (via papal declaration) violates Rev. 22:18 ("add not unto" Scripture).

∴ The dogma of Mary's Immaculate Conception conflicts with Scripture itself.
```

### [Immaculate Conception Collapse Evidence 2] Luke 1:47 — Mary's Own Self-Confession ⚡NUCLEAR

**KJV Text:**
> *"And my spirit hath rejoiced in **God my Saviour**."* (Luke 1:47)

In the Magnificat, which Mary sang while filled with the Holy Spirit, Mary herself confesses **"God my Saviour."**

**Logical Structure:**

| Premise | Content |
|:---|:---|
| Scriptural Principle | A Saviour comes to save sinners (Matt. 1:21) |
| Mary's Confession | Mary confessed God as "my Saviour" (Luke 1:47) |
| Logical Consequence | Mary needs a Saviour = Mary is a sinner in need of salvation |
| Catholic Claim | Mary was born without original sin and needs no salvation |
| **Conclusion** | **The Catholic dogma directly contradicts Mary's own confession** |

> **Key One-Liner**: "To assert Mary's Immaculate Conception is to strip Mary of her own confession — 'my Saviour.'"

**[Demolition of Catholicism's Strongest Rebuttal]**
> Catholic: "The 'Saviour' in Luke 1:47 is Mary's expression of gratitude for being **pre-emptively** saved from original sin (pre-emptive salvation)."

Demolition (TYPE-S Lexical Independence + TYPE-AL):
- Exhaustive survey of "Saviour" usage in Scripture (Matt. 1:21, Luke 19:10, John 3:17): A Saviour always saves those who are **actually in danger or under sin**.
- The concept of "pre-emptive salvation" itself is extra-biblical logic (Scholastic theology) with no basis in the biblical text.
- Following this logic leads to the conclusion that "all people could have been pre-emptively saved but were not," creating a self-contradiction that conflicts with Scripture's doctrine of predestination.

### [Immaculate Conception Collapse Evidence 3] Romans 5:12 — Original Sin Passed Upon "All Men"

**KJV Text:**
> *"Wherefore, as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon **all men**, for that all have sinned."* (Rom. 5:12)

Through the sin of one man, Adam, death passed upon "all men." Since Mary was born as a descendant of Adam (Luke 3:23-38 — Mary's genealogy traces back to Adam), she cannot automatically be exempt from the imputation of original sin.

---

### [Immaculate Conception Collapse Evidence 4] Luke 2:22-24 — Mary Performed the Purification Rite

**KJV Text:**
> *"And when the days of her purification according to the law of Moses were accomplished..."* (Luke 2:22)

After giving birth to Jesus, Mary performed **the purification rite according to the Law of Moses (Lev. 12:2-8)**. The purification rite presupposes a state of uncleanness. If one were born without original sin, there would be no reason for uncleanness and thus no need for a purification rite. Yet Mary herself underwent the purification rite required by the Law.

## Part 2 — Mary's Bodily Assumption

> **Catholic Dogmatic Definition (1950, Pius XII — Munificentissimus Deus):**

In all 27 books of the New Testament, there is **not a single line** recording Mary's bodily assumption.

### [Assumption Collapse Evidence 3] 1 Corinthians 15:23 — Violation of the Resurrection Order
