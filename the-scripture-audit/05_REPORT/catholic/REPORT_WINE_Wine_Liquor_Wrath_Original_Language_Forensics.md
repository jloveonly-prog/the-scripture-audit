### 🔍 8-B. "Even if Water is Added to Wine, It is Still Wine" — Ancient Naming Customs and Metonymy Analysis

#### Core Question

> Ancient people drank undiluted wine mixed with water at a 3:1 ratio (water 3 : wine 1). So, 75% of what is in that cup is **water**. Yet, the Bible still calls this diluted beverage **"wine"**. Why is it called "wine" instead of "water"? Are there other naming customs like this?

#### Answer: Metonymy — "The Core Component That Grants Identity" Determines the Name

In linguistics, this phenomenon is called **metonymy**. The name of the entire beverage does not follow the component with the largest volume, but the name of the **core component that determines its identity, taste, and function**.

#### Universal Usage in Modern Daily Life

| Beverage | Actual Composition | Volume Ratio | Name Called | Basis for the Name |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| ☕ **Coffee** | Water + Coffee extract | Water 98% : Coffee 2% | "Coffee" | Coffee determines identity, not water |
| 🍵 **Tea** | Hot water + Steeped tea leaves | Water 99% : Tea 1% | "Tea" | Tea leaves determine taste and function |
| 🥛 **Almond Milk** | Water + Almond extract | Water 97% : Almond 3% | "Milk" | Not mammal's milk, but similar in appearance/use |
| 🍷 **Ancient Wine** | Water + Undiluted wine | Water 75% : Wine 25% | **"Wine"** | Wine determines taste/sterilizing power/function |
| 🧃 **Lemonade** | Water + Lemon juice + Sugar | Water 90%+ | "Lemonade" | Lemon determines identity |
| 🍺 **Beer** | Water + Malt fermented product | Water 90~95% | "Beer" | Malt determines identity |

**Core Principle:** 98% of a cup of coffee is water, but no one calls it "water". 97% of almond milk is water, but it is called "milk". In exactly the same principle, 75% of a cup of ancient table wine was water, but it was called "wine". **The name follows identity, not volume.**

#### Particularly Noteworthy Usage: Modern Greek "Krasi (κρασί)"

The word for "wine" in modern Greek, **krasi (κρασί)**, originated from ancient Greek **krasis (κρᾶσις)**. The meaning of krasis is **"mixture"**. That is, to the ancient Greeks, the word "wine" itself already meant **"that which is mixed (with water)"**. Modern Greeks no longer mix their wine with water, but the name "mixture" remains and has become the standard word for "wine".
