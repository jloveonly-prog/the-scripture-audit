## 📐 Principles for Handling Italics (Codified 2026-08-19)

Italics in the KJV indicate **words supplied by translators where there are no corresponding words in the original languages (TR, Masoretic).** In plaintext files, **`[ ]` is the italic itself** — it is not a substitute symbol but the sole means of expressing italics in plaintext.

```
Printed Bible    darkness *was* upon        (Italics)
KJBO(HTML)       darkness <em>was</em>
KJV_1769.txt     darkness [was] upon        ← Same information, different grammar
```

**4 Major Rules**

1. **Italicized words are also Bible text.** Use them exactly as they are in quotations and judgments. Do not omit or ignore them.
2. **Maintain the `[ ]` notation when quoting.** Reveal simultaneously the fact that there are no corresponding original language words.
3. **When placing decisive argumentative weight on an italicized word, specify this fact.** If used while hidden, an opponent will dismantle it with a single remark: *"That's a supplied word."* **By disclosing it first, that counterattack is blocked.**
4. **The reverse direction is also a weapon.** If an opponent bases an argument on an italicized word, **pointing out that it is a supplied word is in itself a valid counterargument.**

**Why these rules are necessary — Actual Verses**

| Verse | Text | Implication |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **2 Sam. 21:19** | Elhanan … slew **[the brother of]** Goliath | Removing the italics results in *"Elhanan slew Goliath,"* which conflicts with the record of David. Supplied from 1 Chron. 20:5. |
| **1 John 2:23** | …**[(but) he that acknowledgeth the Son hath the Father also]** | **The entire latter half is supplied words.** Arguing without knowing this will get you caught. |
| **Isa. 9:6** | Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God… | **0 italics** — means all have original language equivalents, strengthening the argument itself. |
| **Acts 20:28** | the church of God, which he hath purchased… | **0 italics** — the fact that it is entirely original text supports the deity controversy. |

> 🚨 **Searches are conducted as words without `[ ]`.** That is the reason for `KJV_1769_search.txt`'s existence. **The judgment authority rests in the italic-preserved version (`KJV_1769.txt`).**

## Why 1769?

## 5 Evidences

### 1️⃣ 1769 is the standard text for all KJVs today

Over about four years in 1769, **Benjamin Blayney** comprehensively reviewed the KJV, correcting thousands of typographical errors, standardizing weights and measures, and rectifying spelling and punctuation. **Virtually all KJVs printed and distributed today are descendants of this 1769 standard.**

### 2️⃣ The alternative PCE was **not** an edition produced by Cambridge University

* The text belongs to the Cambridge printing lineage from **c. 1900**.
* **The name "Pure Cambridge Edition" is a title given in the 2000s by Matthew Verschuur** (Australia, bibleprotector.com).
* **Cambridge University Press (CUP) neither publishes nor recognizes an edition by this name.** Current CUP editions include Concord, Cameo, etc.
* It reads like a university-authorized edition due to the word "Cambridge," but it is not. → **Decisive reason for disqualification as a canonical text.**

### 3️⃣ ★ Spelling differences create "0 total instances" misjudgments — Empirical Case

> **Isaiah 9:6** is a **reference case for GATE 8** of `SVAP_Pipeline.md`.
>
> | Edition | Text |
> |:---|:---|
> | **1769 (Current Canonical)** | his name shall be called Wonderful, **Counsellor**, The mighty God… |
> | PCE (Removed) | his name shall be called Wonderful, **Counseller**, The mighty God… |
>
> **Searching for `Counsellor` in PCE yields 0 results.**
> If this is directly recorded as "0 total instances," TYPE-AG (argument from silence) will make a judgment based on a **non-existent silence**. This is the **most dangerous failure type** in this project, and it happens exactly in the verse our field manual uses as an empirical case.

### 4️⃣ "Open it up yourself" only works if it matches the opponent's Bible

The combat card of SVAP (PART D) relies on the 🟢 argument that **"Showing them the text with their own eyes ends it."** The Bible in the opponent's hands is nine times out of ten a modern printed edition. If we quote from the PCE, we write `rasor`, `inquire`, `Counseller`, and the opponent **cannot find those words** in their Bible. Even if the argument is sound, the delivery fails.

### 5️⃣ It matches the reference site

The text of the **[King James Bible Online](https://www.kingjamesbibleonline.org/)** belongs to the 1769 lineage, and it matches the title page of our canonical text (§4).
**The judgment text, the cross-reference site, and the opponent's printed Bible** are all aligned in a single lineage.

### ⚖️ Korean Text Quotation Rules — Confirming the Scope of ND Application (2026-08-19)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Correction of previous edition errors**: The execution rules stated *"**Abbreviation** and notation changes prohibited"*, and the interpretation table stated *"Quoting in a processed form → Prohibited"*.
> **Grouping `abbreviation` and `notation changes` together was a mistake.** The former is free, while the latter must be obeyed.

#### ① Quotation is not "explanation" — Let's distinguish the terms first

| Term | Meaning | Copyright |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Quotation** | Using the original text **exactly as is** | Statutorily permitted (Article 28) |
| **Explanation/Analysis** | Text written directly by the auditor | Irrelevant to copyright |
| **Derivative** | Modifying the original to **create a new work** | 🔴 **What ND prohibits** |

Transcribing a Bible verse into a report is a **quotation** and is not subject to ND.

#### ② What is allowed and what is not

Original Text Example: `And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.`

| Usage in Report | Judgment | Basis |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Quoting **only the necessary one verse** out of 31,102 | 🟢 | **Excerpting = Normal form of quotation** |
| Quoting **only three words** (`the Spirit of God`) | 🟢 | Quotation to the extent necessary for the argument |
| Indicating omissions with `…` | 🟢 | Academic quotation convention |
| Gathering several verses to make a comparison table | 🟢 | Merely a collection of quotations |
| **Analyzing, criticizing, or refuting** the quoted verse | 🟢 | The very purpose of quotation |
| **Changing words in the quotation** (`Spirit` → `Holy Spirit`) | 🔴 | **This is an alteration, not a quotation** |
| **Publicly distributing the processed file itself** | 🔴 | **This is the only place ND is violated** |

> 🚨 **The boundary is not "length," but "did you alter the letters?"**
> Using only three words is a quotation, and using a whole verse is a quotation. **The moment you change a letter,** it is no longer a quotation.
> Since we have no practical reason to modify and distribute words, **there are virtually no restrictions on report work.**

#### ③ Why quotation is not caught by ND — 3 Bases

| # | Basis |
|:--:|:---|
| **1** | **Statutory permission overrides licenses.** Copyright Act **Article 28** (Quotation from Publicly Disclosed Works) — Quotation within a justifiable scope for *news reporting, criticism, education, or research* is **directly permitted by law**. It is not an area a license can restrict. |
| **2** | **The CC 4.0 text stipulates this itself.** *"Nothing in this Public License constitutes or may be construed as permission to assert or imply that You are, or that Your use of the Licensed Material is, connected with, or sponsored, endorsed, or granted official status by, the Licensor or others designated to receive attribution as provided in Section 3(a)(1)(A)(i)."* wait, no, *"This license does not affect exceptions and limitations to copyright such as fair use"* — **Even with ND attached, the right to quote remains.** |
| **3** | **The actual target of the copyright page text.** *"Dishonest change of the text is forbidden"* targets **alteration (distortion) of the main text**. Excerpting for analysis is standard practice in commentaries and papers. |

#### ④ 🔴 Source attribution is mandatory — There are no exceptions to this

| # | Basis |
|:--:|:---|
| **1** | **Copyright Act Article 37 — Obligation to indicate sources.** Quotation is permitted, but **attribution is a statutory obligation.** The law demands it independently of the license. |
| **2** | **CC BY Condition.** The minimum requirement of the license |
| **3** | **This project's principle.** It is the **exact same logic** as the principle that *"You must state which Bible's 0 results are being claimed when stating 0 search results."* If the version is not stated, **the audit itself becomes unverifiable**.
