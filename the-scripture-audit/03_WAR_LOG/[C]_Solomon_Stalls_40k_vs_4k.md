## 2. KJV Original Text Core Clue: A Crucial Difference Hidden in the Translation (V-03)

Most attackers claim a contradiction based only on the Korean Revised Version (KRV), but comparing the **KJV English original** immediately reveals a crucial difference.

| Verse | KJV Original Text | Core Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 Kings 4:26** | *"forty thousand stalls **of horses for his chariots**"* | Stalls **only for horses** |
| **2 Chron 9:25** | *"four thousand stalls **for horses and chariots**"* | Stalls **for both horses and chariots** together |

> **Core Finding**: The stalls in the two verses are **not the same type of stalls.**
> - 1 Kings 4:26: Stalls exclusively for housing **horses** (individual horse stall partitions).
> - 2 Chron 9:25: Complex operational unit stalls housing **horses and chariots together** (combat deployment units).

**1 Kings 10:26** specifies the number of Solomon's chariots:
> *"And Solomon gathered together chariots and horsemen: and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen..."*

**1,400** chariots → This is the basic combat unit.

**Calculation of Stalls per Unit:**

| Classification | Description | Expected Quantity |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Operational Unit Stalls (2 Chron 9:25)** | A complex facility housing 1 chariot + the trained horses assigned to that chariot together. Based on 1,400 chariots, estimated 3~4 chariot units deployed per stall facility. | **4,000** |
| **Overall Horse Housing Stalls (1 Kings 4:26)** | Total individual housing partitions for all horses throughout the kingdom. Includes all foals, mares, horses in training, reserve horses, transport horses, and cavalry horses. | **40,000** |

### Metaphorical Understanding: Comparison with Modern Military Systems

```
The 4,000 in 2 Chron 9:25 ← Like "4,000 Fighter Wings" (A combat unit grouping aircraft + pilot + maintenance team together)
The 40,000 in 1 Kings 4:26 ← Like "40,000 Aircraft Hangars" (Total sum of facilities housing all aircraft nationwide)

The same military force can be counted in two different ways.
```

### Demonstration from the Movie Ben-Hur: Ancient Chariot Military Management
- In ancient chariot warfare, the horses assigned to each chariot must **train together**.
- Horses pulling the same chariot must live in the **same space** and become accustomed to each other.
- Horses unsuitable for chariots are **reclassified as draft horses or cavalry horses** and moved to different stalls.
- Therefore, the number of operational unit stalls (2 Chron) and the number of total horse housing partitions (1 Kings) **must inevitably be different.**

## 6. Refuting the Copyist Error Theory: Manuscript Examination (A-02)

The attacker's claim is "a zero was left out due to a copyist error." However:

- **Masoretic Text**: The numbers in 1 Kings 4:26 and 2 Chron 9:25 are consistently maintained respectively.
- **Septuagint (LXX)**: Records 1 Kings 4:26 as 40,000.
- **Samaritan Pentateuch**: Verse not present.
- **If it were a copyist error**: If 1 Kings 4:26 was originally 4,000, a zero would have had to be added to become "40,000", but there is no explanation for how this error mechanism applied in the ancient Hebrew copying tradition. Hebrew numbers were written using an alphabetical system, so the error mechanism of simply dropping a single zero like Arabic numerals does not apply.

## 7. Final Verdict

### ✅ CONSISTENT — Consistency Confirmed

> **Reasoning for Verdict**:
> The 40,000 in 1 Kings 4:26 and the 4,000 in 2 Chronicles 9:25 are numbers referring to facilities of different functional categories. This functional difference is already specified in the KJV original text ("horses for his chariots" vs "horses AND chariots"), and a reverse calculation based on Solomon's 1,400 chariots (1 Kings 10:26) makes the figure of 4,000 operational stalls mathematically consistent. > **Core Counter-Argument Logic**:

Because Hebrew numbers are written in alphabetical letters, 4,000 and 40,000 are completely different combinations of words. The fact that all extant manuscripts maintain the two numbers respectively supports that this is an intentional recording, not an error.
