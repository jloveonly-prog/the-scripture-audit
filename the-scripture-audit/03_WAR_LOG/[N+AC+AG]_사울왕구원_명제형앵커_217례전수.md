## 1. Problem Structure — Why the existing ANCHOR-1 did not work

The original 3-step search algorithm in `ANCHOR_ThirdData.md` is **exclusive to numerical anomalies**.

| Existing Step | Actual Instruction | Applicable to King Saul incident? |
|:---:|:---|:---:|
| 1st | Scan **age, years, duration** within 5 chapters before and after | ❌ No numbers to substitute |
| 2nd | Cross-check **same incident** between Kings ↔ Chronicles | △ Partially |
| 3rd | Backtrack **genealogical data** | ❌ Irrelevant |
| STEP 4 | `[Anchor Value] ± [A Value] = [B Value]` | ❌ Formula cannot be established |
| STEP 5 | MATRIX Reverse Calculation — "It is a proof only if the math is correct" | ❌ |

**The Reality of the Conflict**: 1 Samuel 28:19 *"to morrow shalt thou and thy sons be **with me**"* — Samuel grouped Saul and his sons into a single pronoun group, declaring a single destination. However, whether "with me" means the **same compartment**, or the **macro-Sheol as a whole** as the opposing side claims, can never be settled by contrasting just two verses.

Since there are no numbers, reverse calculation is impossible, and there is nothing more to dig into the immediate context. **Here the existing anchor protocol stops.**

## 2. Decisive Blow ① — TYPE-N Exhaustive Search (Securing Anchor)

Instead of a formula, the **exhaustive usage** was taken as the anchor.

```
[Search Proposition] P = "'with me' signifies exactly the same coordinates/state"
[Search Scope] The entire 66 books of the KJV, usages of "with me" between persons
[Sample Size] 217 cases
```

| Search Result | Number of Cases |
|:---|:---:|
| Usages signifying **same coordinates** or **complete sharing of state/fate** (`abide with me`, `eat with me`, `die with me`) | **217 cases (All)** |
| Usages grouping opposite states across a great gulf as "together" | **0 cases** |

**Symmetry Check (Automatic search for counterexamples)**: To support the "macro-Sheol cohabitation theory" proposed by the opposing side, there must be at least 1 usage grouping two entities in different compartments into a single "with me". The exhaustive search result is **0 cases**.

**Crucial Cross Check**: In Luke 16:26, Abraham, speaking to the rich man in torment across the gulf, **never once groups them with "us" or "with me" and strictly separates them**. The Scripture itself keeps this distinction.

> 📌 **Reusability Point**: When there are no numbers to substitute, the **exhaustive statistics of "how this expression is actually used within the Scripture"** takes the place of a numerical anchor.

## 3. Decisive Blow ② — TYPE-AG Symmetry of Silence (Simultaneous Measurement of Both Sides)

Counting only one side's silence is biased. **Both sides were measured with the same standard.**

| Camp | KJV verses explicitly stating their conclusion | Argument Structure |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Hell Theory | **0 cases** | 100% indirect inference (All 4 extreme arguments dismissed in Round 3) |
| Paradise Theory | **0 cases** | Possesses evidence that is not indirect or silence (`immi` grouping, sin unto death pattern) |

Neither camp has an explicit verse. However, **the state of the argumentative material is different** — this asymmetry itself becomes a slight cue of weight.

> 📌 **Reusability Point**: The argument from silence **must be applied to both sides simultaneously**. Pointing out only one side's silence becomes evidence of bias in itself.

## 4. Decisive Blow ③ — TYPE-AA Separation of Ancient Law Provisions

The categories were split not by vocabulary but by the **structure of the law provisions**.

| Verse | Subject | Punishment |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Lev 20:6 | The **client** seeking after familiar spirits — Saul falls here (1 Chron 10:13 *"asking counsel of"*) | karet, cut off from among his people |
| Lev 20:27 | The **practitioner** of familiar spirits themselves | *"shall surely be put to death"* — Human court execution |

The fact that the Law itself distinguished the two statuses into **separate provisions** dismantles the equation "karet = automatic damnation to hell" at the level of the original language's legal structure.

> 📌 **Reusability Point**: When the opponent quotes a provision by lumping it together, first check **whether the code of law itself has separated the provisions**.

## 5. Grade Cap — The most important record in this precedent

Even though the exhaustive search secured STRONG-level circumstantial evidence, **it did not ascend to IRONCLAD.** The reason is the core asset of this precedent.

```
[TYPE-AC Null Hypothesis Substitution]
  ¬P = Substitute "Saul went to a place of torment" into the KJV text
  → Result: No decisive internal contradiction found
  → Namely, the opposing interpretation does not self-destruct within the text
  → Does not meet the definition of IRONCLAD (= Established only if alternatives die)
```

**Cross-Witness Verification also failed**: The sample of 217 cases is large, but **the verse it applies to is only one: 1 Sam 28:19**. The cross-witness requirement of two or more different books/authors is not secured (`ANCHOR_ThirdData.md` cross-witness independence verification).

**Actual thought record**: The original §6 ignored this gateway and attempted a re-upgrade to IRONCLAD based on the "217 cases exhaustive search", but it was invalidated in the §7 re-correction. This was because it merely raised the grade without new evidence (Violation #1 of OVERRIDE-2).

> 🚨 **Reusability Point — The conclusion of this precedent**
> **Exhaustive search is a tool to secure an anchor, not a tool to raise the grade.**
> No matter how large the sample is (217 cases), if ⓐ the null hypothesis does not die and ⓑ there are no cross-witnesses, IRONCLAD is impossible.
