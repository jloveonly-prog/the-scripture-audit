## 1. Overall Structure of Zechariah Chapter 5 — Two Visions

### Vision 1: The Flying Roll (Zech 5:1-4)

> **Zech 5:1-2 KJV** — *"Then I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a **flying roll**. ...I see a flying roll; the **length** thereof is **twenty cubits**, and the **breadth** thereof **ten cubits**."*

> **Zech 5:3 KJV** — *"This is the **curse** that goeth forth over **the face of the whole earth**: for every one that **stealeth** shall be cut off as on **this side** according to it; and every one that **sweareth** shall be cut off as on **that side** according to it."*

> **Zech 5:4 KJV** — *"I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall **enter into the house** of the thief, and into the house of him that **sweareth falsely** by my name: and it shall **remain** in the midst of his house, and shall **consume** it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof."*

| Item | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **Object** | The flying roll (מְגִלָּה megillah) — flying in an unrolled state |
| **Size** | Length 20 cubits (approx. 9.1m) × breadth 10 cubits (approx. 4.6m) |
| **Identity** | The **curse (אָלָה alah)** that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth |
| **Target** | Every one that stealeth + every one that sweareth falsely |
| **Two-sided Writing** | "this side" and "that side" → the curse is written on both sides of the roll |
| **Action** | It shall **enter into the house** of the transgressor and shall **consume** it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof |

#### Physical Form of the Roll

```
        Direction of flight →

     ┌───────────┐
     │           │
     │           │  ← Breadth 10 cubits (4.6m) = width of the roll (front)
     │           │
     │           │
     │  Length   │
     │           │
     │ 20 cubits │
     │  (9.1m)   │
     │           │
     │           │
     └───────────┘
          ↑
    Direction it is unrolled
```

- Breadth 10 cubits = the fixed width of the roll → becomes the front as it flies
- Length 20 cubits = the distance the roll is unrolled backwards
- The curse is written on both sides → it can be read from below and from above

### Turning Point of the Vision (Zech 5:5) — Why the Angel Went Forth out of the Sanctuary

> **Zech 5:5 KJV** — *"Then the angel that talked with me **went forth**(יָצָא, yatsa), and said unto me, Lift up now thine eyes, and see what is this that goeth forth."*

- The angel **"went forth"** — Came out from within somewhere
- During the vision of the roll (v1-4), they were conversing **inside the temple (sanctuary)**
- In v5, he went forth and showed the vision of the ephah
- Transition from **inside (temple/sanctuary) → outside**

#### ✅ The Reason the Angel Went Forth — Wickedness Cannot Be Shown Inside the Holy Sanctuary

> 💡 **[User Narrative Note: Holiness of the Sanctuary]**
> 
> *"The reason the angel went forth is because it could not be shown inside the sanctuary."*

| Vision | Content | Place Where It Can Be Shown |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| **Vision 1 (v1-4)** | **Judgment (curse) based on God's holy law** — Roll the size of the sanctuary (20×10 cubits) | ✅ **Inside the sanctuary** — Because the standard of judgment cometh forth from the holy law |
| **Vision 2 (v5-11)** | **Wickedness** spread over the whole earth — Borne by those having the wings of an unclean fowl | ❌ **Outside the sanctuary** — Wickedness and uncleanness cannot be shown inside the holy sanctuary |

> **Hab 1:13** — *"Thou art of purer eyes than to **behold evil**, and canst not look on iniquity"*

> **Lev 16:2 KJV** — *"Speak unto Aaron thy brother, that he **come not at all times into the holy place** within the vail... **that he die not**: for I will appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat."*

> **Ezek 44:9 KJV** — *"**No stranger, uncircumcised in heart, nor uncircumcised in flesh, shall enter into my sanctuary**"*
>
> Hab 1:13 (cannot behold evil) + Lev 16:2 (restricted access to sanctuary — death upon violation) + Ezek 44:9 (unclean forbidden from entering sanctuary) → The principle firmly established by these three anchors together: In God's sanctuary, that which is unclean **can neither exist nor be shown.** The holy judgment (the roll) was shown inside the sanctuary, but to show wickedness (the ephah), he naturally **had to depart from the holy sanctuary and go forth outside.**

> 💡 **[User Narrative Note: Church → World → Spiritual Logistics of Babylon]**
> 
> *"This is right. Corrupted in the church, and going forth from the church into the world to be sent to Babylon, this order."*
> 
> *"The woman is the bride.. but totally corrupted. Is she not the one that sweareth falsely by my name? Thou art speaking of the false churches of today."*
>
> **"sweareth falsely by my name"** — To swear by the name of God is an act that only one **in a covenant relationship** with God can perform. This points to one who was originally the **bride (church)** but became completely **corrupted** = **false churches**. It perfectly matches the pattern of Hos 1:2, "a wife of whoredoms." Corruption originating inside the sanctuary (church) → Expulsion outside the sanctuary (world) → Sent to Shinar (Babylon) — this is the complete order of spiritual logistics shown in this vision.

### Vision 2: The Woman in the Ephah (Zech 5:5-11)

> **Zech 5:6 KJV** — *"This is **an ephah** that goeth forth. ...This is **their resemblance through all the earth**."*

> **Zech 5:7 KJV** — *"And, behold, there was lifted up a **talent of lead**: and this is **a woman** that sitteth in the midst of the ephah."*

> **Zech 5:8 KJV** — *"And he said, **This is wickedness.** And he **cast it** into the midst of the ephah; and he **cast the weight of lead upon the mouth** thereof."*

> **Zech 5:9 KJV** — *"Then lifted I up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came out **two women**, and the **wind**(רוּחַ, ruach) was in their **wings**; for they had wings like the wings of a **stork**: and they lifted up the ephah **between the earth and the heaven**."*

> **Zech 5:10-11 KJV** — *"Whither do these bear the ephah? ...To build it an **house** in **the land of Shinar**: and it shall be established, and set there upon **her own base**."*

| Item | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **Ephah (אֵיפָה)** | A commercial measuring basket — it goeth forth |
| **The woman in the ephah** | "Wickedness (רִשְׁעָה rishah)" — she sitteth in the midst of the ephah |
| **A talent of lead** | Approx. **34kg** — The lid sealing the mouth of the ephah |
| **Two women** | Having wings of a stork (חֲסִידָה chasidah — unclean fowl, Lev 11:19) |
| **ruach (רוּחַ spirit/wind)** | The wind/spirit was in their wings |
| **Transport** | They lifted up the ephah **between the earth and the heaven** |
| **Destination** | **Shinar = Babylon** — To build it an house and set it there |

> **Original Language Note:** רִשְׁעָה (rishah, wickedness) is a **feminine noun** in Hebrew. Personifying "wickedness" as a "woman" is not a mere metaphor but a natural connection in Hebrew grammar itself.

#### ✅ The Angel's Rough Suppression — The Woman's Attempted Escape and Forced Sealing

```
[1] The leaden lid is lifted up (v7 "lifted up")

    └── She attempteth escape or raiseth herself up through the opening of the lifted lid

[3] The angel immediately suppresseth her by force (v8 "he cast it into the midst")
    └── Defineth her as "This is wickedness" → roughly casteth her down into the midst of the ephah

[4] Complete sealing with a 34kg weight of lead (v8 "he cast the weight of lead upon the mouth")
    └── Casteth the heaviest metal to cover the mouth of the ephah
    └── Crusheth and confineth her without mercy so she can never escape again

> The reason the angel dealeth with this woman so roughly and resolutely: Because this wickedness originally stood on God's side and became corrupted, God's holy wrath and hatred are even more intense. This is directly connected to the **"sweareth falsely"** of the first vision — one who originally swore by God's name (= stood on God's side) swore **falsely**, that is, became corrupted.

```
