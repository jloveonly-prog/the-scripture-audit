## 1. ⚔️ The Question Raised

> **"Could the devils not simply have gone out? Why did they specifically have to enter the swine?"**

On the surface, this is a question the text does not answer. Mark 5:12 records only the **content** of what they requested, not the **reason.**

Existing weapons stop here — TYPE-G (grammar) analyzes syntax but does not address motive, and TYPE-AR (spiritual mechanism) classifies behavioral patterns but does not ask "why." **A zone of motive-silence was a blind spot for BVCAP.**

## 2. 🛡️ Sequential Deployment of Four Types

### ① TYPE-AV — Statement Analysis: What Was Leaked Without Being Asked

The only thing Jesus asked was **a single name** (Mark 5:9, *"What is thy name?"*).

| Content of the Answer | Was It Requested by the Question? | Verse |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| "My name is Legion" | ✅ | Mark 5:9 |
| "for we are many" | ❌ | Mark 5:9 |
| "torment us **before the time**" | ❌ | Matt. 8:29 |
| "send us not away **out of the country**" | ❌ | Mark 5:10 |
| "not command them to go out **into the deep**" | ❌ | Luke 8:31 |

**Four instances of over-disclosure.** Though asked for a name, they answered with a scale rather than an individual designation, and continued exposing information that had not been requested.

**Exposed Premises → KJV Cross-Verification**
- "There is an appointed time" → 2 Pet. 2:4, *"reserved unto judgment"* + Jude 1:6, *"reserved… unto the judgment of the great day"* → **confirmed by 2 authors ✅ anchor**
- "The bottomless pit is a real place" → Rev. 20:1-3, *"bottomless pit"* → **confirmed ✅**

### ② TYPE-AY — Demand Tier: What Was Held to the Very End

| Tier | Demand | Verse | Author |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Maximum demand | Torment us not | Mark 5:7 / Matt. 8:29 | Mark, Matthew |
| Intermediate demand | Send us not out of this country | Mark 5:10 | Mark |
| **Minimum line** | Command us not to go out into the deep | Luke 8:31 | Luke |
| **Voluntary alternative** | Send us into the swine | Mark 5:12 | Common to all 3 authors |

**Examination of the Unmentioned Option ★ The Core Discovery of This Case**

Matthew 12:43 explicitly specifies a third option — *"When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he **walketh through dry places, seeking rest, and findeth none**."*

**Wandering without a host is possible.** Only that "he findeth no rest."

| Option | Textual Basis | The Legion's Response |
|:---|:---|:---|
| The bottomless pit | Luke 8:31 | Explicitly refused |
| Wandering without a host | Matt. 12:43 | **Not even brought to the negotiating table** |
| A new host | Mark 5:12 | Voluntarily requested |

> Here is the answer to the initial question ("could they not simply have gone out?"). **They could have gone out.** Yet they did not even mention that option. The refusal of the bottomless pit and the non-mention of wandering point in **the same direction** — an avoidance of the bodiless state itself.

### ③ TYPE-AW — Action-Statement Discrepancy: Where Speech and Action Diverged

| Point in Time | Speech | Action | Verse |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| T1 | "Give us a host" | | Mark 5:12 |
| T2 | | Enters the herd of swine | Mark 5:13 |
| T3 | | **About 2,000 head instantly drowned** | Mark 5:13 |

💥 **Discrepancy.** If securing a host was the goal, the total destruction within minutes is an immediate self-destruction of their own purpose.

**Exhaustive Competing Explanations**
- (a) Loss of control — the scale of the Legion overwhelmed the herd of swine
- (b) Destruction was the goal — the host was merely the means
- (c) A change in circumstance — **no textual basis → rejected**

→ Neither (a) nor (b) dies from an internal textual contradiction. **Transferred to TYPE-AX.**

### ④ TYPE-AX — Cui Bono: Whose Demand Does the Outcome Match?

**The Chain of Outcomes (traced within the text alone)**

| Stage | Outcome | Verse |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| ① | About 2,000 head of swine drowned | Mark 5:13 |
| ② | Those who fed them tell it in the city and in the country | Mark 5:14 |
| ③ | The people come out to see, and are afraid | Mark 5:15 |
| ④ | **They beseech him to depart out of their coasts** | Mark 5:17 |
| ⑤ | Jesus boards the ship | Mark 5:18 |

**Comparison**

> The Legion's demand ②: *"send us not away out of the country"* (Mark 5:10)
> The actual final outcome: **Jesus departs from that country** (Mark 5:18)

Of the two parties, it is not the devils but Jesus who departs from that country.

**Examination of Competing Beneficiaries (an item that must not be omitted)**

| Candidate | Outcome | Ruling |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| The owners/keepers of the swine | Economic loss | ❌ |
| The inhabitants of the city | Fell into fear | ❌ |
| The healed man | Made whole (Mark 5:15) — but not gained through the destruction of the swine | ❌ |
| **The Legion** | Remains in the region + the threat of expulsion removed | ✅ **The sole beneficiary** |

## 3. 🎯 Forensic Lesson

### Lesson ① — The Four Types Operate in a Sequential Handoff Structure

```
TYPE-AV (what was leaked)  →  TYPE-AY (what was held to the end)
                                   ↓ hands off the demand list
TYPE-AW (did speech and action diverge?) →  TYPE-AX (does the outcome match whose demand?)
```

When TYPE-AW stalled without narrowing the competing hypotheses (a) and (b), **TYPE-AX resolved that deadlock through outcome-consistency.** Neither one alone could have reached this point.

### Lesson ② — An Unmentioned Option Can Sometimes Be Stronger Evidence Than the Demand List

The greatest discovery of this case was not **what they demanded** (the swine), but **what they did not even mention** (wandering). Had TYPE-AY STEP 3 been omitted, the analysis would have been trapped in a two-option choice of "bottomless pit vs. swine," missing the actual priority (avoiding the bodiless state itself).

> **Generalization**: when handling a record of negotiation, looking only at "what was demanded" is seeing only half the picture. Options actually recorded elsewhere in Scripture that were **not brought to the table** must always be counted as well.

### Lesson ③ — A Reverse Calculation Cannot Confirm Motive ★ A Grade Ceiling

| What Is Confirmed | Grade |
|:---|:---:|
| The chain of outcomes (Mark 5:13→14→15→17→18) | **EXPLICIT** — a continuous textual record |
| The consistency between the final outcome and demand ② | **EXPLICIT** — a comparative result |
| The Legion as the sole beneficiary | **STRONG** — all 3 competing candidates rejected |
| **"Destruction was the motive from the start"** | **Cannot be confirmed** |

The last line is the core of this precedent. **The text does not narrate motive, and TYPE-AW's competing hypothesis (a), loss of control, does not die from an internal contradiction.** The grade ceiling confirmed in the King Saul precedent (`[N+AC+AG]_Saul_Salvation_Propositional_Anchor_217_Instances.md` STEP 4-P Gate ③) applies here as well without exception.

> **"It benefited, therefore it was planned" is a logical leap.** Outcome-consistency and prior intent are separate propositions, and the latter requires separate evidence. The maximum these four weapons can reach is **the outline of a motive**, not the motive itself.

### Lesson ④ — Psychology Is a Lens, Not Evidence

In this case, the concepts of negotiation psychology and statement psychology **helped recognize the pattern**, but were not used even once as grounds for a verdict. The grounds for every item are the literal words of the KJV.

Substituting a human psychological model onto a spiritual being to confirm inner motive violates OVERRIDE-1 Rule 2 (prohibition of metaphorical interpretation) and TYPE-C (functional category separation) (`01_MANDATE/IDENTITY_Scribe42.md`, the Base Layer boundary).
