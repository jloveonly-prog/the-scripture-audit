### 🏹 TYPE-G: Original Text Grammar Anatomy — ὅπου is an adverb of place

> **John 13:36 (KJV):** *"Whither I go, thou canst not follow me now; but thou shalt follow me afterwards."*

| Greek | Original Meaning | Grammatical Function |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **ὅπου** (hopou) | "where, whither" | **Adverb of space/place** — Not "how (πῶς)" |
| **ὑπάγω** (hupagō) | "to go away, to depart" | Verb of movement — presupposes movement to a physical place |

> **Verdict:** If Jesus had meant only a method or spiritual destination, He would have used `πῶς (how)` or `ᾗ ὁδῷ (by what way)`. Since He used `ὅπου`, **a locational meaning is primarily implied**.

## 📖 [Appendix: Apologetic Creative Work] Calvary – The Moment Prophecy Closes

*(This story is a fiction reconstructing Peter's final moments based on the biblical typology above.)*

---

### 1. The Shadow of Calvary

The wind sprinkled the smell of blood and dust onto my face.
The shadow of the cross cast over the dry, red dirt was stretching long.
That shadow felt as though death was already covering my body.

My hands and feet were gradually losing sensation, and with every last breath I took, the ends of my lungs stung as if tearing.
Then, the words I had spoken to the Lord long ago came alive in my ears.

> *"Lord, why cannot I follow thee now? I will lay down my life for thy sake."*

I held a tremor on my lips, not knowing if it was a smile or pain.
As my eyelids lowered, the world sank into darkness like submerging in water.
Then, as if time rewound, the shores of Galilee from 40 years ago revived before my eyes.

---

### 2. The First Encounter – The Secret of the Name Jonah

Back then, I was just Simon.
A hasty and fiery fisherman, known by people for having a 'fiery temper'.

Andrew came running, panting.

> "We have found the Messias."
> "Come and see."

He looked at me for a long time.
It was a deep gaze that pierced the inside before the outside.
It was a fearful stare, as if He had already read my entire life.

And He spoke.

> "Thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas."

It was strange. He did not say my father's name. Was He speaking of the prophet Jonah who went into the belly of the fish?

I muttered inwardly.

*'Is there a need to say my father's name that everyone in town knows? Andrew must have misheard... Could this man really be the Messiah?'*
*A thought crossed my mind, 'If He just shows a miracle, I would believe.'*

However, I did not know then that the words 'son of the prophet Jonah' contained a mission and prophetic structure that would run through my entire life.

---

### 3. Mother-in-Law's Fever – Shaken

A few days later, my mother-in-law suffered from a severe fever and reached the brink of death.
The house was filled with wailing and screams, and I could do nothing but wipe my sweat-drenched palms.

Then, He was led by the people from the synagogue and entered our house.

> "Fever, leave."

Instantly, my mother-in-law's eyes opened, her hot breath returned to normal, and she got up.

I was frozen.

Fear, awe, and a very small spark of faith emerged.
But it was not yet... a decision.

*'Is this man really the Messiah?'*

---

### 4. The Miracle of the Fish – Complete Collapse

That day was despondent.
We let down our nets all night but caught nothing.

Around sunrise, He quietly boarded my boat.

> "Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught."

I muttered inwardly.

*'There's no way we'll catch fish~'*

But His eyes were deeper and firmer than the sea.

As soon as we let down the net—
Fish poured in, filling the boat to the point of sinking.

I was so astonished that I fell down and said,

> "Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord."

Those words were a confession of fear.

He spoke.

> "Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men."

Thus, I became His disciple.

---

### 5. Episodes — Keys, Sea, Tabernacle

**The Story of the Keys**

Among the disciples, I was the first to confess Him.

> "Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God."

He spoke.

> "Thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.
> And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven."

Those words were not merely words.
Through me, the path of the gospel was opening.

**The Story of the Sea**

I saw the Lord in the storm,
and walking on the water toward Him, I fell into the water from the fear of the storm.
Just as Jonah was cast into the sea in the storm and feared.

**The Story of the Tabernacles**

I recalled the glory on the Mount of Transfiguration.
Even in that moment when I offered to build three tabernacles without knowing what I was saying,
I was already within His plan. In my human longing to stay with Him,
I was prophesying the completion of the eternal tabernacle.

---

### 6. Betrayal – The Climax of Human Weakness

On the night He was taken, I was trembling in the darkness.

> "Thou also wast with Jesus of Galilee?"

I denied Him three times.
My voice cracking, the sound of my conscience tearing was vivid.

And the cock crew.
I ran away. Guilt threw me deep into the night.

---

### 7. Restoration – Rising Again

The morning in Galilee after the resurrection.
A campfire was burning.

He looked at me and asked.

> "Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me more than these?"

Three times.
As many times as I had denied Jesus.

There was no rebuke in His eyes.
Only love and mission.

> "Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee."

He accepted my heart and healed the bitterness of my betrayal of Him.

> "Feed my sheep."

---

### 8. Pentecost — The Era of the Holy Spirit

Cloven tongues like as of fire sat upon me, and I was filled with the Holy Ghost.
The Holy Spirit also descended in a bodily shape like a dove, and the word 'Jonah' meant 'dove' in Hebrew.
That day, the heart of the church began to beat, and just as Jonah saved the Gentiles in Nineveh,
I preached the gospel in the house of the Gentile Cornelius, and the Holy Spirit fell on the Gentiles for the first time.

---

### 9. Jonah and Peter – The Hidden Parallels

"Lord, why did You call me 'son of Jonah' from the beginning?"
Then, the thoughts began to unlock one by one.

Jonah disobeyed God's command, boarded a ship, and fled. I also denied Jesus and fled.
Jonah fell asleep in the ship during the storm, and I also fell asleep in the Garden of Gethsemane.
Jonah had the sailors cast lots, and I also had lots cast for the apostle Matthias.
Jonah fell into the sea, and I also fell into the sea while walking on the water.
Jonah was in the belly for three days and nights, and I denied the Lord three times and confessed Him three times.

I can't believe it. I just realized everything.
My entire life was prophesied as a life like Jonah's!

---

### 10. Dying at Calvary

I was so startled that my closed eyes flew open.
It was the moment all the puzzle pieces of prophecies penetrating my life finally fit together.

"I... was Jonah," I whispered, exhaling a trembling breath.

> "Lord, how dare I... be dying in the same manner on this hill of Calvary where You bled, the very spot where Your cross stood."

Then, the voice I heard long ago sent ripples deep into my soul.

> *"Whither I go, thou canst not follow me now; but thou shalt follow me afterwards."*

I had not properly understood those words back then.
It was a prophecy of the day when I would follow even the place and manner in which He went.
The place I most wanted to avoid was Jerusalem, where the memory of my sin of denying the Lord was vivid, yet I finally arrived at this hill of Golgotha where the Lord died.

> "Beloved Lord... The prophecy You spoke to me... has been fulfilled today on this hill of Calvary, glorifying God."

I took my last breath with a trembling heart.

> "Lord Jesus... receive my spirit."

Right before the world melted into darkness, I heard the Lord's final voice.

> *"Simon, son of Jona, from the day I first called you, I loved you. Well done, Peter. You have finally followed me to this place."*

All darkness vanished. I walk into the embrace of light. To the place where He is waiting...
