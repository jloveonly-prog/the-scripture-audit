# COL-009 — Absolute Submission to the Pope Required (Unam Sanctam) vs Salvation for Those Who Do Not Know the Gospel (LG 16)

- **Document A**: UNAM-SANCTAM (Boniface VIII, 1302, De Fide Definita) — "Outside this Church, which is one and holy catholic and apostolic, there is neither salvation nor remission of sins... we declare, state, define, and pronounce that it is absolutely necessary (omnino) for every human creature to be subject to the Roman Pontiff for salvation."
- **Document B**: LG-16 (Second Vatican Council, Lumen Gentium 16, 1964) — "Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart, and, moved by grace, try in their actions to do his will as they know it through the dictates of their conscience... can attain eternal salvation. The plan of salvation also includes those who acknowledge the Creator, among these especially Muslims."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 5 (De Fide doctrinal definition vs. conciliar dogmatic constitution, direct contradiction)
- **Detection Path**: Automated embedding detection (similarity 0.906) → LLM secondary review: YES → manual source-text comparison confirmed (2026-07-08)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: Unam Sanctam and LG 16 are simultaneously true
  - **Data**: Unam Sanctam uses the formula of definition (definimus) to declare as De Fide that "submission to the pope = absolutely necessary for the salvation of every human." LG 16 teaches that even those who do not know the Gospel of Christ itself — let alone the pope — (including Muslims) can attain salvation. Muslims, by definition, are not subject to the Roman Pontiff.
  - **Contradiction**: If submission to the pope is absolutely (omnino) necessary, a Muslim who does not submit cannot attain salvation. Yet LG 16 says they can. P ∧ ¬P
  - **Conclusion**: The two propositions cannot both be true simultaneously. One must be false — yet Document A is a document declared infallible (incapable of error).
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-01 (Theological development), CE-10 (Difference in theological rank), CE-02 (Magisterium's interpretive authority)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-01: Development is A → A+. "Absolutely (omnino) no salvation without submission" → "salvation is possible without even knowing the Gospel, let alone submitting" is not an expansion but a reversal. The universal adverb 'omnino' itself foreclosed any room for exception.
  - Rebuttal to CE-10: Unam Sanctam is a De Fide document bearing the formula of definition. If a lower-ranked document (the pastoral language of a dogmatic constitution) substantively nullifies a higher De Fide definition, the ranking system itself admits to being "a device for circumventing an inconvenient infallible declaration."
  - Rebuttal to CE-02: That the Magisterium, which holds interpretive authority, taught opposite content in 1302 and 1964 is precisely the data of this card. Appealing to interpretive authority does not resolve the problem — it merely restates it.
