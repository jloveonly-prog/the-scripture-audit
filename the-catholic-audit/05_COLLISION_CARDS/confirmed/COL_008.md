# COL-008 — The Circular Logic of Ex Cathedra (The Self-Referential Fallacy of Infallibility)

- **Document A**: VAT1-PASTOR-AETERNUS — "The Roman Pontiff, when he speaks ex cathedra... is possessed of that infallibility. This dogma of infallibility is itself an ex cathedra declaration (First Vatican Council, Pastor Aeternus, 1870)."
- **Document B**: Logic — "A self-referential proposition cannot be independently verified and constitutes the fallacy of circular reasoning (petitio principii)."
- **Collision Type**: Circular Reasoning / Petitio Principii
- **Collision Grade**: Level 4 (Logical Structural Flaw)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: Ex cathedra declarations are infallible.
  - **Data**: The proposition "Ex cathedra declarations are infallible" is itself an ex cathedra declaration. Thus, to verify the truth value of this proposition, one must first know that ex cathedra declarations are infallible. Yet, it is this very ex cathedra declaration that tells us ex cathedra declarations are infallible.
  - **Contradiction**: To prove the truth of A, one uses A. In logic, this is petitio principii.
    - Circular Structure: "The Pope is infallible" → Reason: "The First Vatican Council declared it" → "Why is the declaration of the First Vatican Council true?" → "Because the Pope approved it ex cathedra" → "Why is the Pope's ex cathedra true?" → "Because the First Vatican Council declared it" → ∞ loop.
  - **Conclusion**: The infallibility of ex cathedra possesses no independent foundation outside itself. A self-referential system cannot prove its own consistency (cf. Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems).
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-15 (Foundation by Sacred Tradition), CE-16 (Biblical foundation in Matthew 16:18).
- **Containment**:
  - Refutation of CE-15: The claim that "Sacred Tradition is the foundation" — yet it is the Magisterium that determines the validity of Tradition. The authority of the Magisterium is founded upon Tradition, and the authority to interpret Tradition rests with the Magisterium. This merely expands the loop by one step; it does not escape it.
  - Refutation of CE-16: The claim that "Matthew 16:18 is the foundation" — yet to assert that Matthew 16:18 signifies papal primacy is itself an interpretation of the Magisterium. Scripture → Magisterium's interpretation → Magisterium's authority → Authority to interpret Scripture → ∞ loop. Moreover, the Eastern Church interprets the very same verse quite differently.
- **Additional Note**: This circular logic becomes even more formidable when combined with COL-003 (Papal Infallibility vs. Honorius). When evading the heresy of Honorius by claiming "it was not ex cathedra," who determines whether it was ex cathedra? The Pope himself or the Magisterium. This brings us straight back into the loop.
