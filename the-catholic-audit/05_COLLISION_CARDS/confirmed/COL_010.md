# COL-010 — Even Martyrs Go to Hell Without Exception (Florence) vs Those Who Do Not Know Can Be Saved (CCC 846-848)

- **Document A**: COUNCIL-FLORENCE (Council of Florence, bull Cantate Domino, 1442, De Fide Definita) — "No one remaining outside the Catholic Church, not just pagans, but also Jews, heretics, and schismatics, can ever be partakers of eternal life... unless before the end of life they are joined to the Catholic Church. **Even if they shed their blood in the name of Christ [martyrdom]**, they cannot be saved unless they remain within the Catholic Church."
- **Document B**: CCC-846_848 (Catechism of the Catholic Church, paragraphs 846-848, 1992) — "Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church... but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart... can achieve eternal salvation." It also explicitly rejects the proposition that "anyone not belonging to the Catholic Church unconditionally goes to hell (rigorism)."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 4 (De Fide conciliar bull vs. modern catechism, direct contradiction)
- **Detection Path**: Automated embedding detection (similarity 0.848) → LLM secondary review: YES → manual source-text comparison confirmed (2026-07-08)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: Cantate Domino and CCC 846-848 are simultaneously true
  - **Data**: Florence states "without exception" — specifying that not even the extreme act of faith that is martyrdom qualifies as an exception, thereby foreclosing any exception category such as ignorance, good faith, or conscience. CCC 846-848 recognizes precisely that exception category (inculpable ignorance) and condemns the no-exception interpretation under the name "rigorism."
  - **Contradiction**: "There is no exception" (A) and "there is an exception" (B) cannot both be true simultaneously. Furthermore, B classifies A's very position as an error (rigorism) — an infallible document has been reclassified as an error by a later Magisterial document.
  - **Conclusion**: P ∧ ¬P. One must be false — yet Document A is a document declared infallible (incapable of error).
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-01 (Theological development), CE-03 (Retroactive tradition — "the ignorance exception existed all along"), CE-05 (Flight into mystery)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-01: The shift from "no exception, not even for martyrs" to "salvation possible for those who do not know" is not a refinement of scope but a reversal of judgment. The 1442 infallible document specifically mentioned martyrs precisely to close the door on every exception that might later be opened.
  - Rebuttal to CE-03: If the exception of "inculpable ignorance" were apostolic tradition, why did the Council of Florence not know of it? If an infallible council omitted an apostolic tradition, infallibility is broken; if no such tradition existed, then the CCC invented it — a dilemma (L-04).
  - Rebuttal to CE-05: The collision between "no exception" and "an exception exists" is elementary logic, not mystery. Abandoning reason is abandoning apologetics.
