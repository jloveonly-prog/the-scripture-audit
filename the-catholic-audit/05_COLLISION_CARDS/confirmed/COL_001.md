# COL-001 — The Necessity of Baptism vs The Possibility of Muslim Salvation

- **Document A**: CCC_1257 — "Baptism is necessary for salvation... The Lord Himself affirms that Baptism is necessary for salvation (John 3:5). The Church does not know of any means other than Baptism that assures entry into eternal beatitude."
- **Document B**: LG_16 — "But the plan of salvation also includes those who acknowledge the Creator. In the first place amongst these there are the Muslims, who, professing to hold the faith of Abraham, along with us adore the one and merciful God."
- **Type of Collision**: A → Not A
- **Grade of Collision**: Level 5 (Dogma vs Dogma, Direct Contradiction)
- **Reductio ad absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: CCC 1257 and LG 16 are both true at once.
  - **Data**: CCC 1257 declareth that "salvation cannot be assured without Baptism." LG 16 declareth that "the Muslims are included in the plan of salvation." Muslims, by definition, receive not Christian Baptism.
  - **Contradiction**: If Baptism be necessary for salvation, a Muslim without Baptism cannot be included in the plan of salvation. Yet LG 16 saith they are included. P ∧ ¬P
  - **Conclusion**: Both propositions cannot be true at once. One must be false.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasions**: CE-01 (Extension of the Baptism of Desire), CE-02 (Plan of salvation ≠ Assurance of salvation)
- **Blockade**:
  - Refutation of CE-01: They argue that the Baptism of Desire may be applied to them that know not Christ explicitly; but CCC 1257 clearly stateth that "the Church knoweth not of any means other than Baptism." Furthermore, Muslims belong to a religious system that explicitly rejecteth Christ.
  - Refutation of CE-02: They argue that "being included in the plan of salvation" differs from the "assurance of salvation"; but if one is included in the plan yet salvation is impossible, such inclusion is vain, and the context of LG 16 manifestly implieth the possibility of salvation.
