# COL-004 — No Salvation Outside the Church vs Salvation Outside the Church Is Possible

- **Document A**: COUNCIL-LATERAN_IV / UNAM-SANCTAM — "Outside the Church there is neither salvation nor remission of sins (Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus)." Boniface VIII's Unam Sanctam (1302): "We declare, state, define, and pronounce that it is absolutely necessary for the salvation of every human creature to be subject to the Roman Pontiff." This is a De Fide doctrine also affirmed at the Fourth Lateran Council (1215).
- **Document B**: CCC-0847 — "Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart, and, moved by grace, try in their actions to do his will as they know it through the dictates of their conscience... they too may achieve eternal salvation."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 5 (Papal bull + conciliar doctrine vs. modern catechism, direct contradiction)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: "No salvation outside the Church" (Lateran IV, Unam Sanctam) and CCC 847 are simultaneously true
  - **Data**: Unam Sanctam declares as De Fide that "subjection to the Roman Pontiff is necessary for the salvation of every human creature." CCC 847 declares that "even those who do not know the Church can be saved."
  - **Contradiction**: If subjection to the pope is "necessary for every human," then a person who does not know the Church (and thus is not subject to the pope) cannot be saved. Yet CCC 847 says they can. P ∧ ¬P
  - **Conclusion**: An absolute necessary condition and a permitted exception are logically incompatible.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-07 (Development of doctrine), CE-08 (Invincible ignorance exception)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-07: It is Catholicism's own principle (the Vincentian Canon) that the "development" of doctrine cannot contradict prior doctrine. The shift from "no salvation outside the Church" to "salvation is possible outside the Church" is not development but negation.
  - Rebuttal to CE-08: Unam Sanctam says "every human creature" (omni humanae creaturae). There is no exception clause here. Creating an exception to "every" is a falsification of the original text.
