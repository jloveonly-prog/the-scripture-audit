# COL-004 — No Salvation Outside the Church vs. Salvation Possible Outside the Church

- **Document A**: COUNCIL-LATERAN_IV / UNAM-SANCTAM — "There is but one universal Church of the faithful, outside of which there is absolutely no salvation." Boniface VIII in Unam Sanctam (1302): "Furthermore, we declare, we proclaim, we define that it is absolutely necessary for salvation that every human creature be subject to the Roman Pontiff." This De Fide dogma was also affirmed by the Fourth Lateran Council (1215).
- **Document B**: CCC-0847 — "Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart, and, moved by grace, try in their actions to do his will as they know it through the dictates of their conscience—those too may achieve eternal salvation."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 5 (Papal Bull + Conciliar Dogma vs. Modern Catechism, Direct Contradiction)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: "Outside the Church there is no salvation" (Lateran IV, Unam Sanctam) and CCC 847 are simultaneously true.
  - **Data**: Unam Sanctam declares De Fide that subjection to the Roman Pontiff is "absolutely necessary for salvation that every human creature." CCC 847 declares that one who knows not the Church may achieve salvation.
  - **Contradiction**: If subjection to the Pope is "absolutely necessary for every human creature," then one who knows not the Church (one not subject to the Pope) cannot be saved. Yet CCC 847 says he can be. P ∧ ¬P.
  - **Conclusion**: An absolute necessity and the allowance of an exception are logically incompatible.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-07 (Development of Dogma), CE-08 (Exception of Invincible Ignorance).
- **Containment**:
  - Refutation of CE-07: The Catholic Church's own principle is that dogmatic "development" cannot contradict existing dogma (the Vincentian Canon). To shift from "No salvation outside the Church" to "Salvation is possible outside the Church" is no development, but a negation.
  - Refutation of CE-08: Unam Sanctam speaks of "every human creature" (omni humanae creaturae). There is no clause of exception found therein. To fashion an exception out of "every" is an adulteration of the original text.
