# COL-003 — Papal Infallibility vs the Condemnation of Honorius I as a Heretic

- **Document A**: VAT1-PASTOR-AETERNUS — "When the Roman Pontiff speaks ex cathedra... he possesses, by the divine assistance promised to him, that infallibility with which the divine Redeemer willed his Church to be endowed in defining doctrine concerning faith or morals."
- **Document B**: COUNCIL-CONST_III — "The Third Council of Constantinople (681) officially condemned Pope Honorius I as a heretic for Monothelitism. This condemnation was subsequently reaffirmed by the Fourth Council of Constantinople and the Second Council of Nicaea."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 5 (Doctrine vs. conciliar decision, collision between historical fact and doctrine)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: The pope is infallible in matters of faith and morals (Vatican I)
  - **Data**: Pope Honorius I taught Monothelitism. The Third Council of Constantinople — a council Catholicism itself recognizes as valid — condemned Honorius as a heretic.
  - **Contradiction**: If the pope is infallible, Honorius cannot be a heretic. Yet a council Catholicism recognizes as valid condemned a pope as a heretic. Infallible pope ∧ heretical pope = P ∧ ¬P
  - **Conclusion**: The doctrine of papal infallibility and the condemnation of Honorius as a heretic cannot both be true simultaneously.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-05 (Honorius's statement was not ex cathedra), CE-06 (Condemnation as heretic ≠ Proclamation of heretical doctrine)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-05: "It was not ex cathedra" is a retroactive application. The concept of ex cathedra was not defined until 1870. The council fathers of 681 condemned Honorius simply as pope. This evasion is also completely neutralized when combined with COL-008 (the circular logic of ex cathedra).
  - Rebuttal to CE-06: Honorius's letters contained doctrinal content (teaching concerning the will of Christ) and functioned as doctrinal guidance for the whole Church. If this is not the teaching of doctrine, what is?
