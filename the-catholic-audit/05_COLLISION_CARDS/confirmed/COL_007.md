# COL-007 — Original Sin upon All Men vs. The Exception of Mary

- **Document A**: CCC-0402 — "All men are implicated in Adam's sin... original sin is transmitted to human nature: 'by propagation, not by imitation.'"
- **Document B**: CCC-0491 — "The most Blessed Virgin Mary was, from the first moment of her conception, by a singular grace and privilege of almighty God and by virtue of the merits of Jesus Christ, Savior of the human race, preserved immune from all stain of original sin." (Immaculate Conception)
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A (Exception to a Universal Proposition)
- **Collision Grade**: Level 3 (Dogma vs. Dogma, Logical Tension)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: "Original sin is transmitted to all men" (CCC 402) and "Mary was preserved from original sin" (CCC 491) are simultaneously true.
  - **Data**: CCC 402 proclaims the universality of original sin—"all men." CCC 491 declares Mary as an exception to this universal law.
  - **Contradiction**: If "all" is a true universal quantifier, it admits no exception. ∀x P(x) ∧ ¬P(Mary) = Contradiction.
  - **Conclusion**: "Original sin upon all men" and "one person is an exception" are logically incompatible.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-13 (Exception by singular grace), CE-14 (Interpretation of Romans 3:23).
- **Containment**:
  - Refutation of CE-13: If an "exception by singular grace" is possible, the word "all" loses its meaning. If God could grant an exception to one person, He could grant it to many, in which case the very dogma of the universality of original sin collapses.
  - Refutation of CE-14: To exclude Mary from Romans 3:23, "For all have sinned," the scriptural "all" must also be reinterpreted. This is the tailoring of the plain text of Scripture to fit dogma.
