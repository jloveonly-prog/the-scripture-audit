# COL-007 — Original Sin Applies to All vs Mary Is the Exception

- **Document A**: CCC-0402 — "By his sin, Adam, as the first man, lost the original holiness he had received... all men are implicated in Adam's sin... Original sin is transmitted to all men... it is a sin which will be transmitted by propagation, not by imitation."
- **Document B**: CCC-0491 — "The Most Blessed Virgin Mary was, from the first moment of her conception, by a singular grace and privilege of almighty God and by virtue of the merits of Jesus Christ, Savior of the human race, preserved immune from all stain of original sin (Immaculate Conception)."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A (Exception to a universal proposition)
- **Collision Grade**: Level 3 (Doctrine vs. doctrine, logical tension)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: "Original sin is transmitted to all men" (CCC 402) and "Mary was preserved from original sin" (CCC 491) are simultaneously true
  - **Data**: CCC 402 declares the universality of original sin — "all men." CCC 491 declares Mary an exception to this universal law.
  - **Contradiction**: If "all" (All) is a genuine universal quantifier, it admits no exception. ∀x P(x) ∧ ¬P(Mary) = contradiction.
  - **Conclusion**: "Original sin for all men" and "one person is an exception" are logically incompatible.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-13 (Exception by special grace), CE-14 (Interpretation of Romans 3:23)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-13: If an "exception by special grace" is possible, the word "all" loses its meaning. If God can grant an exception to one person, he can grant it to many, in which case the very doctrine of the universality of original sin collapses.
  - Rebuttal to CE-14: To exclude Mary from Romans 3:23 ("for all have sinned"), Scripture's "all" would also have to be reinterpreted. This is tailoring the clear text of Scripture to fit doctrine.
