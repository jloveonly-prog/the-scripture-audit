# COL-005 — The Absolute Necessity of Priestly Absolution vs. Forgiveness by Perfect Contrition

- **Document A**: TRENT-S14-CONFESSION — "In the sacrament of Penance, the absolution of the priest is a judicial act necessary for the forgiveness of sins. If any one saith, that the sacrament of Penance is not necessary for salvation; let him be anathema."
- **Document B**: CCC-1452 — "When it arises from a love by which God is loved above all else, contrition is called 'perfect' (contrition of charity). Such contrition remits venial sins; it also obtains forgiveness of mortal sins if it includes the firm resolution to have recourse to sacramental confession as soon as possible."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 4 (Decree of Anathema vs. Catechism, Conditional Contradiction)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: Trent S14 and CCC 1452 are simultaneously true.
  - **Data**: Trent declares that the priest's absolution is "necessary" for the forgiveness of sins. CCC 1452 asserts that perfect contrition alone can obtain the forgiveness of mortal sins (prior to the priest's absolution).
  - **Contradiction**: If the priest's absolution is "necessary," then forgiveness without it (by contrition alone) is impossible. Yet CCC 1452 says forgiveness is wrought by contrition alone.
  - **Conclusion**: Being "necessary" and being "possible without it" cannot simultaneously be true.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-09 (The resolution to confess is included), CE-10 (Exception at the hour of death).
- **Containment**:
  - Refutation of CE-09: Even if the condition "if it includes the firm resolution" is present, the actual forgiveness occurs at the moment of contrition (as explicitly stated in CCC 1452). If the mere resolution can stand in the place of the priest's absolution, then the priest's absolution is no longer "necessary," but merely "desirable."
  - Refutation of CE-10: Trent specified no exception at the hour of death. To append a circumstantial exception to an absolute term like "necessary" is to alter the meaning of the original text.
