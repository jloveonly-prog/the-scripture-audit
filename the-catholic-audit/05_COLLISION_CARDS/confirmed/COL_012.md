# COL-012 — No Authority to Bless Same-Sex Couples (2021) vs Pastoral Blessing Permitted (2023)

- **Document A**: CDF-RESPONSUM (Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, response approved by Pope Francis, 2021) — "The Church does not have, and cannot have, the power to bless unions of persons of the same sex. **Because God cannot bless sin.** Therefore any form of blessing for same-sex couples is illicit."
- **Document B**: FIDUCIA-SUPPLICANS (Declaration of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, ratified by Pope Francis, 2023) — "Pastoral blessings may be given to couples in irregular situations, including same-sex couples." It also explicitly rejects the proposition that "every form of blessing for same-sex couples is prohibited."
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 1+ (Direct contradiction between documents from the same body, approved by the same pope — only 2 years apart)
- **Detection Path**: Automated embedding detection (similarity 0.681) → LLM secondary review: YES → manual source-text comparison confirmed (2026-07-09)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Assumption**: The 2021 response and the 2023 declaration are simultaneously true
  - **Data**: A grounded the impossibility of blessing not in circumstance but in **the nature of God** ("God cannot bless sin") — an ontological ground, a type of argument that cannot change with time or pastoral circumstance. B, two years later, permits a blessing for the same subject.
  - **Contradiction**: "No form of blessing is licit" (A) and "a pastoral form of blessing is possible" (B) cannot both be true simultaneously. For B to be true, either (a) God's nature changed within two years, or (b) A's ontological ground was wrong from the start. P ∧ ¬P
  - **Conclusion**: The same Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, under the approval of the same pope, contradicted itself within a two-year interval. The very reliability of Magisterial documents collapses under its own internal data.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-09 (Shield of pastoral consideration — "not a liturgical blessing but a pastoral blessing"), CE-01 (Development), CE-05 (Mystery)
- **Blocking**:
  - Rebuttal to CE-09: A explicitly stated that "any form (any form)" of blessing is illicit — a clause that preemptively foreclosed the very distinction between 'pastoral' and 'liturgical.' The 2023 category of 'pastoral blessing' is a goalpost shift (Filter 5) that reopens, under a new name, the door A had already closed.
  - Rebuttal to CE-01: A judgment grounded in an ontological premise ("God cannot bless sin") is not a candidate for 'development.' If it developed, this means the understanding of God's nature was reversed within two years — and the very body proclaiming that understanding is the Magisterium that itself claims infallibility.
