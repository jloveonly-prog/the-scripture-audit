# COL-012 — Absolutely No Power to Bless Same-Sex Couples (2021) vs. Allowance of Pastoral Blessings (2023)

- **Document A**: CDF-RESPONSUM (Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, Responsum, approved by Pope Francis, 2021) — "The Church does not have, and cannot have, the power to bless unions of persons of the same sex... **For God does not and cannot bless sin.** Therefore, any form of blessing that tends to acknowledge their unions is illicit."
- **Document B**: FIDUCIA-SUPPLICANS (Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, Declaration, approved by Pope Francis, 2023) — "It is possible to bless couples in irregular situations and same-sex couples without officially validating their status or changing in any way the Church's perennial teaching on marriage." It thus explicitly permits a pastoral blessing.
- **Collision Type**: A → Not A
- **Collision Grade**: Level 1+ (Direct Contradiction between documents of the same institution approved by the same Pope—with a time difference of a mere 2 years)
- **Detection Path**: Automatic Embedding Detection (Similarity 0.681) → LLM Secondary Review YES → Manual Textual Verification Confirmed (2026-07-09)
- **Reductio ad Absurdum**:
  - **Premise**: The 2021 Responsum and the 2023 Declaration are simultaneously true.
  - **Data**: A based the impossibility of blessing not upon circumstances, but upon the **nature of God** ("God cannot bless sin")—since this is an ontological foundation, it is a type of argument that cannot change with time or pastoral circumstances. B, two years later, permits blessings for the very same subjects.
  - **Contradiction**: "Any form of blessing is illicit" (A) and "A pastoral form of blessing is possible" (B) cannot simultaneously be true. For B to be true, either (a) the nature of God changed within two years, or (b) the ontological foundation of A was wrong from the beginning. P ∧ ¬P.
  - **Conclusion**: The same Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, under the approval of the same Pope, negated itself within an interval of two years. The reliability of magisterial documents collapses upon its own internal data.
- **Anticipated Catholic Evasion**: CE-09 (The Shield of Pastoral Care — "It is a pastoral blessing, not a liturgical one"), CE-01 (Development), CE-05 (Mystery).
- **Containment**:
  - Refutation of CE-09: A specified that "any form" of blessing is illicit—a phrase that preemptively blocked the very distinction between 'pastoral/liturgical.' The category of 'pastoral blessing' in 2023 is moving the goalposts (Filter 5), opening a door under a different name that A had already shut.
  - Refutation of CE-01: A judgment based on an ontological foundation ("God cannot bless sin") is not a subject of 'development.' If it developed, it means the understanding of the nature of God was overturned in two years, and the subject proclaiming that understanding is the very Magisterium that claims infallibility.
