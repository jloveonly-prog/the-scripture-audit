# TRENT-S22-C01 — The Mass is a true propitiatory sacrifice

| Item | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | TRENT-S22-C01 |
| **Source** | Council of Trent (Concilium Tridentinum) |
| **Article** | Session 22, Decree on the Sacrifice of the Mass, Canon 1 |
| **Dogmatic Grade** | De Fide |
| **Anathema** | ✅ Yes |
| **Year** | 1562 |

## Original Text (Summary)
> "If any one saith, that in the Mass a true and proper sacrifice (verum et proprium sacrificium) is not offered to God; or, that to be offered is nothing else but that Christ is given us to eat; let him be anathema."

## Latin Original
> "Si quis dixerit, in Missa non offerri Deo verum et proprium sacrificium aut quod offerri non sit aliud quam nobis Christum ad manducandum dari: anathema sit."

## Tags
`Sacramental Theology`, `Eucharist`, `Soteriology`, `de_fide`, `necessary`, `forbidden`

## Claims
1. In the Mass, a true and proper sacrifice is offered to God.
2. The offering of the Mass is more than merely giving Christ to us to eat.
3. The Mass is a re-presentation of the sacrifice of the cross and a propitiatory sacrifice.
4. The sacrificial nature of the Mass is a dogma (De Fide).

## Negates
1. The proposition that a true sacrifice is not offered in the Mass.
2. The proposition that the Mass is merely a memorial meal.
3. The proposition that Christ's sacrifice on the cross is unique and cannot be repeated/re-presented in the Mass.
4. The proposition that the Mass hath no propitiatory value.
