# TRENT-S22-C01 — The Mass Is a True Sacrifice of Atonement

| Field | Content |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | TRENT-S22-C01 |
| **Source** | Council of Trent (Concilium Tridentinum) |
| **Reference** | Twenty-Second Session, Decree on the Sacrifice of the Mass, Canon 1 |
| **Doctrinal Rank** | De Fide |
| **Anathema** | ✅ Yes |
| **Year** | 1562 |

## Original Text (Summary)
> "If anyone says that in the Mass a true and proper sacrifice (verum et proprium sacrificium) is not offered to God, or that to be offered means nothing other than that Christ is given to us to eat: let him be anathema."

## Latin Text
> "Si quis dixerit, in Missa non offerri Deo verum et proprium sacrificium aut quod offerri non sit aliud quam nobis Christum ad manducandum dari: anathema sit."

## Tags
`sacramental_theology`, `eucharist`, `soteriology`, `de_fide`, `necessary`, `prohibited`

## Claims
1. In the Mass, a true and proper sacrifice is offered to God
2. The offering of the Mass is more than merely giving Christ to be eaten
3. The Mass is a re-presentation of the sacrifice of the cross and an atoning sacrifice
4. The sacrificial character of the Mass is dogma (De Fide)

## Negates
1. The proposition that no true sacrifice is offered in the Mass
2. The proposition that the Mass is merely a memorial meal
3. The proposition that Christ's sacrifice on the cross is unique and cannot be repeated/re-presented in the Mass
4. The proposition that the Mass has no atoning value
