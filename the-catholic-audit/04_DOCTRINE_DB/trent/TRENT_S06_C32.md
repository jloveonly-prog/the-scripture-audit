# TRENT-S06-C32 — Anathema for Denying That Good Works Are Meritorious

| Field | Content |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | TRENT-S06-C32 |
| **Source** | Council of Trent (Concilium Tridentinum) |
| **Reference** | Sixth Session, Decree on Justification, Canon 32 |
| **Doctrinal Rank** | De Fide |
| **Anathema** | ✅ Yes |
| **Year** | 1547 |

## Original Text (Summary)
> "If anyone says that the good works of the one justified are in such a way the gifts of God that they are not also the good merits of the one justified; or that the one justified, by the good works which he performs through the grace of God... does not truly merit an increase of grace, eternal life, and the attainment of that eternal life itself... as well as an increase of glory: let him be anathema."

## Latin Text
> "Si quis dixerit, hominis iustificati bona opera ita esse dona Dei, ut non sint etiam bona ipsius iustificati merita, aut ipsum iustificatum bonis operibus... non vere mereri augmentum gratiae, vitam aeternam et ipsius vitae aeternae... consecutionem atque etiam gloriae augmentum: anathema sit."

## Tags
`soteriology`, `justification`, `merit`, `grace`, `sanctification`, `de_fide`, `necessary`, `prohibited`

## Claims
1. The good works of the justified are truly meritorious (meritum) before God
2. Through good works, an increase of grace can truly be earned (mereri)
3. Through good works, eternal life can truly be earned
4. Through good works, an increase of glory can truly be earned
5. Merit is the result of a combination of God's grace and human cooperation

## Negates
1. The proposition that the good works of the justified carry no merit
2. The proposition that good works are only a gift from God, never a human merit
3. The proposition that grace or eternal life cannot be increased through human works
4. The proposition that salvation depends entirely on grace alone (an exclusive reading of Sola Gratia)
