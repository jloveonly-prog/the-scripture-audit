# TRENT-S06-C09 — Anathema if one saith justified by faith alone (Sola Fide)

| Item | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | TRENT-S06-C09 |
| **Source** | Council of Trent (Concilium Tridentinum) |
| **Article** | Session 6, Decree on Justification, Canon 9 |
| **Dogmatic Grade** | De Fide |
| **Anathema** | ✅ Yes |
| **Year** | 1547 |

## Original Text (Summary)
> "If any one saith, that by faith alone the impious is justified, in such wise as to mean, that nothing else is required to co-operate in order to the obtaining the grace of Justification, and that it is not in any way necessary, that he be prepared and disposed by the movement of his own will; let him be anathema (anathema sit)."

## Latin Original
> "Si quis dixerit, sola fide impium iustificari, ita ut intelligat nihil aliud requiri, quod ad iustificationis gratiam consequendam cooperetur, et nulla ex parte necesse esse, eum suae voluntatis motu praeparari atque disponi: anathema sit."

## Tags
`Soteriology`, `Justification`, `Grace`, `Merit`, `de_fide`, `necessary`, `forbidden`

## Claims
1. Justification requireth the preparation and disposition (dispositio) of the human will in addition to faith.
2. Human cooperation (cooperatio) is required to obtain the grace of justification.
3. Faith alone is not sufficient for justification — works and sacramental participation are also required.
4. Whosoever denieth this doctrine is anathematized (anathema).

## Negates
1. The proposition that one is justified by faith alone (sola fide).
2. The proposition that no preparation of the human will is needed for justification.
3. The proposition that human cooperation is unnecessary for justification.
4. Luther's doctrine of "faith alone".
