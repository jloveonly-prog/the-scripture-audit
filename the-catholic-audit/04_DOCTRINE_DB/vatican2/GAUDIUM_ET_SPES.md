## Original Text (Summary)
> "The joys and the hopes, the griefs and the anxieties" of the people of this age are the joys and hopes, the griefs and anxieties of the Church as well. Rather than treating the world as an adversary, the Church must stand in solidarity with it and cooperate in advancing human rights, peace, social justice, and human dignity.

## Tags
`the_modern_world`, `social_justice`, `human_rights`

## Claims
1. The Church must not remain in a ghetto separated from secular society, but must actively engage in solidarity with and service to the problems of the modern world.
2. Defending all human life and dignity, human rights, and peace is an important mission of the Church.

## Negates
1. The proposition that the Church should not involve itself in secular political, social, or human rights matters, and should focus solely on the salvation of the spiritual afterlife.
