# LG-16 — Lumen Gentium §16: The Possibility of Salvation for Those Who Do Not Know the Gospel

## Original Text (Summary)
> "Finally, those who have not yet received the Gospel are related in various ways to the People of God. In the first place we must recall the people to whom the testament and the promises were given... But the plan of salvation also includes those who acknowledge the Creator, and among these are the Muslims (Musulmanos), who profess to hold the faith of Abraham, and along with us adore the one and merciful God, who on the last day will judge mankind. Nor is God far distant from those who in shadows and images seek the unknown God... Divine Providence does not deny the helps necessary for salvation to those who, without any fault of theirs, have not yet arrived at an explicit knowledge of God, and who, not without grace, strive to lead a good life."

## Tags
`ecclesiology`, `soteriology`, `baptism`, `grace`, `sententia_certa`, `conditional`

## Claims
1. Those who have not yet received the Gospel are still related to the People of God in various ways
2. Muslims profess to hold the faith of Abraham and adore the one God
3. Muslims are also included in the plan of salvation
4. Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart and try to live rightly according to the dictates of conscience, can attain eternal salvation
5. Divine Providence does not deny such persons the help necessary for salvation

## Negates
1. The strict interpretation that there is no salvation whatsoever outside the visible Church (the exclusive reading of Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus)
2. The proposition that one who has not actually received baptism can never, under any circumstance, be saved
3. The proposition that Muslims are entirely excluded from the plan of salvation
4. The proposition that God gives no opportunity for salvation to those who do not know the Gospel
