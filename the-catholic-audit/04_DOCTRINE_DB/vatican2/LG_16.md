## Original Text (Summary)
> "Finally, those who have not yet received the Gospel are related in various ways to the people of God. In the first place we must recall the people to whom the testament and the promises were given... But the plan of salvation also includes those who acknowledge the Creator. In the first place amongst these there are the Muslims (Musulmanos), who, professing to hold the faith of Abraham, along with us adore the one and merciful God, who on the last day will judge mankind. Nor is God far distant from those... Divine Providence doth not deny the helps necessary for salvation to those who, without blame on their part, have not yet arrived at an explicit knowledge of God and with His grace strive to live a good life."

## Claims
1. They who have not yet received the Gospel are related to the people of God in divers ways.
2. The Muslims profess to hold the faith of Abraham, and adore the one God.
3. The Muslims also are comprehended within the plan of salvation.
4. They who, through no fault of their own, know not the Gospel of Christ nor His Church, yet seek God with a sincere heart, and strive to do His will as it is known to them by the dictates of conscience, may attain everlasting salvation.
5. Divine Providence doth not deny the helps necessary for salvation unto such men.

## Negates
1. The strict interpretation that outside the visible Church there is absolutely no salvation (the exclusive interpretation of Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus).
2. The proposition that he who hath not actually received baptism can in no wise be saved.
3. The proposition that Muslims are entirely excluded from the plan of salvation.
4. The proposition that God giveth no opportunity for salvation to them that know not the Gospel.
