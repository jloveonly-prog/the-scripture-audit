# UNAM-SANCTAM — Submission to the Pope Is Absolutely Necessary for Salvation

## Original Text (Summary)
> "Furthermore, we declare, state, define, and pronounce that it is absolutely necessary for the salvation of every human creature (omnino esse de necessitate salutis) to be subject to the Roman Pontiff."

## Latin Text
> "Porro subesse Romano Pontifici omni humanae creaturae declaramus, dicimus, definimus et pronuntiamus omnino esse de necessitate salutis."

## Tags
`papal_theology`, `ecclesiology`, `soteriology`, `primacy`, `de_fide`, `absolute`, `necessary`

## Claims
1. Submission (subesse) to the Roman Pontiff is absolutely necessary for the salvation of every human creature
2. Outside the one, holy, catholic, and apostolic Church there is neither salvation nor remission of sins (Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus)
3. The Church holds two swords, the spiritual sword (gladius spiritualis) and the temporal sword (gladius temporalis), both belonging to the Church's power
4. Temporal power is subordinate to spiritual power

## Negates
1. The proposition that one can be saved without submission to the pope
2. The proposition that salvation is possible outside the Catholic Church
3. The proposition that temporal power is independent of spiritual power
4. The proposition that the pope's primacy is limited to the spiritual realm alone
