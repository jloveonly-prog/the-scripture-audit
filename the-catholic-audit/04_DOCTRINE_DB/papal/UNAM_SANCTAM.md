# UNAM-SANCTAM — Subjection to the Pope is absolutely necessary for salvation

| Item | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | UNAM-SANCTAM |
| **Source** | Pope Boniface VIII, Bull Unam Sanctam |
| **Article** | Conclusion (Dogmatic Definition) |
| **Dogmatic Grade** | De Fide |
| **Anathema** | ❌ No (No direct anathema clause, but declared as a De Fide dogma) |
| **Year** | 1302 |

## Original Text (Summary)
> "Furthermore, we declare, we proclaim, we define that it is absolutely necessary for salvation (omnino esse de necessitate salutis) that every human creature be subject to the Roman Pontiff."

## Latin Original
> "Porro subesse Romano Pontifici omni humanae creaturae declaramus, dicimus, definimus et pronuntiamus omnino esse de necessitate salutis."

## Tags
`Papacy`, `Ecclesiology`, `Soteriology`, `Primacy`, `de_fide`, `absolute`, `necessary`

## Claims
1. Subjection (subesse) to the Roman Pontiff is absolutely necessary for salvation for every human creature.
2. Outside the one, holy, catholic, and apostolic Church there is neither salvation nor remission of sins (Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus).
3. In the Church there are two swords, the spiritual sword (gladius spiritualis) and the temporal sword (gladius temporalis), and both belong to the power of the Church.
4. The temporal power is subjected to the spiritual power.

## Negates
1. The proposition that one can be saved without subjection to the Pope.
2. The proposition that salvation is possible outside the Catholic Church.
3. The proposition that the temporal power is independent of the spiritual power.
4. The proposition that the primacy of the Pope is restricted only to the spiritual realm.
