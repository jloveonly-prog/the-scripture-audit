## Original Text (Summary)
> Opens the possibility of pastoral blessings for couples in irregular situations and same-sex couples. This blessing is not liturgical and must not be confused with the sacrament of matrimony. It is a descending blessing of grace (bendición descendente) bestowed "without gender, upon all", not an approval (validatio) of their moral state, but a plea for God's mercy.

## Tags
`Sacramental Theology`, `Matrimony`, `Ecclesiology`, `pastoral`, `allowance`

## Claims
1. Pastoral blessings can be given to couples in irregular situations (including same-sex couples).
2. This blessing is not a liturgical blessing (benedictio liturgica) but a non-liturgical, spontaneous (spontanea) blessing.
3. This blessing must not be confused with the sacrament of matrimony, nor is it an approval of irregular unions.
4. God's mercy and grace can be entreated without the prerequisite of moral perfection.
5. The doctrine that marriage is the indissoluble union between a man and a woman remaineth unchanged.

## Negates
1. The proposition that no form of blessing whatsoever can be given to those in irregular unions.
2. The proposition that all forms of blessings for same-sex couples are forbidden.
3. The proposition that a pastoral blessing signifieth the approval of a moral state.
