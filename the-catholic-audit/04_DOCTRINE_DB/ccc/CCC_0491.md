## Original Text (Summary)
> The most blessed Virgin Mary was, from the first moment of her conception, by a singular grace and privilege of almighty God and by virtue of the merits of Jesus Christ, Saviour of the human race, preserved immune from all stain of original sin. This "Immaculata Conceptio" was proclaimed as dogma by Pius IX in 1854.

## Tags
`Mariology`, `Original Sin`, `Soteriology`, `de_fide`, `Absolute`

## Claims
1. Mary was preserved from original sin from the first moment of her conception.
2. This preservation was by a singular grace and privilege of God.
3. This grace was the anticipated application of the redemptive merits of Christ.
4. The Immaculate Conception of Mary is a dogma (De Fide).
5. Mary is the only human being conceived without original sin.

## Negates
1. Mary was conceived with original sin like all other men.
2. There is no exception to the universal transmission of original sin.
3. Mary's sinlessness is natural.
4. Mary's salvation was accomplished in the same manner as that of other men.
