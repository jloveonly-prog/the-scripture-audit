# CCC-0491 — The Immaculate Conception of Mary

## Original Text (Summary)
> The Most Blessed Virgin Mary was, from the first moment of her conception, by a singular grace and privilege of almighty God and by virtue of the merits of Jesus Christ, Savior of the human race, preserved immune from all stain of original sin. This "Immaculate Conception" (Immaculata Conceptio) was proclaimed as dogma by Pius IX in 1854.

## Tags
`mariology`, `original_sin`, `soteriology`, `de_fide`, `absolute`

## Claims
1. Mary was preserved from original sin from the first moment of her conception
2. This preservation is by a special grace and privilege of God
3. This grace is a pre-application of the merits of Christ's redemption
4. Mary's Immaculate Conception is dogma (De Fide)
5. Mary is the only human being conceived without original sin

## Negates
1. Mary, like every other human being, was conceived with original sin
2. There is no exception to the universal transmission of original sin
3. Mary's sinlessness is natural (a matter of her nature)
4. Mary's salvation occurred in the same way as that of other human beings
