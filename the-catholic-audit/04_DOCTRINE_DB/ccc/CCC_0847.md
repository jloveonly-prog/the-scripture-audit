# CCC-0847 — Possibility of Salvation Outside the Church

| Item | Content |
|:---|:---|

| **Dogmatic Grade** | Sententia Certa |

## Original Text (Summary)
> Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart, and, moved by grace, try in their actions to do his will as they know it through the dictates of their conscience - those too may achieve eternal salvation (Second Vatican Council, Lumen Gentium §16).

## Tags
`Soteriology`, `Ecclesiology`, `sententia_certa`, `Conditional`, `Pastoral`

## Claims
1. He who knoweth not Christ and the Church through no fault of his own may also be saved.
2. The condition for salvation is seeking God with a sincere heart.
3. The endeavour to fulfil the will of God according to the dictates of conscience is required.
4. Such salvation is brought to pass under the influence of grace.
5. The possibility of salvation existeth even outside the visible bounds of the Church.

## Negates
1. Absolutely no salvation is possible outside the Church (absolute interpretation of Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus).
2. Without baptism, one cannot be saved under any circumstances.
3. All who have not heard the Gospel are damned.
4. Salvation is strictly confined to the visible members of the Catholic Church.
