# CCC-0847 — The Possibility of Salvation Outside the Church

## Original Text (Summary)
> Those who, through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart, and, moved by grace, try in their actions to do his will as they know it through the dictates of their conscience — those too may achieve eternal salvation (Second Vatican Council, Lumen Gentium §16).

## Tags
`soteriology`, `ecclesiology`, `sententia_certa`, `conditional`, `pastoral`

## Claims
1. Even one who, through no fault of their own, does not know Christ and the Church can be saved
2. The condition for salvation is seeking God with a sincere heart
3. An effort to carry out God's will according to the dictates of conscience is required
4. This salvation occurs under the influence of grace
5. The possibility of salvation exists even outside the Church's visible boundaries

## Negates
1. No salvation whatsoever is possible outside the Church (the absolute reading of Extra Ecclesiam nulla salus)
2. Without baptism, salvation is impossible under any circumstance
3. Everyone who has not heard the Gospel is condemned
4. Salvation is limited solely to the visible members of the Catholic Church
