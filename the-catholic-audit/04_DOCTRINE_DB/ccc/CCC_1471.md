## Original Text (Summary)
> An indulgence is a remission before God of the temporal punishment (poena temporalis) due to sins already forgiven. A member of the Christian faithful who is duly disposed obtains an indulgence through the action of the Church. The Church holds the authority to dispense the treasury (thesaurus) of the merits of Christ and the saints, and distributes indulgences from this treasury. Indulgences are distinguished into partial and plenary.

## Claims
1. An indulgence is a remission of the temporal punishment due to sins already forgiven
2. The Church holds the authority to grant indulgences
3. The basis for indulgences is the treasury (thesaurus) of the merits of Christ and the saints
4. Indulgences are distinguished into partial and plenary
5. Indulgences apply to the faithful who are duly disposed

## Negates
1. The Church has no authority to remit temporal punishment
2. No treasury of the saints' merits exists apart from Christ's merit
3. All punishment is completely removed together with the forgiveness of sin
4. The system of indulgences is a human invention with no scriptural basis
