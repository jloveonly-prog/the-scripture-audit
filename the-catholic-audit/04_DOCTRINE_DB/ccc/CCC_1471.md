## Original Text (Summary)
> An indulgence is a remission before God of the temporal punishment (poena temporalis) due to sins whose guilt has already been forgiven. A faithful Christian who is duly disposed gaineth indulgences under certain prescribed conditions through the action of the Church, which, as the minister of redemption, dispenseth and applieth with authority the treasury (thesaurus) of the satisfactions of Christ and the saints. Indulgences are divided into partial and plenary.

## Claims
1. An indulgence is the remission of the temporal punishment due to sins already forgiven.
2. The Church hath authority to grant indulgences.
3. The foundation of indulgences is the treasury of the merits of Christ and the saints.
4. Indulgences are divided into partial and plenary.
5. Indulgences are applied to a believer who hath the proper conditions.

## Negates
1. The Church hath no authority to remit temporal punishment.
2. Besides the merits of Christ, no treasury of the merits of the saints doth exist.
3. With the forgiveness of sin, all punishment is likewise entirely removed.
4. The institution of indulgences is a human invention without scriptural foundation.
