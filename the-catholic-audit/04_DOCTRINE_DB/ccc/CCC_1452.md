# CCC-1452 — Perfect Contrition (Contritio Perfecta)

## Original Text (Summary)
> Perfect contrition, motivated by a love of God, remits venial sins, and it also obtains forgiveness of mortal sins if it includes the firm resolution to have recourse to sacramental confession as soon as possible. That is, even prior to sacramental confession, mortal sin can be forgiven through perfect contrition.

## Tags
`sacramental_theology`, `confession`, `soteriology`, `grace`, `sententia_certa`, `conditional`

## Claims
1. Perfect contrition arises from love of God
2. Perfect contrition brings the forgiveness of venial sin
3. When accompanied by the resolve to go to confession, perfect contrition also forgives mortal sin
4. Forgiveness of mortal sin is possible even prior to sacramental confession
5. The resolve to go to confession (votum sacramenti) is a necessary condition

## Negates
1. Mortal sin can be forgiven only through sacramental confession (without exception)
2. No sin whatsoever can be forgiven by inner contrition alone
3. The forgiveness of mortal sin must always be preceded by a priest's absolution
