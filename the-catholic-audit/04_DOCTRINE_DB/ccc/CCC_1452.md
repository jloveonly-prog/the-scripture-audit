## Original Text (Summary)
> Perfect contrition ariseth from a love by which God is loved above all things. It remits venial sins; it also obtains forgiveness of mortal sins if it includes the firm resolution to have recourse to sacramental confession as soon as possible. That is to say, mortal sins may be forgiven by perfect contrition even before the sacrament of Penance.

## Tags
`Sacramentology`, `Penance`, `Soteriology`, `Grace`, `sententia_certa`, `Conditional`

## Claims
1. Perfect contrition ariseth from the love of God.
2. Perfect contrition bringeth the remission of venial sins.
3. With the resolution to receive sacramental confession, perfect contrition forgiveth mortal sins also.
4. The forgiveness of mortal sins is possible even before the sacrament of Penance.
5. The resolution to receive the sacrament (votum sacramenti) is an indispensable condition.

## Negates
1. Mortal sins can be forgiven solely through the sacrament of Penance (without exception).
2. Inward contrition alone can remit no sin.
3. The absolution of a priest must inevitably precede the forgiveness of mortal sins.
