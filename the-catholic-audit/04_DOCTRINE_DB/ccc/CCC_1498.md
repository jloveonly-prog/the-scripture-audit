# CCC-1498 — Applying Indulgences to the Dead

## Original Text (Summary)
> Indulgences can be applied to either the living or the dead. A living believer can obtain an indulgence for themselves, and can also apply an indulgence to the dead in purgatory by way of intercession (suffragium). This is grounded in the doctrine of the communion of saints (communio sanctorum).

## Claims
1. Indulgences can be applied to both the living and the dead
2. The living can apply an indulgence on behalf of the dead in purgatory
3. The application of indulgences to the dead is by way of intercession (suffragium)
4. This doctrine is grounded in the communion of saints (communio sanctorum)
5. Souls in purgatory can be helped by the prayers and indulgences of the living

## Negates
1. The state of the dead cannot be altered by the actions of the living
2. Indulgences apply only to the living
3. No spiritual help whatsoever is possible after death
4. There is no spiritual exchange between the souls in purgatory and the earthly Church
