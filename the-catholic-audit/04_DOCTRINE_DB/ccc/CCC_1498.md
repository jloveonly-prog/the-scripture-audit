## Original Text (Summary)
> Indulgences may be applied to the living or to the dead. The faithful living may obtain indulgences for themselves, and they may also apply them to the dead in Purgatory by way of suffrage (suffragium). This is founded upon the doctrine of the communion of saints (communio sanctorum).

## Claims
1. Indulgences may be applied both to the living and to the dead.
2. The living may apply indulgences for the dead in Purgatory.
3. The application of indulgences to the dead is by way of suffrage (suffragium).
4. This doctrine is founded upon the communion of saints (communio sanctorum).
5. The souls in Purgatory may receive aid from the prayers and indulgences of the living.

## Negates
1. The state of the dead cannot be altered by the acts of the living.
2. Indulgences are applied solely to the living.
3. After death, no spiritual aid is possible.
4. There is no spiritual intercourse betwixt the souls in Purgatory and the Church on earth.
