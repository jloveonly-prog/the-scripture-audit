# CCC-2181 — The Sunday Mass Obligation

## Original Text (Summary)
> The Sunday celebration of the Lord's Day and his Eucharist is at the heart of the Church's life. The faithful are obliged to participate in the Eucharist on days of obligation, unless excused for a serious reason (e.g. illness, the care of infants) or dispensed by their own pastor. Those who deliberately fail in this obligation commit a grave sin (peccatum grave).

## Claims
1. Participation in Mass on Sundays and holy days of obligation is a duty
2. Deliberately failing to fulfill the Mass obligation is a mortal sin
3. One may be excused from this obligation for a serious reason
4. The Mass is the foundation of the whole life of the Church
5. Canon law establishes Mass attendance as an obligation (Canon 1247)

## Negates
1. Attending Mass is a merely optional matter
2. Missing Mass is not a sin
3. Private prayer can substitute for Sunday Mass
4. The Church's liturgical regulations carry no binding force
