## Original Text (Summary)
> The Sunday Eucharist (Mass) is the foundation and confirmation of all Christian practice. The faithful are obliged to participate in the Eucharist on days of obligation, unless excused for a serious reason (for example, illness, the care of infants). Those who deliberately fail in this obligation commit a grave sin (peccatum grave).

## Tags

## Claims
1. Participation in the Mass on Sundays and holy days of obligation is a duty.
2. Deliberately to fail in fulfilling the obligation of the Mass is a mortal sin.
3. One may be excused from this obligation if there be a serious reason.
4. The Mass is the foundation of the life of the whole Church.
5. Canon law prescribeth participation in the Mass as an obligation (Canon 1247).

## Negates
1. Participation in the Mass is merely an optional matter.
2. Absence from the Mass constitutes no sin.
3. Personal prayer may be substituted for the Sunday Mass.
4. The liturgical precepts of the Church hold no binding force.
