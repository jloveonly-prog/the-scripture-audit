## Original Text (Summary)
> Our justification cometh from the grace of God. Grace is a favour, the free and undeserved help that God giveth us to respond to his call to partake of his life. Grace is supernatural and gratuitous; it cannot be merited by the natural abilities or works of man. Grace precedeth all preparative acts or achievements of man.

## Tags
`Soteriology`, `Grace`, `Justification`, `de_fide`, `Absolute`

## Claims
1. Grace is a free and undeserved gift of God.
2. The fount of justification is the grace of God.
3. Grace is supernatural and cannot be merited by the natural abilities of man.
4. Grace precedeth the merits of man.
5. Grace is a call to partake of the life of God.

## Negates
1. Man can attain grace solely by his own effort and merit (Pelagianism).
2. Justification springeth from the works of man.
3. Grace is a reward which man doth rightly deserve.
4. The preparative acts of man precede grace.
