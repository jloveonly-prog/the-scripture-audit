## Original Text (Summary)
> Our justification comes from the grace of God. Grace is favor, the free and undeserved help God gives us to respond to his call to become his children, and a free gift by which he calls and helps us to share in his own life. Grace is supernatural and gratuitous, something that cannot be obtained by human natural power or merit. Grace precedes any act of preparation or work on the part of the human being.

## Tags
`soteriology`, `grace`, `justification`, `de_fide`, `absolute`

## Claims
1. Grace is a free, undeserved gift God gives us
2. The source of justification is God's grace
3. Grace is supernatural and cannot be obtained by natural human power
4. Grace precedes human merit
5. Grace is the call to share in the life of God

## Negates
1. Human beings can obtain grace by their own effort and merit alone (Pelagianism)
2. Justification originates in human works
3. Grace is a reward that human beings rightly deserve
4. An act of human preparation precedes grace
