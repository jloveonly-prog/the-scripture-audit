## Original Text (Summary)
> Since the initiative belongs to God in the order of grace, no one can merit the initial grace of forgiveness and justification at the beginning of conversion. Moved by the Holy Spirit and charity, however, good deeds performed in the state of sanctifying grace are meritorious in strict justice (meritum de condigno), meriting the growth of grace, an increase in holiness, and the attainment of eternal life.

## Claims
1. No merit whatsoever can be attached to initial grace (the beginning of conversion and justification)
2. Good works performed in the state of sanctifying grace become merit (meritum de condigno)
3. Through merit, growth in grace and increase in holiness are possible
4. Through merit, eternal life can be obtained
5. The source of all merit is the prompting of the Holy Spirit and God's grace
6. Merit is possible only in the state of grace

## Negates
1. A human being can build up no merit whatsoever before God (the absolute reading of Sola Gratia)
2. Eternal life is given by faith alone (Sola Fide)
3. Good works contribute nothing at all to salvation
4. Human acts have no value whatsoever before God
