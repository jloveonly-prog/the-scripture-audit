## Original Text (Summary)
> The Lord himself affirms that Baptism is necessary for salvation (cf. John 3:5). Wherefore Baptism is necessary for salvation for those to whom the Gospel has been proclaimed and who have had the possibility of asking for this sacrament. The Church does not know of any means other than Baptism that assures entry into eternal beatitude. God has bound salvation to the sacrament of Baptism, but he himself is not bound by his sacraments.

## Tags

## Claims
1. Baptism is necessary for salvation.
2. Baptism is an obligation for all to whom the Gospel has been proclaimed.
3. The Church knoweth of no other means of salvation save Baptism.
4. Notwithstanding, God is not bound by his sacraments (acknowledging the possibility of exceptions).
5. The necessity of Baptism resteth upon the Lord's own words (John 3:5).

## Negates
1. One may ordinarily be saved without Baptism.
2. Baptism is but an optional matter for salvation.
3. Inward faith alone without Baptism is sufficient (in ordinary cases).
4. The sacraments are merely outward ordinances unnecessary for salvation.
