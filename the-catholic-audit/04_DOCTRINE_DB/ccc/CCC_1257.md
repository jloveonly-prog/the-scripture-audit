## Original Text (Summary)
> The Lord himself affirms that baptism is necessary for salvation (cf. John 3:5). He also commands his disciples to proclaim the Gospel to all nations and to baptize them. Baptism is necessary for salvation for those to whom the Gospel has been proclaimed and who have had the possibility of asking for this sacrament. The Church does not know of any means other than Baptism that assures entry into eternal beatitude. God himself is not bound by his sacraments.

## Tags

## Claims
1. Baptism is necessary for salvation
2. Baptism is obligatory for all who have had the possibility of hearing the Gospel
3. The Church does not know of any means to salvation other than Baptism
4. God himself, however, is not bound by his sacraments (an exception is acknowledged)
5. The necessity of baptism is grounded in the Lord's own words (John 3:5)

## Negates
1. One can ordinarily be saved without baptism
2. Baptism is merely optional for salvation
3. Inner faith alone suffices without baptism (in the ordinary case)
4. The sacraments are merely external rites unnecessary for salvation
