## Original Text (Summary)
> All men are implicated in Adam's sin. St. Paul affirms that "by one man's disobedience many (that is, all men) were made sinners" (Romans 5:19). Original sin is transmitted to all of Adam's descendants, placing the whole of humanity in a state of sin. This is by propagation, not by imitation.

## Tags
`soteriology`, `original_sin`, `de_fide`, `absolute`, `necessary`

## Claims
1. Original sin is transmitted from Adam to all humanity
2. The transmission of original sin is by propagation (propagatio), not imitation
3. All humans are in the state of original sin from birth
4. Adam's sin altered not merely a personal sin but the condition of all humanity
5. Because of original sin, human nature is in a fallen state

## Negates
1. Human beings are born originally without sin
2. Original sin is limited to Adam alone
3. Original sin is transmitted by mere imitation (imitatio)
4. A human being can exist without original sin on their own
