## Original Text (Summary)
> All men are implicated in Adam's sin. St. Paul affirmeth, "By one man's disobedience many were made sinners" (Romans 5:19). Original sin is transmitted to all descendants of Adam, and thereby the whole human race is placed in a state of sin. This is by propagation, not by imitation.

## Tags
`Soteriology`, `Original Sin`, `de_fide`, `Absolute`, `Mandatory`

## Claims
1. Original sin is transmitted from Adam unto all mankind.
2. The transmission of original sin is by propagation, not by imitation.
3. Every man is in a state of original sin from the moment of his birth.
4. Adam's sin changed the state of all mankind, not being merely a personal sin.
5. By original sin, human nature is placed in a fallen state.

## Negates
1. Man is by nature born in a sinless state.
2. Original sin is confined solely to Adam's person.
3. Original sin is transmitted merely by imitation.
4. Man may exist of himself without original sin.
