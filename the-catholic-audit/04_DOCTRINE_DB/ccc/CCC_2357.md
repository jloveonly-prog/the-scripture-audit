## Original Text (Summary)
> Homosexuality refers to relations between men or between women who experience an exclusive or predominant sexual attraction toward persons of the same sex. Holy Scripture presents homosexual acts as acts of grave depravity. Tradition has always declared that homosexual acts are intrinsically disordered (intrinsece inordinati). They are contrary to the natural law. They close the sexual act to the gift of life. They do not proceed from a genuine affective and sexual complementarity. Under no circumstances can they be approved.

## Claims
1. Homosexual acts are intrinsically disordered (intrinsece inordinati).
2. Homosexual acts are contrary to the natural law.
3. Homosexual acts can under no circumstances be approved.
4. Homosexual acts close the sexual act to the gift of life.
5. Holy Scripture presents homosexual acts as acts of grave depravity.

## Negates
1. Homosexual acts are morally neutral or permissible.
2. Homosexual acts may be approved in certain circumstances.
3. The natural law renders no judgment concerning homosexual acts.
4. The sexual union of the same sex holds a value equal to that of matrimony.
5. Holy Scripture is silent concerning homosexuality.
