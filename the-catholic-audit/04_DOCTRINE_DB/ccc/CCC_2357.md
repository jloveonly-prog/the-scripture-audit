## Original Text (Summary)
> Homosexuality refers to relations between men or between women who experience an exclusive or predominant sexual attraction toward persons of the same sex. Homosexual acts are presented in Scripture as acts of grave depravity. Tradition has always declared that homosexual acts are intrinsically disordered (intrinsece inordinati). They are contrary to the natural law, close the sexual act to the gift of life, and do not proceed from a genuine affective and sexual complementarity. Under no circumstances can they be approved.

## Negates

2. Homosexual acts can be approved under certain circumstances
3. Natural law renders no judgment whatsoever on homosexual acts
4. A same-sex sexual union holds a value equal to marriage
5. Scripture is silent about homosexuality
