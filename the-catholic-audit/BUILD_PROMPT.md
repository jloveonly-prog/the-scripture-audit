### 🎯 The Chilling Truth: "What is the 100% infallible document?" → "No such book exists"

If you ask a Catholic, **"Then what is the single book containing documents that are 100% without error?"**, they must answer **"No such book exists."** The CCC is not infallible.

What Catholicism acknowledges as "infallible" is **not an entire 'book'**, but **"specific sentences or declarations"** scattered throughout 2,000 years of history:

#### ☢️ Infallible Declaration Type 1: Council "Anathema" Statements
- Not **every word** spoken at councils is infallible
- Only the sentences determining doctrine and stating **"If any man say..., let him be anathema"** are infallible
- Example: Council of Trent Session 6, 33 Canons — "If any man say that one is justified by faith alone, let him be anathema"

#### ☢️ Infallible Declaration Type 2: Papal Ex Cathedra Declarations
- Vatican I (1870) declared "when the Roman Pontiff speaks ex cathedra... he possesses infallibility"
- Out of tens of thousands of documents poured out by hundreds of popes over **2,000 years**, the ones theologians **unanimously** accept as "100% infallible Ex Cathedra" are:

> **Shockingly, exactly two. And both are not in the scripture.**

# Mission: Transform Catholic Doctrinal Documents into Structured "Doctrine Cards"

## Project Background
I am building an engine to automatically detect **internal logical conflicts (A → Not A)** within the Catholic Magisterium's documents. I need to convert core documents (CCC, Trent, etc.) into a **structured card** format.

## Your Role
Convert the original texts of the Catechism (CCC), Council of Trent, Ecumenical Councils, Papal Bulls, Canon Law, etc., into "doctrine cards" fitting the schema below.

## ⚠️ Infallibility Tier — Must be indicated

Indicate Catholicism's own claim of infallibility on each card. This determines the destructive power of a conflict:

| Tier | Catholic Claim | Can error be admitted? |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| **De Fide (Absolute Dogma)** | "Absolutely infallible — denial is heresy" | ❌ Impossible |
| **De Fide Definita** | "Defined ex cathedra or in council" | ❌ Impossible |
| **Sententia Certa** | "Certain opinion — theologically certain" | ❌ Practically impossible |
| **Sententia Communis** | "Common teaching — most theologians agree" | △ Theoretically possible |
| **Pastoral** | "Pastoral doc — dogmatic binding debated" | ✅ Possible |

**Key**: If De Fide conflicts with De Fide = Catholicism cannot say "it was a mistake" (since they declared it "infallible").

## Doctrine Card Schema

Format each article into the markdown below:

```markdown
# [ID] — [Title]

| Field | Content |
|:---|:---|
| **ID** | (e.g., CCC-0402, TRENT-S06-C09) |
| **Source** | (e.g., Catechism of the Catholic Church, Council of Trent Sess 6) |
| **Article** | (e.g., Para 402, Canon 9) |
| **Dogmatic Tier** | De Fide / De Fide Definita / Sententia Certa / Sententia Communis / Pastoral |
| **Infallibility** | ☢️ Infallible / 🔶 Authoritative / 🟢 Pastoral |
| **Anathema** | Yes / No |
| **Year** | (e.g., 1992, 1563) |

## Original Text (Summary)
> (Summarize the core content of the article in 2-3 sentences)

## Tags
`Soteriology`, `Baptism`, `Sacraments` (Related tags)

## Claims — ⚠️ Most Important
(List the propositions this doctrine **claims to be true**)
1. 
2. 

## Negates — ⚠️ Most Important
(List the propositions this doctrine **denies as false**)
1. 
2. 
```

## ⚠️ Core Rules

1. **Claims and Negates are most important.** This is the core data for automatic collision detection.
   - If Card A's Claims match Card B's Negates → Conflict found
   - So make the **propositions clear and specific**.
   
2. **Must indicate Infallibility.** 
   - Ecumenical Council dogma = ☢️ Infallible
   - Trent Canon + Anathema = ☢️ Infallible
   - Ex Cathedra Papal = ☢️ Infallible
   - CCC = 🔶 Authoritative (Not infallible)
   - Pastoral docs = 🟢 Pastoral

3. **Must indicate Anathema.** Anathema clauses are the highest tier dogma.

4. **Be faithful to the original text.** Do not insert your own interpretation, only write what the doctrine actually claims.
