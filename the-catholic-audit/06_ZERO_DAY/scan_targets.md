# List of Priority Scan Targets for Zero-Day Excavation

These are the priority search targets for the CVCAP 2.0 Zero-Day Engine. The list below contains collision candidates highly likely to be found between recent papal documents and existing dogmas.

---

## 1. Fiducia Supplicans (2023) vs. Trent's Doctrine of Marriage + CCC 2357

- **Fiducia Supplicans** (2023, DDF): Permits pastoral blessings for irregular unions (including same-sex couples)
- **Trent's Doctrine of Marriage**: Marriage is a sacrament between one man and one woman; let him who denies this be anathema.
- **CCC 2357**: Homosexual acts are "intrinsically disordered" and "under no circumstances can they be approved."
- **Potential Collision**: A blessing is a form of approval. "Under no circumstances can they be approved" and "allowance of blessing" are potentially contradictory.

## 2. Amoris Laetitia Chapter 8 (2016) vs. Canon 915

- **Amoris Laetitia** Chapter 8, footnote 351 (2016, Francis): Suggests the possibility that believers in irregular situations might receive the sacraments (the Eucharist).
- **Canon 915**: Those who obstinately persist in manifest grave sin are not to be admitted to holy communion.
- **Potential Collision**: Allowing the Eucharist to the divorced and civilly remarried (a state of adultery according to Canon Law) is a potential violation of Canon 915.

## 3. The Abolition of Limbo (2007) vs. Trent's Anathema on Baptism + CCC 1261

- **International Theological Commission Document** (2007): There are "serious theological and liturgical grounds for hope that unbaptized infants who die will be saved," and Limbo is an "unnecessary hypothesis."
- **Trent's Decree on Baptism**: Baptism is necessary unto salvation; let him who denies this be anathema.
- **CCC 1261**: "As regards children who have died without Baptism, the Church can only entrust them to the mercy of God."
- **Potential Collision**: The necessity of baptism (Trent's anathema) vs. Salvation is possible without baptism (Abolition of Limbo).

## 4. Fratelli Tutti (2020) on the Death Penalty vs. the Former CCC 2267

- **Fratelli Tutti** (2020, Francis): The death penalty is "inadmissible."
- **CCC 2267 (1997 Edition)**: The death penalty is permissible if it is the only practicable way to defend the lives of human beings effectively against the aggressor.
- **CCC 2267 (2018 Revised Edition)**: The death penalty is "inadmissible because it is an attack on the inviolability and dignity of the person."
- **Potential Collision**: An absolute alteration of doctrine — From "permissible" to "inadmissible" is not dogmatic development but dogmatic negation.

## 5. Candidates for 3-Stage Combos

### 5-1. Infant Baptism + Necessity of Baptism + Abolition of Limbo
- CCC 1250 (Encouragement of Infant Baptism) + CCC 1257 (Necessity of Baptism) + Abolition of Limbo (2007)
- Structure: If baptism is necessary, but an unbaptized infant can be saved when he dies, the urgency of infant baptism vanishes. Yet the Church still urges infant baptism as a necessity.

### 5-2. Same-Sex Blessing + CCC 2357 + The Meaning of a Blessing
- Fiducia Supplicans (2023) + CCC 2357 + CCC 1671 (Definition of a Sacramental Blessing)
- Structure: A blessing is a prayer asking for "God's grace" (CCC 1671). To bless a same-sex union = To ask for God's grace upon a same-sex union. However, CCC 2357 defines homosexual acts as "intrinsically disordered." To ask for God's grace upon a disordered act is a contradiction.

### 5-3. Infallibility + Honorius + Circular Logic
- Combination of COL-003 + COL-008
- Structure: The Pope is infallible (VAT1) → Honorius was a heretic (CONST3) → "It was not ex cathedra" (Evasion) → Who determines if it was ex cathedra? → The infallible Pope/Magisterium (Loop) → The very infallibility that is the basis of the loop is threatened by Honorius → ∞ Self-destructing loop.

### 5-4. Indulgences + Purgatory + Application to the Dead
- CCC 1471 (Indulgences) + CCC 1030 (Purgatory) + CCC 1471 Latter half (Indulgences for the dead)
- Structure: Purgatory is a "necessary purification," yet it is mitigable by indulgences, and the living can obtain indulgences for the dead. Substituting the "necessary purification" of another with the actions of a different person nullifies the personal nature of the purification.
