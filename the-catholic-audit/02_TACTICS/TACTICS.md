## 1. Core Tactical Philosophy: "Strike with the enemy's sword (Implosion)"
The core tactic of CVCAP is not to push Protestant logic. It is to **juxtapose the actual texts of the 'Magisterial documents' (CCC, Councils) that Catholicism claims are absolutely infallible, inducing an internal contradiction and implosion.**

## 2. Jurisdiction Split Tactic
* **💣 Document Court (CVCAP)**: Exposes fatal collisions between Catholic internal documents through OODA 10-Rounds.
* **⚔️ Scriptural Court (BVCAP)**: Collisions between KJV original text and Catholic doctrine.

## 3. Direct Document Strikes (Anchors)

### A. Justification and Salvation: Trent vs Vatican II
* **Anchor 1: Council of Trent**: Canon 9 on Justification: "If any one saith, that by faith alone the impious is justified... let him be anathema."
* **Anchor 2: Vatican II**: Lumen Gentium 16: "But the plan of salvation also includes those who acknowledge the Creator. In the first place amongst these there are the Muslims... can attain to everlasting salvation."
* **Tactic**: "Which council is infallible?" (L-04 Dilemma).

### B. Dilemma of Grace and Merit: CCC
* **Anchor 3: Free Grace**: CCC 1996: "Grace is a free gift."
* **Anchor 4: Necessity of Merit**: CCC 2010: "We can then merit for ourselves... eternal life."
* **Tactic**: Socratic questioning (L-06). Expose the contradiction of a "free gift" requiring "merit".

### C. Marian Dogma and Historical Rupture
* **Anchor 5: Papal Infallibility Declaration**: Ineffabilis Deus (1854) declaring Immaculate Conception.
* **Tactic**: Block CE-03 (Retroactive tradition) by showing the complete absence of this in 1st-3rd century Fathers.
