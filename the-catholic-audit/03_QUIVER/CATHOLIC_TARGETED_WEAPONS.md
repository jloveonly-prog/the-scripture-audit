## ⚔️ Weapon Card A: John 6:63 — Striking the Heart of Transubstantiation
### Original Text (KJV)
> *"It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life."*

### Strike Logic
Transubstantiation premise: The flesh (sarx) in John 6:53-55 is physical flesh and blood, necessary for salvation.
John 6:63: Jesus says the flesh (sarx) profiteth nothing.
Conclusion: The same word cannot be both profitable for eternal life and profiteth nothing.

## ⚔️ Weapon Card C: 1 Tim 2:5 — Blocking Intercession of Saints
### Original Text (KJV)
> *"For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus"*

### Strike Logic
1 Tim 2:5 says there is only ONE mediator. Mary/Saints intercession violates this "one mediator". The Greek word μεσίτης (mesites) means one who connects in the middle. Functionally identical.

## ⚔️ Weapon Card D: Matt 16:18 Rock — Original Language Strike on Petrine Primacy
### Original Text (KJV)
> *"thou art Peter (Πέτρος), and upon this rock (πέτρα) I will build my church"*

### Strike Logic
Petros = a piece of rock, a stone (masculine).
Petra = a mass of rock, bedrock (feminine).
If Jesus meant Peter was the rock, He would have used the same word. The rock is Peter's confession in v16.
Confirmation: In v23, Jesus calls Peter "Satan". The foundation of the church cannot be called Satan verses later.
